# Dining area: execution plan

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31 (D-069). The wall and the arches stand and the mesh is being tied on, in the founder's photos of 2026-10-31; every size is read off those photos against the people in them, not measured [A] (AS-31).

Every step from the wall that stands today to a dinner for 24 under the thatch. Breakfast and dinner come from the [galley](../galley/plan.md); the dining area is where the whole house eats them at once.

## Day 1 measuring sheet

Measure before buying the thatch and the furniture; send the numbers and they replace the photo readings.

1. The outline's outside length and width, and the wall's thickness and height.
2. The door gap: where it is and how wide.
3. How many arches, how far apart, and the height of the crown above the inside ground.
4. The mesh's square size and wire gauge.
5. The inside ground's level: does rain run out through the door?

## Steps

1. **Wall (done):** mud brick in earth mortar, the door gap in the east end.
2. **Frame (done):** the arches set into the top of the long walls, the ridge pole along the crown, a purlin each side.
3. **Mesh (going on):** welded mesh tied to every arch and pole with wire, all over the vault.
4. **Thatch:** grass tied to the mesh in courses from the eaves up to the ridge, 250 mm thick, its eave 300 mm past the long walls so the rain drips clear of the earth wall.
5. **Floor:** 100 mm of compacted laterite inside the walls, falling a little to the door.
6. **Furnished:** the two tables and four benches, the serving counter at the west end, the lamps along the ridge.

## Hold points

1. **Keep the wall dry.** Mud brick softens in rain: the thatch must overhang the walls before the long rains, and the wall's foot needs a dry, raised base or a splash course.
2. **No flame under the thatch.** No charcoal stove, brazier or open lamp inside; food arrives cooked from the galley.
3. **The frame carries the thatch.** Wet thatch is heavy: check every arch's foot is firm in the wall and every tie is tight before thatching.

## Done when

1. The thatch covers the vault, sheds a heavy rain without a drip inside, and the wall stays dry.
2. 24 people sit at the tables at once, with the dishes set out on the serving counter.
