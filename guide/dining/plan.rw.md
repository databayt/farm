## Uyu munsi (2026-10-31)

Ibyo amafoto (media dn-3279 kugeza dn-3282) yerekana:

1. **Urukuta ruhagaze** impande zose, ku burebure bw'idirishya.
2. **Imiheto ihagaze**, hafi itandatu, n'igiti kirekire hejuru.
3. **Akayunguruzo kari kuziritswa** hejuru y'imiheto, ntikarageza ku gisenge cyose.

## Urupapuro rwo gupima ku munsi wa 1

Pima mbere yo kugura ibyatsi n'ibikoresho byo mu nzu; ohereza imibare isimbure iyasomwe ku mafoto.

1. Uburebure n'ubugari by'inyuma, n'ubunini n'uburebure bw'urukuta.
2. Umwanya w'urugi: aho uri n'ubugari bwawo.
3. Imiheto ingahe, intera hagati yayo, n'uburebure bwo hejuru uvuye ku butaka bw'imbere.
4. Ingano y'utuzu tw'akayunguruzo n'ubunini bw'urutsinga.
5. Uko ubutaka bw'imbere buringaniye: imvura isohoka inyuze ku rugi?

## Intambwe

1. **Urukuta (rwarangiye):** amatafari ya rukarakara mu byondo, umwanya w'urugi ku ruhande rw'iburasirazuba.
2. **Imiheto (yarangiye):** imiheto ishinzwe hejuru y'inkuta ndende, igiti cyo hagati hejuru, igiti gifatanya kuri buri ruhande.
3. **Akayunguruzo (kari gukorwa):** akayunguruzo gasudiwe kaziritswe kuri buri muheto n'igiti n'urutsinga, ku gisenge cyose.
4. **Ibyatsi:** ibyatsi biziritswe ku kayunguruzo umurongo ku wundi uhereye hasi ugana hejuru, mm 250, birenga inkuta ndende mm 300 kugira ngo imvura igwe kure y'urukuta rw'ibyondo.
5. **Hasi:** mm 100 za laterite isindagiye imbere y'inkuta, imanuka gato igana ku rugi.
6. **Hatunganyijwe:** ameza abiri n'intebe ndende enye, aho gutegurira ku mpera y'iburengerazuba, amatara munsi y'igiti cyo hagati.

## Aho guhagarara ugenzura

1. **Urukuta rugume rwumutse.** Amatafari ya rukarakara yoroha mu mvura: ibyatsi bigomba kurenga inkuta mbere y'imvura ndende, kandi hasi h'urukuta hakeneye urufatiro rwumutse rwazamuwe.
2. **Nta muriro munsi y'ibyatsi.** Nta mbabura, nta muriro cyangwa itara rifunguye imbere; ibiryo bihagera bitetse bivuye mu gikoni.
3. **Imiheto ifata ibyatsi.** Ibyatsi bitose biraremera: genzura ko buri muheto ushinze neza mu rukuta kandi buri kiziriko gikomeye mbere yo gusakara.

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Ibyatsi bitwikiriye igisenge cyose, imvura nyinshi ntiyinjira imbere, kandi urukuta rukaguma rwumutse.
2. Abantu 24 bicara ku meza icyarimwe, amafunguro ashyizwe aho gutegurira.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Aho hari ku ifamu, ugereranyije n'igishushanyo cy'inzu: ntiharashyirwa ku gishushanyo cy'urupapuro rw'itangiriro.
2. Amashanyarazi y'amatara: REG ivuye ku kibaho cya hafi, cyangwa agace k'imirasire y'izuba?
3. Ubwoko bw'ibyatsi byo gusakara, n'uzasakara.
4. Ingano zasomwe ku mafoto: urupapuro rwo ku munsi wa 1 ruzazipima.
5. Gusiga urukuta (ibyondo cyangwa ishwagara), cyangwa amatafari agasigara uko ari?
