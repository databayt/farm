Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31 (D-069). Ingano ziva muri dining.json, zasomwe ku mafoto ya nyir'ifamu (AS-31); nta kintu kirashakwa cyangwa ngo gihabwe igiciro [A].

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Tangirira ku bihindura igiciro cyose: ibyatsi n'abakozi babisakara, ameza n'intebe ndende.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe n'umubare.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'umucuruzi, ushyireho ko umurongo wemejwe.
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/dining/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bibigaragaze.
