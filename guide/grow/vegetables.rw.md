# Umurima w'imboga: kuva ku butaka bwambaye ubusa kugeza mu gikoni

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: amabwiriza yo ku wa 2026-10-28. Umurima, ubutaka bwawo n'amazi yawo ntibirapimwa; ingano zikurikiza igishushanyo mbonera cy'agateganyo (provisional master plan).

Uko watangiza umurima w'imboga ugaburira igikoni gikuru (galley) kandi ukagurisha ibisagutse byawo: ibihembwe, uturima, amazi, ibihingwa n'ukwezi kwa mbere, intambwe ku yindi.

## Ukwezi kwa mbere, intambwe ku yindi (Ugushyingo)

1. **Pima ubutaka.** Fata ingero z'ubutaka (samples) ahantu 10 hatandukanye mu gice cyose, uzivange, maze uzohereze muri laboratwari y'ubutaka ya RAB: pH (ubusharire), organic carbon, azote (nitrogen), fosifore (phosphorus), imiterere y'ubutaka (texture) [V CRP-72].
2. **Pima amazi.** Pima amazi borehole (iriba ricukuwe) itanga mu gihe cy'amasaha 6, kandi usuzumishe urugero rw'amazi; umurima ukenera hafi 2 m³ ku isaha mu gihe cy'amasaha 6 mu mezi y'izuba [E].
3. **Zitira igice cy'imboga** kugira ngo inyamaswa zitinjiramo, kandi usige irembo rimwe ku nzira y'ubusitani (garden walk) ijya ku gikoni gikuru.
4. **Shyira ibimenyetso aho uturima tuzaba:** uturima twa m 1.2, inzira za m 0.5, inzira za m 1 hagati y'amatsinda y'uturima 10; uturima dutambikwa ku buhaname bw'ubutaka (across the slope).
5. **Kora uturima 40 twa mbere:** cukura, uvangemo ifumbire y'imborera (compost) cyangwa iy'amatungo (manure), hafi kg 250 kuri 100 m² ku nyanya (tomatoes) [V CRP-68], hanyuma utuzamure cm 20.
6. **Shyiraho amatiyo y'ibitonyanga:** itiyo nkuru (header pipe) iva ku kigega cy'amazi, akayunguruzo (filter), n'amatiyo abiri y'ibitonyanga kuri buri karima; fungura amazi mu gihe cy'isaha imwe, ugenzure buri kenge gatonyanga (emitter).
7. **Tangiza ubuhumbikiro (nursery):** udusanduku tw'ingemwe (trays) cyangwa akarima k'ingemwe (seedbed) gatwikiriwe n'urushundura rw'igicucu (shade net), ku ngemwe za sukuma (kale), ishu (cabbage), igitunguru (onion), inyanya na puwavuro (sweet pepper).
8. **Biba imbuto mu turima, nta guhumbika:** dodo (amaranth) n'imiteja (French beans), bitanga umusaruro mu byumweru 4 kugeza 8.
9. **Shyiramo ishwagara (lime) gusa iyo ibisubizo by'igerageza bibisabye:** imboga zikenera pH 6.0 kugeza 7.0; birashoboka ko ubutaka bwo hano buri hafi ya 5.5 kugeza 6.1 [V CRP-68, E LOC-37].

## Amazi

1. Mu mezi y'izuba ryinshi, buri m² y'akarima ikenera litiro 4.1 kugeza 5.3 ku munsi: 10 kugeza 13 m³ ku munsi ku murima wose [E CRP-75, LOC-54].
2. Ayo mazi akubye hafi inshuro icumi ayo ibisenge bishobora gufata, bityo borehole cyangwa umuyoboro rusange (mains) ni byo bitunga umurima; mu gihembwe C, tera gusa ibyo amazi yemejwe n'igerageza ashobora kuhira.
3. Uhire mu museke cyangwa nimugoroba, ntuzigere uhira saa sita z'amanywa; sasira uturima ibyatsi byumye (mulch) kugira ngo amazi agume mu butaka.
4. Kuhira ubuso buri munsi ya ha 1 ntibisaba uruhushya rw'Ikigo gishinzwe umutungo kamere w'amazi (Water Resources Board) [V LOC-08].

## Kurwanya ibyonnyi

1. Simburanya ibihingwa (rotation): ntuzigere utera umuryango umwe w'ibihingwa (inyanya, puwavuro n'intoryi; ishu na sukuma; imiteja n'ibindi bishyimbo) mu karima kamwe ibihembwe bibiri bikurikiranye.
2. Zenguruka uturima kabiri mu cyumweru; randura kandi utwike igihingwa cyose kiraba mu buryo butunguranye (bacterial wilt).
3. Manika imitego ikoresha impumuro (pheromone traps) ya Tuta absoluta mu turima tw'inyanya.
4. Tera umuti gusa uwo umujyanama w'ucuruza inyongeramusaruro (agro-dealer) akubwiye ku cyonnyi wabonye, kandi wandike buri muti uteye.

## Ibigaragaza ko byarangiye

1. Ibisubizo by'igerageza ry'ubutaka n'iry'amazi byagarutse, kandi uturima twashyizwemo ishwagara n'ifumbire nk'uko ibyo bisubizo bibisaba.
2. Uturima mirongo ine twatewe, amatiyo y'ibitonyanga arakora, kandi buri kenge gatonyanga gatanga amazi.
3. Igikoni gikuru gihabwa urutonde rw'ibyasaruwe buri gitondo, kandi buri kilo gisaruwe mu murima kirandikwa.
