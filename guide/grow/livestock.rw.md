# Amatungo: amagi mbere, hanyuma ihene

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: amabwiriza yo ku wa 2026-10-28. Nta tungo rizazanwa mbere y'uko ibigenzurwa biri hepfo birangira; aho inzu z'amatungo zizubakwa hazemezwa nyuma yo gupima intera ya m 200 uvuye ku kiyaga.

Uko watangira korora amatungo aha igikoni gikuru (galley) amagi, nyuma akazatanga n'ihene zo kugurisha: ayo korora n'impamvu, inzu zayo n'ingano zazo, uko yitabwaho buri munsi n'amategeko.

## Amatungo yo korora, n'igihe

| Itungo | Icyemezo | Impamvu |
|---|---|---|
| Inkoko zitera amagi | Ni zo zibanza: inkoko hafi 100 | Amagi y'igikoni gikuru buri munsi; ntizirebwa n'akato kashyizweho kubera Umuriro wa Rift Valley (Rift Valley fever, RVF); zishobora kwishingirwa; zitangira gutera amagi zifite hafi ibyumweru 20 z'amavuko [V LVS-67, MKT-21] |
| Ihene | Nyuma: ihene z'ingore za gakondo 2 kugeza kuri 4 | Urwuri (paddock) rwa hegitari 0.32 rutunga ihene z'ingore 2 kugeza kuri 4 [E]; tegereza kugeza umuganga w'amatungo w'akarere yemeje mu nyandiko uko Umuriro wa Rift Valley uhagaze n'uko ingendo z'amatungo zihagaze [V LVS-03, LVS-29] |
| Inkoko z'inyama (broilers) | Ku gikoni gusa, niba zinakenewe | Mu 2025, igiciro cyo ku ifamu (farm-gate) cyari munsi y'ikiguzi cyo kuzorora [V LVS-72] |

## Mbere y'uko hagira itungo rizanwa

1. **Iyandikishe nk'umworozi** ukurikije itegeko rishya rigenga amatungo, rikurikizwa kuva ryatangazwa ku wa 2026-09-11: aborozi bariyandikisha, umuganga w'amatungo wenyine ni we uvura amatungo, kandi buri rugendo rw'itungo rusaba icyemezo cyo kwimura amatungo (movement certificate) [V REG-24].
2. **Pima intera:** buri nzu y'amatungo igomba kuba nibura kuri m 200 uvuye ku murongo wa kure cyane amazi y'ikiyaga yagezeho mu gihe cy'imyuzure (flood line), na m 20 uvuye ku gishanga icyo ari cyo cyose; utabyubahirije acibwa ihazabu ya RWF 500,000 kandi inzu igasenywa [V LOC-01, LOC-02].
3. **Hitamo umuganga w'amatungo:** umuganga w'amatungo w'umurenge cyangwa w'akarere, cyangwa umuganga w'amatungo wigenga ukorera kimwe mu bigo byahawe ububasha na Leta (mandated companies) [V LOC-48].
4. **Banza uruzitiro n'amazi:** uruzitiro rw'urwuri, amazi ageze kuri buri nzu, n'ibiti bitanga igicucu mu nguni z'urwuri ([igishushanyo mbonera](../master-plan.md)).
5. **Gura ku ituragiro rizwi (hatchery):** imishwi yaho iba yarakingiwe indwara z'inkoko zitwa Newcastle disease, Gumboro, Marek's disease, infectious bronchitis na avian influenza (ibicurane by'ibiguruka) [V LVS-68].

| Inkuta n'igisenge | inkuta zifunze kugeza kuri m 1, hejuru yazo urushundura rw'icyuma (wire mesh) rwinjiza umwuka; igisenge kirenga inkuta ho m 0.6 kugira ngo imvura itinjira |

Icyo inkoko 100 zitanga [E]: iyo zitera ku kigero cya 80%, ni hafi amagi 80 ku munsi: amagi 20 kugeza kuri 30 ajya mu gikoni gikuru, n'amakarito abiri yo kugurisha buri munsi. Ibiryo bitwara hafi RWF 8,000 ku munsi, bityo inyungu ishingiye ku giciro ifamu ibona ku magi yayo.

## Kugurisha

1. Amagi abanza kujya mu gikoni gikuru, asagutse agahabwa abashyitsi n'abaguzi b'i Kigali; nta ruhushya rwo kugurisha amagi rwabonetse ko rukenewe.
2. Ihene zigurishwa ari nzima: kugurisha inyama bisaba uruhushya rwa RICA rwo kubaga amatungo (slaughter licence), aho kubagira handitswe (registered premise) n'imodoka itwara inyama yanditswe (registered carrier) [V REG-03, REG-06].
3. Inyama zigaburirwa mu gikoni gikuru ziva mu ibagiro rifite uruhushya.
4. Shyira inkoko mu bwishingizi binyuze muri gahunda y'igihugu y'ubwishingizi bw'ubuhinzi n'ubworozi (yishingira ibiguruka byororwa, ariko ntiyishingira ihene) [V MKT-21].

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Umworozi yiyandikishije, intera za m 200 na m 20 zapimwe, kandi umuganga w'amatungo yatoranyijwe.
2. Inzu y'inkoko yubatswe ku bipimo biri hejuru, kandi inkoko 100 za mbere zayigezemo.
3. Amagi agera mu gikoni gikuru buri gitondo, kandi buri igi, buri kilo cy'ibiryo na buri muti watanzwe byose byandikwa.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Umurima n'inzu z'amatungo ziwurimo biri ku ntera ingana iki uvuye ku murongo w'imyuzure y'ikiyaga (flood line)?
2. Ni nde uzita ku matungo buri munsi, kandi ni nde uzamusimbura ku munsi w'ikiruhuko cye?
3. Ese akarere kamaze gukuraho, mu nyandiko, ingamba zafashwe kubera Umuriro wa Rift Valley?
