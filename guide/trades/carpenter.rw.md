# Amabwiriza y'umubaji: imbere muri sitidiyo

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: verisiyo {{studio.rev}}, {{studio.rev_date}}, {{studio.status}}. Ibipimo biva ku mafoto kugeza igihe hapimwe ku Munsi wa 1; atelier ntigira icyo ikata mbere ya verisiyo B.

Ibyo umubaji akeneye byose ngo atunganye imbere muri kontineri ya 20 ft ibe icyumba cy'abashyitsi (sitidiyo), intambwe ku yindi, n'ibipimo byose. Sitidiyo zombi zirasa, bityo aya mabwiriza akora kuri zombi.

## Amagambo akoreshwa hano

- **End A (impera A)**: urukuta rufunze rw'impera, iruhande rw'igitanda. **End B (impera B)**: impera ifite inzugi za kontineri, iruhande rw'ubwiherero.
- **Back wall (urukuta rw'inyuma)**: urukuta rurerure rudafite aho gufungukira (igitanda na sofa). **Front wall (urukuta rw'imbere)**: urukuta rurerure rufite urugi runyerera rw'ikirahure n'idirishya rito W1.
- **From the steel (uhereye ku cyuma)**: bipimwe uhereye ku cyuma cyambaye ubusa, mbere yo gushyiraho lining. **From the finished wall (uhereye ku rukuta rurangiye)**: bipimwe uhereye ku isura ya lining.
- Ibipimo byose ni milimetero (mm) keretse handitswe ukundi.

## Ingingo eshanu zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Ibipimo byagarutse.** Ibipimo byo ku Munsi wa 1 byageze, verisiyo B irasohoka. Kugeza icyo gihe atelier ntigira icyo ikata; imirimo yo ku kibanza S01 kugeza S03 irakomeza.
2. **Gusudira byarangiye.** Gusudira no gusya (grinding) kose (S01) birangira mbere y'uko foam ishyirwamo.
3. **Igerageza ry'umuvuduko ryatsinze.** First fix (S04) igeragezwa kuri bar 10, ibisubizo bikandikwa mbere ya foam.
4. **Foam yumye.** Foam imaze amasaha 24 yuma kandi yaciwe ingana n'ibiti mbere y'urupapuro rwa mbere rwa lining.
5. **Inkuta z'amazi zagenzuwe.** Membrane y'ubwiherero igenzurwa mbere y'uko ushyira amakaro atangira.

Amategeko abiri adahinduka:

- Ntuzigere ukuba (sand), uharura (plane) cyangwa ukata hasi ha kontineri: hatewe umuti wica udukoko. Hapfuke na membrane, hanyuma ushyireho impapuro nshya ukoresheje vis.
- Pima uhereye ku cyuma nk'uko imbonerahamwe zibivuga, hanyuma ugenzure ibipimo birangiye ukurikije aya mabwiriza.

## Umunsi wa 1: pima kontineri

Pima kontineri zombi za sitidiyo mbere y'ikindi cyose, wohereze imibare uwo munsi. Andika **U1** na **U2** ku nzugi zazo ukoresheje ingwa (chalk). Kontineri ya gatatu ni ibiro (office); ntipimwa hano.

[Table: Urupapuro rw'ibipimo, uzuza inkingi ya value_mm](../../study/studio/survey.csv)

1. **int_length**: uburebure bw'imbere, kuva ku cyuma cya end A kugeza ku cyuma cya end B, kuri m 1 hejuru ya hasi.
2. **int_width**: ubugari bw'imbere, icyuma ku cyuma, kuri m 1 hejuru ya hasi, hagati mu burebure.
3. **int_height**: kuva hasi kugeza ku cyuma cy'igisenge, hagati mu bugari.
4. **o1_start** na **o1_end**: aho umwanya munini w'urugi runyerera utangirira n'aho urangirira, uhereye ku cyuma cya end A.
5. **o2_start** na **o2_end**: aho urugi rwa kera rufunganye rutangirira n'aho rurangirira, uhereye ku cyuma cya end A.
6. **diag_diff**: pima diyagonale zombi za hasi, inguni ku nguni; andika itandukaniro.
7. **level_diff**: ukoresheje level y'amazi, inguni (corner casting) iri hejuru ukuyemo iri hasi.
8. Fata amafoto: buri rukuta urureba imbere, hasi, inguni enye (corner castings), ikadiri y'urugi runyerera, urugi rwa kera rufunganye.

## S01: gupima, kuringaniza no gusudira kose

1. Uzuza urupapuro rw'ibipimo (Umunsi wa 1 hejuru).
2. Ringaniza inguni enye (corner castings) ku bitereko bya beto (concrete pads) cyangwa ibyuma byo kuringaniza (shims); inguni iri ku rutare ikeneye pad. Gerageza ko itandukaniro hagati y'inguni iri hejuru n'iri hasi riba munsi ya mm 5, uryandike muri level_diff.
3. Umusuderi: sudira icyuma cya mm 2 mu rugi rwa kera rufunganye (kuva {{studio.w1_x0}} kugeza {{studio.w1_x1}} uhereye ku rukuta rurangiye rwa end A, kugeza kuri {{studio.w1_sill}} hejuru), ushyireho idirishya rya louvre 520 × 890 hejuru yacyo.
4. Umusuderi: funga kandi ushyireho ingufuri ku nzugi za kontineri za end B, ukate umwobo wa mm 75 wa AC mu rukuta rw'inyuma.
5. Guhera kuri S05, nta gusudira cyangwa gusya kuri kontineri: byatwika foam.

## S02: membrane n'impapuro za hasi

![AS-02 Membrane and floor sheets](../../public/designs/studio/drawings/as-02.svg)

1. Siga rust converter, hanyuma zinc primer, ku cyuma cyambaye ubusa cyangwa cyafashwe n'ingese ku nkuta no ku gisenge (L 4 kuri sitidiyo).
2. Kubura hasi ha kontineri. Shyiraho isashi ya membrane (250 µm) hasi hose: ibice byegeranye bigahurira kuri mm 150 (laps), bifatanyijwe na tape, bizamurwa mm 50 ku nkuta ({{studio.membrane_m2}} m² harimo laps).
3. Shyiraho impapuro za pulayiwudi 12 mm (2440 × 1220), uruhande rurerure rukurikiye kontineri, umwanya wa mm 3 hagati y'impapuro na mm 5 uvuye ku cyuma: impapuro {{studio.floor_sheets}} kuri sitidiyo.
4. Umurongo wa 1, ukurikiye urukuta rw'inyuma: urupapuro rwuzuye, urundi rwuzuye, hanyuma urwa nyuma rukatwa ngo rukwire (uburebure bwa hafi 1000).
5. Umurongo wa 2, ukurikiye urukuta rw'imbere, ukatwa ku bugari busigaye (hafi 1120): tangira n'igice cy'urupapuro (uburebure 1220, kivuye ku gisigazwa cy'umurongo wa 1), hanyuma urupapuro rwuzuye, hanyuma urwa nyuma rukatwa ngo rukwire (uburebure bwa hafi 2220). Nta guhurira (joint) guhuye n'ukundi ko mu murongo wa 1.
6. Bifunge ukoresheje vis z'igiti 4 × 30 (bag F) buri mm 300 mu mpande zombi, hafi {{studio.floor_screws}} kuri sitidiyo. Koresha vis za mm 30 gusa, kugira ngo zigume muri hasi ha kontineri ha mm 28.
7. Genzura: nta ntera iri ku guhurira na kumwe, kandi hasi harangana kugeza kuri mm 3 munsi y'umutambiko ugororotse wa m 2.

## S03: ibiti (battens)

![AS-03 Battens](../../public/designs/studio/drawings/as-03.svg)

1. Koresha ibiti byatewe umuti 38 × 50, bihagaze ku mpapuro za hasi: {{studio.battens}} kuri sitidiyo.
2. Shyira igiti kimwe munsi ya buri guhurira kw'impapuro za lining, n'ikindi hagati yabyo, kugira ngo nta mwanya urenga mm 600. Shyira akamenyetso hagati ya buri giti uhereye ku cyuma, nk'uko imbonerahamwe ibivuga.
3. Fata buri giti ku cyuma ukoresheje vis zifite amababa zicukura ubwazo 5.5 × 50 (bag L): imwe kuri buri mpera n'imwe buri mm 600, {{studio.batten_screws}} kuri sitidiyo.
4. Hejuru y'urugi runyerera, shyiraho igiti kimwe gitambitse ku mutwe wacyo kuri {{studio.slider_head}} (ibice bibiri bya 1500).
5. Genzura: buri giti gihagaze neza (plumb), kandi byose biri ku rwego rumwe: umutambiko ugororotse ushyizwe ku biti bitatu ukora kuri byose uko ari bitatu.

## S05: spray foam, hanyuma impapuro za lining

![AS-05 Spray foam and lining panels](../../public/designs/studio/drawings/as-05.svg)

1. Ushyiraho spray foam yuzuza hagati y'ibiti foam ifunze (closed-cell), hafi mm 38, ku nkuta no ku gisenge: {{studio.foam_m2}} m² kuri sitidiyo. Banza upfuke ikadiri y'urugi runyerera, idirishya na hasi.
2. Nyuma y'amasaha 24, kata foam ingane n'ibiti.
3. Shyiraho impapuro {{studio.lining_panels}} za lining zifite nimero (pulayiwudi 12, nimero K1-01 kugeza K1-15), banza igisenge, hanyuma inkuta. Buri ruhande rw'urupapuro rugwa hagati ku giti.
4. Fata ukoresheje vis z'igiti 4 × 30 buri mm 200 ku mpande na buri mm 300 hagati, hafi {{studio.lining_screws}} kuri sitidiyo.
5. Genzura buri mwobo watunganyijwe (cut-out) ugereranyije n'agasanduku kawo mbere yo gufunga urupapuro; hanyuma ugenzure ko nta foam igaragara kandi isura z'impapuro ziri ku rwego rumwe.

## Atelier: gukata ibice bya kits

Ni nyuma ya verisiyo B gusa, ivuye ku bipimo.

1. Gura impapuro z'ibiti za sitidiyo zombi icyarimwe (batch imwe). Pima impapuro icumi: pulayiwudi 18 igomba kuba 18.0 ± 0.3 mm, bitabaye ibyo bimenyeshe uwakoze igishushanyo mbere yo gukata.
2. Kata mu mirongo (± 0.5 mm), hanyuma ukate mu bice; andika nimero ya buri gice ku isura itagaragara ako kanya. Impapuro nini: diyagonale zingana kugeza kuri mm 1.
3. Shyiraho edge band aho cut list ibigaragaza: L1 ni uruhande rumwe rurerure, L2S2 ni impande zose.
4. Cukura ukoresheje templates: banza ucape T-01 kuri 100% ugenzure akabari kayo ka mm 100.
5. Teranya by'igerageza igikoni n'igitanda bya sitidiyo 1 muri atelier mbere yo gupakira.
6. Pakira ipaki imwe kuri buri kit kuri buri sitidiyo, itarenze hafi kg 25, yanditseho "Studio n / Kit / bundle x of y".

Kuri sitidiyo: ibice {{studio.parts}}, biri muri [cut list](../../public/designs/studio/data/cut-list.csv), hamwe n'ibishushanyo byo gukata C-101 kugeza C-107 na dosiye za CNC.

## Mbere yo kuvuga ko byarangiye

1. Inzugi n'utubati dukururwa bigenda neza, urugi rw'ubwiherero rufunguka nta kirubangamira, urugi runyerera rufungwa, insect mesh irareze.
2. Nta foam igaragara, buri guhurira kw'impapuro kuri ku giti, nta mutwe wa vis usohoka.
3. Imyanya isigara: inzira ku mpera ya sofa nibura {{studio.aisle_min}}, 600 imbere ya WC, 600 aho binjirira muri douche.
4. Igishushanyo ubwacyo cyatsinze igenzura ryacyo 33 ([checks](../../public/designs/studio/data/checks.csv)).

## Ibibazo bigomba gusubizwa mbere y'intambwe ivugwa

1. Ni izihe kontineri ebyiri ziba U1 na U2? Mbere y'uko Umunsi wa 1 urangira.
2. Hari umusuderi wateganyijwe kuri S01? Mbere y'uko Umunsi wa 1 urangira.
3. Ni nde uzashyiraho spray foam? Nta muhanga wa spray foam i Kigali uremezwa. Mbere ya S05.
4. Ubunini bw'impapuro za hasi: mm 12, keretse ibipimo bigaragaje ko hasi ha kera hatarangana birenze mm 5 kuri m 2; icyo gihe ni mm 18 na verisiyo nshya. Mbere ya S02.
