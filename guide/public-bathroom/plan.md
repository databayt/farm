# Public bathroom: execution plan

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The founder's brief gives the fittings, not their sizes: every size is a design choice in public-bathroom.json [A]. Rwanda's rules for a public toilet are not yet checked [U].

Every step of building the bathroom that everyone without a bathroom of their own uses, in the order the page's build-up draws, from the septic's size to the first guest.

The bathroom drawn: [the public bathroom page](/en/public-bathroom) · Its other pages: [materials](materials.md), [electric](electric.md), [plumbing](plumbing.md) · Each step done in full: [the studio's plan](../studio/plan.md)

## Before you start

1. Size the septic tank for this building's users: it was sized for the two studios, and the campers, the bar and the fire pit must be added before it is built ([the farm's water](../farm/water.md)).
2. Decide what the brief leaves open: a women's and a men's side, a shower for the campers, an accessible toilet room.
3. Measure the container as the studio's survey does, and book the welder for the hot work it finds: the door at the front's east end, the frames.

## Steps

1. **Welder:** all the hot work: the door moved to the east end of the front, the windows' frames, before anything that can burn goes in.
2. **The box:** the outer walls lined on the plinth; the fieldstone landing and its step at the door.
3. **Plumber:** the first fix: the PP-R supply in the linings 300 mm up, the uPVC drains under the floor and out through the plinth to the inspection chamber; every pipe held at 10 bar for an hour before anything closes over it ([plumbing](plumbing.md)).
4. **Electrician:** the first fix for the 4 circuits from the board by the cleaner's cupboard; the tests ([electric](electric.md)).
5. **Carpenter:** the partitions: timber studs, cement board both sides, 100 mm.
6. **Tiler:** the floor tiles falling to the lobby's drain, the wall tiles to 1.5 m.
7. **Plumber and carpenter:** the fittings: the cleaner's cupboard, the two WCs, the basin counter with its mirror, the towels and the urinal ([materials](materials.md)).
8. **Electrician:** the second fix (the lights with their motion sensors, the urinal room's fan, the light over the door, the cleaner's socket), then the tests and the certificate.

## Hold points

1. **The septic sized first.** Nothing drains to the septic tank before it is sized for this building's users.
2. **Measure before you cut.** Nothing is cut or ordered before the container is measured.
3. **Nothing buried untested.** The pipes hold 10 bar for an hour and the cables pass their tests before the tiles go on.
4. **No guests before the inspection.** No one uses the bathroom before the septic system passes its inspection (D-016).

## Done when

1. The plumbing and electrical tests pass and the certificate is signed.
2. The septic system has passed its inspection.
3. Every room's light comes on with someone in it, and the door's light comes on at 18:00 with the path lights.

## Open questions

1. A women's side and a men's side, or every room open to everyone, as drawn?
2. A shower for the campers? The tents have no washing of their own; a shower room would take a fitting's place or a second building.
3. An accessible toilet room fits only by replanning the box round it; is it needed, and what does Rwanda's rule ask?
4. The guest rooms: this file says they have their own bathrooms (D-029), but guest-room.json draws none. Its users, and so the septic's size, depend on it.
