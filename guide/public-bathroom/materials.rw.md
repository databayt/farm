# Ubwiherero rusange: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano ziva muri public-bathroom.json, zigomba kwemezwa n'umufundi w'amazi (plumber) n'umuyagankuba (electrician); nta kintu kirashakirwa aho kiva cyangwa ngo gihabwe igiciro [A].

Ibyo ubwiherero bukeneye: ibikoresho byabwo (fixtures), ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi n'ibice by'imiyoboro y'amazi hamwe n'amatiyo yabyo, nk'uko urupapuro rwabwo rubigaragaza.

English: [read this list in English](materials.md) · Ubwiherero mu bishushanyo: [urupapuro rw'ubwiherero rusange](/en/public-bathroom) · Igihe bikenewe: [gahunda y'imirimo](plan.md)

## Ibyo kugura

[Ibikoresho (fixtures), ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi n'imiyoboro y'amazi](../../src/components/public-bathroom/docs-sections.tsx#PublicBathroomMaterials)

Amakaro, cement board n'inkingi z'ibiti (studs) biva ku buryo urupapuro rugaragaza imyubakire (build-up); septic tank ni iy'ifamu ([ibikoresho by'ifamu](../farm/materials.md)).

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: WC n'aho kunyara (urinal), counter ya lavabo, amakaro.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/public-bathroom/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
