# Public bathroom: plumbing

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. Every run and size is a design choice for the plumber to confirm [A]; the septic tank's new size is not set [U].

Everything the plumber needs for the public bathroom: the supply, the drains and the vent to the septic tank, the tests and every point.

Kinyarwanda: [soma iyi paji mu Kinyarwanda](plumbing.rw.md) · The pipes drawn: [the public bathroom page](/en/public-bathroom#plumbing) · When it happens: [the execution plan](plan.md) · The method in full: [the studio's plumbing](../studio/plumbing.md)

## At a glance

| What | Design |
|---|---|
| Supply | the farm's tank and booster pump at 2.5 to 3 bar, as the studio's ([the farm's water](../farm/water.md)); the stop valve, filter and meter in the cleaner's cupboard, where only staff reach them |
| Pipe | PP-R PN20: 25 mm for the main, 20 mm inside, in the lining 300 mm above the floor, then straight up to each outlet |
| Hot water | none: the basins are cold, as the studio's (D-053), until a shower is added |
| Drains | uPVC under the floor in the plinth, falling at least 1:40, a rodding eye at every bend, out through the back of the plinth to an inspection chamber (IC1), then to the farm's septic tank |
| Vent | a 75 mm open vent |

## Hold points

1. **The septic sized first.** Nothing drains to the septic tank before it is re-sized for the campers, the bar and the fire pit.
2. **The 10 bar test.** Every pipe holds 10 bar for an hour before the tiles go on.
3. **No guests before the inspection.** The septic system passes its inspection first (D-016).

## Steps

1. **First fix:** the main in from the farm's tank to the cleaner's cupboard; the supply in the linings 300 mm up to every outlet; the drains under the floor and out through the plinth to IC1; the vent.
2. **Tests before the tiles:** 10 bar for an hour; photograph every wall; sign the release.
3. **Second fix:** the WCs, the urinal, the basins and their push taps, the floor drains.
4. **Commissioning:** fill-test every drain to IC1; smoke-test the vent.
