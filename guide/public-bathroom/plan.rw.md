## Mbere yo gutangira

1. Hitamo ingano ya septic tank ihwanye n'abazakoresha iyi nyubako: yari yarabariwe sitidiyo zombi, kandi abakambitse, akabari n'umuriro (fire pit) bigomba kongerwamo mbere y'uko yubakwa ([amazi y'ifamu](../farm/water.md)).
2. Fata icyemezo ku byo ibyasabwe bisize bidasobanutse: uruhande rw'abagore n'urw'abagabo, douche y'abakambitse, icyumba cy'ubwiherero gikoreshwa n'abafite ubumuga (accessible).
3. Pima kontineri nk'uko isuzuma rya sitidiyo ribikora, kandi uteganye umusuderi ku mirimo yo gusudira isuzuma rizagaragaza: urugi ku mpera y'iburasirazuba y'imbere, amakadiri.

## Intambwe

1. **Umusuderi:** imirimo yose yo gusudira: urugi rwimurirwa ku mpera y'iburasirazuba y'imbere, amakadiri y'amadirishya, mbere y'uko hashyirwamo ikintu cyose gishobora gushya.
2. **Inyubako:** inkuta zo hanze zishyirwaho lining ku gitereko; aho kugera ku rugi h'amabuye (landing) n'intambwe yaho.
3. **Umufundi w'amazi (plumber):** first fix: amatiyo ya PP-R y'amazi meza muri lining, mm 300 hejuru, amatiyo ya uPVC y'amazi yanduye munsi ya hasi asohoka anyuze mu gitereko agana kuri inspection chamber; buri tiyo igumana bar 10 mu gihe cy'isaha imwe mbere y'uko hari ikintu kiyifunga hejuru ([imiyoboro y'amazi](plumbing.md)).
4. **Umuyagankuba (electrician):** first fix y'imizunguruko 4 uhereye kuri board iri muri lobby; igerageza ([amashanyarazi](electric.md)).
5. **Umubaji:** partitions: inkingi z'ibiti (studs), cement board ku mpande zombi, mm 100.
6. **Ushyiraho amakaro (tiler):** amakaro ya hasi ahanamye agana ku mwobo w'amazi wa lobby, amakaro y'inkuta kugeza kuri m 1.5.
7. **Umufundi w'amazi n'umubaji:** ibikoresho: akabati k'ukora isuku, WC ebyiri, counter ya lavabo n'indorerwamo yayo, ibitambaro n'aho kunyara (urinal) ([ibikoresho](materials.md)).
8. **Umuyagankuba:** second fix (amatara afite sensor zibona umuntu (motion sensors), fan y'icyumba cyo kunyaramo, itara riri hejuru y'urugi, priza y'ukora isuku), hanyuma igerageza n'icyemezo (certificate).

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Ingano ya septic ibanza.** Nta mazi yanduye ajya muri septic tank mbere y'uko ibarirwa abazakoresha iyi nyubako.
2. **Pima mbere yo gukata.** Nta kintu gikatwa cyangwa gitumizwa mbere y'uko kontineri ipimwa.
3. **Nta kintu gihishwa kitageragejwe.** Amatiyo agumana bar 10 mu gihe cy'isaha imwe kandi insinga zigatsinda igerageza ryazo mbere y'uko amakaro ashyirwaho.
4. **Nta bashyitsi mbere y'igenzura.** Nta muntu ukoresha ubwiherero mbere y'uko septic system itsinda igenzura ryayo (D-016).

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Igerageza ry'imiyoboro y'amazi n'iry'amashanyarazi ryatsinze kandi icyemezo (certificate) cyasinywe.
2. Septic system yatsinze igenzura ryayo.
3. Itara rya buri cyumba ryaka iyo harimo umuntu, kandi itara ry'urugi ryaka saa 18:00 hamwe n'amatara y'inzira.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Uruhande rw'abagore n'urw'abagabo, cyangwa buri cyumba gikoreshwa na bose, nk'uko byashushanyijwe?
2. Douche y'abakambitse? Amahema nta hantu ho kwiyuhagira afite; icyumba cya douche cyafata umwanya w'igikoresho kimwe cyangwa kigasaba indi nyubako.
3. Icyumba cy'ubwiherero gikoreshwa n'abafite ubumuga gishobora kujyamo gusa iyo inyubako yongeye gutegurwa hashingiwe kuri cyo; ese kirakenewe, kandi itegeko ry'u Rwanda risaba iki?
4. Ibyumba by'abashyitsi ubu bifite ubwiherero bwabyo (D-061), bityo ubu bwiherero bukorera abakambitse, akabari, umuriro (fire pit) n'abakozi ba galley. Ni abantu bangahe ku isaha iba ifite abantu benshi? Ingano ya septic ishingira kuri ibyo.
