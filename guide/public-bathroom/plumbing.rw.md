# Ubwiherero rusange: imiyoboro y'amazi

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Buri muyoboro na buri ngano ni amahitamo y'igishushanyo umufundi w'amazi (plumber) agomba kwemeza [A]; ingano nshya ya septic tank ntiremezwa [U].

Ibyo umufundi w'amazi akeneye byose ku bwiherero rusange: amazi meza, imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye na vent bijya kuri septic tank, igerageza na buri point.

English: [read this page in English](plumbing.md) · Amatiyo mu bishushanyo: [urupapuro rw'ubwiherero rusange](/en/public-bathroom#plumbing) · Igihe bikorwa: [gahunda y'imirimo](plan.md) · Uburyo bwose: [imiyoboro y'amazi ya sitidiyo](../studio/plumbing.md)

## Mu ncamake

| Iki | Uko byateganyijwe |
|---|---|
| Amazi meza (supply) | ikigega cy'ifamu na pompe yongera umuvuduko (booster pump) kuri bar 2.5 kugeza 3, nk'ibya sitidiyo ([amazi y'ifamu](../farm/water.md)); stop valve (robine ifunga amazi), filter na mubazi (meter) biri mu kabati k'ukora isuku, aho abakozi bonyine babigeraho |
| Itiyo | PP-R PN20: mm 25 ku muyoboro mukuru (main), mm 20 imbere, muri lining mm 300 hejuru ya hasi, hanyuma ikazamuka igororotse ijya kuri buri aho amazi asohokera (outlet) |
| Amazi ashyushye | nta yo: lavabo zihabwa amazi akonje, nk'iza sitidiyo (D-053), kugeza douche yongewemo |
| Imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye (drains) | uPVC munsi ya hasi mu gitereko, ifite ubuhaname bwa 1:40 cyangwa burenzeho, rodding eye (aho basukurira umuyoboro) kuri buri coude, isohoka inyuma y'igitereko ijya kuri inspection chamber (IC1), hanyuma kuri septic tank y'ifamu |
| Umuyoboro w'umwuka (vent) | open vent ya mm 75 |

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Ingano ya septic ibanza.** Nta mazi yanduye ajya muri septic tank mbere y'uko yongera kubarirwa abakambitse, akabari n'umuriro (fire pit).
2. **Igerageza rya bar 10.** Buri tiyo igumana bar 10 mu gihe cy'isaha imwe mbere y'uko amakaro ashyirwaho.
3. **Nta bashyitsi mbere y'igenzura.** Septic system ibanza gutsinda igenzura ryayo (D-016).

## Intambwe

1. **First fix:** umuyoboro mukuru uva ku kigega cy'ifamu ujya mu kabati k'ukora isuku; amazi meza muri lining, mm 300 hejuru, ajya kuri buri aho asohokera; imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye munsi ya hasi isohoka inyuze mu gitereko ijya kuri IC1; vent.
2. **Igerageza mbere y'amakaro:** bar 10 mu gihe cy'isaha imwe; fotora buri rukuta; sinya uruhushya (release).
3. **Second fix:** WC, aho kunyara (urinal), lavabo na robine zazo zikandwa (push taps), imyobo y'amazi yo hasi (floor drains).
4. **Gutangiza (commissioning):** gerageza buri muyoboro w'amazi yanduye uwuzuza amazi (fill-test) kugeza kuri IC1; gerageza vent ukoresheje umwotsi (smoke-test).
