# Bar: execution plan

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The container is on site today; every size past its shell is a design choice in bar.json [A].

## Before you start

1. Measure the container as the studio's survey does: the hatch, its ledge, the cargo doors.
2. Book the welder for the hot work the survey finds: the flap's struts, and the ledge's brackets if it needs them.

## Steps

1. **Welder:** all the hot work, the flap's struts among it, before anything that can burn goes in.
2. **Carpenter:** the membrane and the floor sheets, then the battens, as in [the studio's plan](../studio/plan.md).
3. **Electrician and plumber:** the first fix: the supply to the board by the way in and the cables for the 7 circuits; the cold main, the heater's feed and the drains to the grease trap ([electric](electric.md), [plumbing](plumbing.md)).
4. **Both:** the tests before anything closes: continuity and insulation on every cable, every pipe held at 10 bar for an hour; photographs; the release.
5. **Insulation and linings:** insulation and off-white painted ply, as the studio's [A]; the oak planks.
6. **Carpenter:** the work counter under the hatch and the back bar ([materials](materials.md)).
7. **Electrician and plumber:** the second fix (the board, the coffee machine's spur, the sockets, the lights inside and the flap's light; the sink, its heater and the coffee machine's feed), then the tests and the certificate.
8. **Carpenter:** the deck along the courtyard side and its step; then the four chairs and two low tables set out on it.

## Hold points

1. **Measure before you cut.** Nothing is cut or ordered before the container is measured.
2. **No welding after the insulation.** All hot work comes first.
3. **Nothing buried untested.** The cables pass their tests and the pipes hold 10 bar before the linings close.
4. **No power on before the certificate.** A RURA class A electrician signs the tests first.

## Done when

1. The electrical certificate is signed and the plumbing has passed its tests.
2. The flap opens on its struts over the ledge, and the lounge is set out on the deck.
3. The bar serves its first night.

## Open questions

1. Which coffee machine? Its 3 to 4 kW sets the coffee circuit and most of the bar's 5 kW peak [E].
2. The soak pit: the galley's, or one of its own?
