# Akabari: imiyoboro y'amazi

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Buri nzira y'amatiyo na buri ngano ni amahitamo y'igishushanyo umufundi w'amazi agomba kugenzura; nta kintu kirashakwa cyangwa ngo gihabwe igiciro [A].

Ibyo umufundi w'amazi (plumber) akeneye byose ku kabari: aho amazi aturuka (supply), amazi ajya ku mashini ya kawa, heater nto, imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye ijya kuri grease trap, igerageza na buri point.

## Mu ncamake

| Iki | Igishushanyo (design) |
|---|---|
| Aho amazi aturuka (supply) | umuyoboro umwe w'amazi akonje uturuka ku kigega cy'ifamu na pompe yacyo yongera umuvuduko (booster pump) kuri bar 2.5 kugeza 3 ([amazi y'ifamu](../farm/water.md)), winjira munsi y'impera y'amajyepfo |
| Aho amazi yinjirira (main in) | robine yo gufunga (stop valve), Y-filter na check meter (mubazi yo kugenzura) mu kabati ka back bar munsi y'imashini ya kawa; aho niho hava filter y'ingese (scale filter) n'amazi y'imashini ubwayo |
| Amazi ashyushye | amazi akonje anyura munsi ya hasi yambukiranya inzira akagera kuri sinki, aho heater ya L 15 iri munsi ya sinki (under-sink) iha sinki amazi ashyushye |
| Imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye | sinki n'agasahani gafata ibitonyanga by'imashini ya kawa (drip tray) bimanuka kuri 1:40 cyangwa birenzeho bijya kuri grease trap nto hanze y'impera y'amajyepfo, hanyuma muri soak pit, ntibijye na rimwe muri septic tank |

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Ibiti bibanza.** Tangira first fix gusa iyo ibiti (battens) bimaze gufatwa kandi impapuro za hasi zimaze gushyirwaho.
2. **Igerageza rya bar 10.** Buri tiyo igumana bar 10 mu isaha imwe, byanditswe kandi byasinywe, mbere y'uko lining zifungwa.
3. **Grease trap ibanza.** Nta muyoboro w'amazi yanduye uhuzwa mbere y'uko grease trap na soak pit yayo bishyirwaho (D-055).

## Intambwe

1. **First fix:** umuyoboro mukuru w'amazi akonje winjira munsi y'impera y'amajyepfo unyuze muri stop valve, filter na mubazi; amazi y'imashini ya kawa anyura muri scale filter yayo; amazi akonje yambukiranya inzira ajya kuri sinki; imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye ijya kuri grease trap.
2. **Igerageza mbere ya lining:** bar 10 mu isaha imwe; fotora buri rukuta; sinya uruhushya (release).
3. **Second fix:** sinki na robine yayo, heater iri munsi ya sinki, amazi y'imashini ya kawa n'umuyoboro w'amazi ava muri drip tray yayo.
4. **Gutangiza (commissioning):** gerageza imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye uyuzuza amazi kugeza kuri grease trap; fungura amazi ashyushye kuri sinki.
