## Mbere yo gutangira

1. Pima kontineri nk'uko ipima rya sitidiyo ribikora: hatch, ledge yayo, inzugi za kontineri.
2. Teganya umusuderi ku mirimo yo gusudira ipima rizagaragaza: struts za flap, na brackets za ledge niba izikeneye.

## Intambwe

1. **Umusuderi:** imirimo yose yo gusudira, harimo struts za flap, mbere y'uko hashyirwamo ikintu cyose gishobora gushya.
2. **Umubaji:** membrane n'impapuro za hasi, hanyuma ibiti (battens), nk'uko biri muri [gahunda ya sitidiyo](../studio/plan.md).
3. **Umuyagankuba (electrician) n'umufundi w'amazi (plumber):** first fix: umuriro ujya kuri board iri aho binjirira n'insinga z'imizunguruko 7; umuyoboro mukuru w'amazi akonje, amazi ajya kuri heater n'imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye ijya kuri grease trap ([amashanyarazi](electric.md), [imiyoboro y'amazi](plumbing.md)).
4. **Bombi:** igerageza mbere y'uko hagira igifungwa: continuity na insulation kuri buri nsinga, buri tiyo igumana bar 10 mu isaha imwe; amafoto; uruhushya (release).
5. **Insulation na lining:** insulation na pulayiwudi isize irangi ry'umweru wijimye, nk'iya sitidiyo [A]; imbaho z'igiti (oak planks).
6. **Umubaji:** ameza y'akazi munsi ya hatch na back bar ([ibikoresho](materials.md)).
7. **Umuyagankuba n'umufundi w'amazi:** second fix (board, spur y'imashini ya kawa, priza, amatara yo imbere n'itara rya flap; sinki, heater yayo n'amazi ajya ku mashini ya kawa), hanyuma igerageza n'icyemezo (certificate).
8. **Umubaji:** deck iri iruhande rw'urugo n'intambwe yayo; hanyuma intebe enye n'ameza magufi abiri bishyirwa kuri yo.

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Pima mbere yo gukata.** Nta kintu gikatwa cyangwa gitumizwa mbere y'uko kontineri ipimwa.
2. **Nta gusudira nyuma ya insulation.** Imirimo yose yo gusudira ibanza.
3. **Nta gihishwa kitageragejwe.** Insinga zitsinda igerageza ryazo kandi amatiyo agumana bar 10 mbere y'uko lining zifungwa.
4. **Nta muriro mbere y'icyemezo.** Umuyagankuba wa RURA class A abanza gusinya igerageza.

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Icyemezo cy'amashanyarazi cyasinywe kandi imiyoboro y'amazi yatsinze igerageza ryayo.
2. Flap ifunguka ku struts zayo hejuru ya ledge, kandi lounge iteguye kuri deck.
3. Akabari kakiriye abakiriya ijoro rya mbere.
