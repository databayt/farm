# Bar: plumbing

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. Every run and size is a design choice for the plumber to check; nothing is sourced or priced [A].

Everything the plumber needs for the bar: the supply, the coffee machine's feed, the small heater, the drains to the grease trap, the tests and every point.

The pipes drawn: [the bar page](/en/bar#plumbing) · When it happens: [the execution plan](plan.md) · The method in full: [the studio's plumbing](../studio/plumbing.md)

## At a glance

| What | Design |
|---|---|
| Supply | one cold supply from the farm's tank and booster pump at 2.5 to 3 bar ([the farm's water](../farm/water.md)), in under the south end |
| Main in | a stop valve, a Y-filter and a check meter in the back bar's cupboard under the coffee machine; from there a scale filter and the machine's own feed |
| Hot water | cold under the floor across the aisle to the sink unit, where a 15 L under-sink heater gives the sink its hot |
| Drains | the sink and the coffee machine's drip tray at 1:40 or steeper to a small grease trap outside the south end, then a soak pit, never the septic tank |

## Hold points

1. **Battens first.** Start the first fix only when the battens are fixed and the floor sheets are down.
2. **The 10 bar test.** Every pipe holds 10 bar for an hour, written down and signed, before the linings close.
3. **The grease trap first.** No drain is connected before the grease trap and its soak pit are in (D-055).

## Steps

1. **First fix:** the cold main in under the south end through the stop valve, filter and meter; the coffee machine's feed through its scale filter; the cold across the aisle to the sink; the drains to the grease trap.
2. **Tests before the linings:** 10 bar for an hour; photograph every wall; sign the release.
3. **Second fix:** the sink and its tap, the under-sink heater, the coffee machine's feed and its drip tray's waste.
4. **Commissioning:** fill-test the drains to the grease trap; run the hot at the sink.
