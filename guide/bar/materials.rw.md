# Akabari: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano ziva muri bar.json; nta kintu kirashakwa cyangwa ngo gihabwe igiciro [A].

Ibyo akabari gakeneye: ibikoresho byako, ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi n'ibice by'imiyoboro y'amazi, nk'uko urupapuro rwako rubigaragaza.

## Ibyo kugura

[Ibikoresho, ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi n'imiyoboro y'amazi](../../src/components/bar/docs-sections.tsx#BarMaterials)

Hasi, ibiti (battens) na lining bikurikiza urutonde rwa sitidiyo, rugabanyijwe ku ngano y'akabari ([ibikoresho bya sitidiyo](../studio/materials.md)).

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: imashini ya kawa, firigo, ameza (counters).
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe, igihe ibyo akabari kazagurisha bimaze kwemezwa.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/bar/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
