# Akabari: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Buri mubare uva muri bar.json [E]; umurongo ubona igiciro gusa aho sitidiyo igiha igice nk'icyo, igiciro cy'agateganyo cya AS-21 [A], indi igategereza ibiciro by'abacuruzi.

Ibyo akabari gakeneye, ku rutonde rumwe: ibikoresho byako, imbaho z'ibikoresho, ibyomekwa ku nkuta za kontineri, urubaho n'aho kuruhukira, amashanyarazi n'amazi.

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: foam y'ibyomekwa, imashini ya kawa, firigo, imbaho z'ibikoresho, deck n'intebe zayo.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Ha umurongo igiciro cyawo muri `src/components/bar/bom.ts`, cyangwa ugihe igice muri `scripts/studio/prices.py` iyo buri hantu hakigura (hanyuma ukoreshe `python3 scripts/site/prices.py`), kugira ngo uru rutonde n'urw'ifamu bikigaragaze.
