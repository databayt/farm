# Ubworozi: gahunda y'imirimo

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: gahunda yo ku wa 2026-10-31. Nta tungo rizazanwa mbere y'uko ibigenzurwa biri hepfo birangira; aho urugo rw'amatungo ruzubakwa hazemezwa nyuma yo gupima intera ya m 200 uvuye ku kiyaga. Ingano z'urugo ni izo igishushanyo giteganya muri livestock.json, kugeza igihe umuganga w'amatungo w'ifamu cyangwa umujyanama mu kubakira amatungo azazigenzura [A].

Uko watangira amatungo ya mbere y'ifamu: ihene 6 n'intama 4 mu rugo rumwe rw'umudugudu, hamwe n'amatungo y'inshuti abashyitsi bahura na yo, ibigenzurwa mbere y'uko itungo rizanwa, uko yitabwaho buri munsi n'amategeko.

## Mbere yo gutangira

1. **Iyandikishe nk'umworozi** ukurikije itegeko rishya rigenga amatungo, rikurikizwa kuva ryatangazwa ku wa 2026-09-11: aborozi bariyandikisha, umuganga w'amatungo wenyine ni we uvura amatungo, kandi buri rugendo rw'itungo rusaba icyemezo cyo kwimura amatungo (movement certificate) [V REG-24].
2. **Pima intera:** ikiraro kigomba kuba nibura kuri m 200 uvuye ku murongo wa kure cyane amazi y'ikiyaga yagezeho mu gihe cy'imyuzure (flood line), na m 20 uvuye ku gishanga icyo ari cyo cyose; utabyubahirije acibwa ihazabu ya RWF 500,000 kandi inzu igasenywa [V LOC-01, LOC-02].
3. **Hitamo umuganga w'amatungo:** umuganga w'amatungo w'umurenge cyangwa w'akarere, cyangwa umuganga w'amatungo wigenga ukorera kimwe mu bigo byahawe ububasha na Leta (mandated companies) [V LOC-48].
4. **Gura unyuze ku muganga w'amatungo:** buri tungo riza rifite icyemezo cyaryo cyo kwimurwa, rigategerereza mu kato (quarantine pen) kari nibura kuri m 30 mbere yo kwinjira mu yandi (D-030).

## Intambwe

1. **Banza uruzitiro n'amazi:** uruzitiro rw'urugo rw'inkingi n'imbaho n'irembo ryarwo, ikigega cy'amazi ku gitereko cyacyo n'imivure ibiri ([amazi](water.md)).
2. **Ikiraro:** urubuga ruzamuye, hanyuma inkingi n'imirunga y'ibiti bya eucalyptus bifite ibishishwa, imbaho zihagaze inyuma no ku mpera y'iburasirazuba, igisenge cy'ibyatsi gihanamye cyane, hanyuma ibyatsi byumye hasi ([ibikoresho](materials.md)).
3. **Urugo:** ibyatsi byumye hejuru y'ubutaka, amabuye, uruzitiro rw'inkingi ruzengurutse igiti gitanga igicucu, udusanduku tw'imyunyu (tw'ihene hejuru, tw'intama hasi).
4. **Ibyumba by'ifumbire:** ibyumba bitatu by'inkingi mu burasirazuba bw'ububiko bw'ibiryo, by'isaso n'amase.
5. **Inshuti n'imbwa:** akazu k'inuma ku nkingi yako, inzu y'injangwe ku giti cyayo, n'urugo rw'imbwa n'utuzu twazo tubiri.
6. **Amatungo:** ihene n'intama bivuye mu kato, ibigenzurwa byose bimaze kurangira.

### Kugurisha

1. Ihene n'intama bigurishwa ari bizima: kugurisha inyama bisaba uruhushya rwa RICA rwo kubaga amatungo (slaughter licence), aho kubagira handitswe (registered premise) n'imodoka itwara inyama yanditswe (registered carrier) [V REG-03, REG-06].
2. Inyama zigaburirwa mu gikoni gikuru ziva mu ibagiro rifite uruhushya.
3. Gahunda y'igihugu y'ubwishingizi bw'ubuhinzi n'ubworozi yishingira ibiguruka byororwa, ariko ntiyishingira ihene [V MKT-21]: amatungo ntafite ubwishingizi.

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Nta tungo mbere y'ibigenzurwa.** Umworozi yiyandikishije, intera zapimwe, umuganga w'amatungo yatoranyijwe, uko Umuriro wa Rift Valley uhagaze byemejwe mu nyandiko.
2. **Akato mbere.** Buri tungo rishya ritegerereza kuri m 30 mbere yo kwinjira mu yandi.
3. **Imyunyu ukwayo.** Imyunyu y'ihene imanikwa hejuru, aho intama zitayigeraho [V LVS-26].
4. **Ifumbire ishyushye.** Ifumbire igera kuri 55 °C mu minsi 3, ihindurwa, mbere yo gukora ku kintu cyose kiribwa.

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Umworozi yiyandikishije, intera za m 200 na m 20 zapimwe, kandi umuganga w'amatungo yatoranyijwe.
2. Uruzitiro, amazi, ikiraro n'urugo byubatswe, kandi imivure yuzura amazi.
3. Amatungo 10 yageze mu rugo, kandi buri tungo, buri muti watanzwe na buri kilo cy'ibiryo byose byandikwa.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Umurima n'urugo rw'amatungo biri ku ntera ingana iki uvuye ku murongo w'imyuzure y'ikiyaga (flood line)?
2. Ni nde uzita ku matungo buri munsi, kandi ni nde uzamusimbura ku munsi w'ikiruhuko cye?
3. Ese akarere kamaze gukuraho, mu nyandiko, ingamba zafashwe kubera Umuriro wa Rift Valley?
4. Inkoko: amabwiriza ya mbere yatangiraga n'inkoko hafi 100 zitera amagi y'igikoni gikuru; urugo nta nkoko rufite, nyamara ibyo igikoni gikuru giteganya bishingira ku magi y'inkoko z'ifamu. Ese inkoko zizororwa, kandi he?
5. Urwuri rwa hegitari 0.32 rutunga ihene z'ingore 2 kugeza kuri 4 gusa [E]: ese amatungo 10 azahabwa ubwatsi buhingwa bugatwarwa (cut-and-carry) cyangwa ubwatsi bwumye bugurwa (D-030)?
