# Ubworozi: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano ziva muri livestock.json; ibiciro bigaragara gusa aho isoko (source) ibitanga (ihene imwe hafi RWF 40,000 [LVS-01], intama imwe hafi RWF 100,000 [LVS-56]), ibindi ni iby'agateganyo cyangwa ntibirashakirwa aho biva [A].

Ibyo urugo rw'amatungo rukeneye: amatungo yarwo, ibikoresho byarwo, amazi yarwo n'uruziga rw'ifumbire, nk'uko urupapuro rwarwo rubigaragaza.

English: [read this list in English](materials.md) · Urugo mu bishushanyo: [urupapuro rw'amatungo](/en/livestock) · Igihe bikenewe: [gahunda y'imirimo](plan.md)

## Ibyo kugura

[Amatungo, urugo, amazi n'ifumbire](../../src/components/livestock/docs-sections.tsx#LivestockMaterials)

Inkingi, imbaho n'ibyatsi byo gusakara ikiraro biza ku mubare umwubatsi abara akurikije uko urupapuro rugaragaza imyubakire yacyo (build-up).

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: amatungo, ikigega cy'amazi, inkingi n'ibyatsi byo gusakara ikiraro.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe; amatungo agurwa unyuze ku muganga w'amatungo.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/livestock/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
