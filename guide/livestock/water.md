# Livestock: water

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The pipe's size, its depth and every route are design intent until the farm's plumber sets them; where the farm's line comes from waits for the farm's water plan and the borehole test [A].

How water reaches the animals: the farm's line to the tank, the buried line to the troughs, the bucket tap, and what the yard drinks a day.

The water drawn: [the livestock page](/en/livestock#water) · When it happens: [the execution plan](plan.md) · Where the water comes from: [the farm's water](../farm/water.md)

## At a glance

| What | Design |
|---|---|
| The tank | 1,000 L by the shed's east end on a fieldstone base, filled through a float valve on its top from the farm's water line (thatch does not collect rain cleanly) |
| The line | from a ball valve at the tank's foot, a 20 mm PE line buried 30 cm down past the shed's east end and along the front of the eaves |
| The troughs | two, under the eaves, each on a low-pressure float valve: the tank stands only about 1.2 m above them [E] |
| The tap | a tap on the riser fills the buckets for the dogs' and the cats' bowls and the doves' dish |

## Hold points

1. **Low-pressure floats.** The head from the tank is low, so only low-pressure float valves are fitted.
2. **The farm's line first.** The tank fills from the farm's water line, which waits for the farm's water plan and the borehole test.

## Steps

1. **Tank:** the fieldstone base, the tank on it, the farm's line in under the back fence to the float valve on its top.
2. **Line:** dig the trench 30 cm down; lay the 20 mm PE line from the ball valve past the shed to the troughs.
3. **Troughs:** a tee up to each trough's float valve; the tap on the riser.
4. **Test:** fill the tank, open the valve, watch both troughs fill and shut off.
