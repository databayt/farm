## Mbere yo gutangira

1. Shinga imambo zerekana 24 × 15 m ku bwatsi kandi urebe ko nta kintu kiri hejuru yabyo munsi ya m 7.
2. Reba ku kibanza aho ubwatsi buhanamiye: ikibuga gihanamira aho, bitarenze mm 5 kuri metero.
3. Umwubatsi arebe ubutaka mbere y'uko ingano y'ibirenge (footings) yemezwa.

## Intambwe

1. **Umuhanga mu bibuga (groundsman):** ringaniza aho bakinira: kata ahazamutse kandi wuzuze ahacukutse ubutaka bwo hejuru buvanze n'umucanga bwa hafi mm 20, bisiribanyijwe (rolled) ku buso bumwe.
2. **Umwubatsi:** shyiraho beto y'ibirenge bibiri n'imiyoboro yabyo (sleeves), hejuru habyo hangana n'ubwatsi ([urushundura n'amazi y'imvura](net.md)).
3. **Umuhanga mu bibuga:** tera imbuto ahatari ubwatsi kandi ukate ikibuga cyose bugufi mu nzira nyinshi, ubwatsi bukizengurutse busigara burebure; ureke gitekane ibyumweru bike mbere yo gukinirwaho.
4. **Umuhanga mu bibuga:** fatisha tape y'umweru ya cm 5 y'imirongo buri metero, no kuri buri nguni na buri hantu imirongo ihurira: imirongo y'impande n'iy'impera, umurongo wo hagati n'umurongo w'ibitero (attack line) uri m 3 kuri buri ruhande.
5. **Umwubatsi:** shyira inkingi mu miyoboro yazo, umanike urushundura kandi ushyireho antenne imwe hejuru ya buri murongo w'uruhande.
6. **Umubaji:** intebe ebyiri, imitaka yazo isakajwe ibyatsi n'ububiko bw'imipira ([ibikoresho](materials.md)).

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Buso bumwe mbere.** Nta kirenge gishyirwamo beto mbere y'uko aho bakinira haringanizwa kandi uko hahanamye hakarebwa.
2. **Nta gukina ku bwatsi butose.** Buranyerera: ikibuga gifungwa nyuma y'imvura.
3. **Gukina bihagarara izuba rirenze.** Mu ntangiriro nta matara manini y'ikibuga (floodlights) ahari.

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Ikibuga kiringaniye, gifite ubwatsi kandi gishushanyijeho imirongo nk'uko amategeko abiteganya, kandi urushundura ruhagaze ku burebure bukwiye hejuru y'umurongo wo hagati.
2. Imvura itemba inyura ku bwatsi nyuma y'imvura nyinshi ya mbere, kandi nta mazi asigara ku kibuga.
3. Intebe, imitaka n'ububiko bw'imipira byageze mu mwanya wabyo, hamwe n'imipira, pompe n'igipimo cy'umwuka (gauge).

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Amatara manini y'ikibuga nyuma: nibura lux 300 ku kibuga (itegeko 1.6), ku nkingi ziri hanze y'umwanya w'ubwisanzure, ingano yayo ibarwa muri [amashanyarazi y'ifamu](../farm/electric.md).
