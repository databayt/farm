# Volleyball: net and drainage

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The net, the posts and their heights follow the FIVB rules; the footings' size waits for a builder to see the soil [A].

Everything the builder needs for the net, from the footings to the antennae, and where the rain goes on a court that is a lawn.

Kinyarwanda: [soma iyi paji mu Kinyarwanda](net.rw.md) · The net drawn: [the volleyball page](/en/volleyball#net) · When it happens: [the execution plan](plan.md)

## At a glance

| What | Design |
|---|---|
| The net | 1 m deep and 9.50 to 10 m long, 10 cm square black mesh, a 7 cm white band along its top over the cable, a 5 cm band at its foot (rule 2.2) |
| Its height | 2.43 m for men and 2.24 m for women over the centre line, the same over both side lines (rule 2.1) |
| The posts | rounded and smooth, 2.55 m high, steel tube 90 mm across, padded to 1.8 m, 1.0 m outside the side lines, no guy wires (rule 2.5) |
| The winch | on the near post, 1.1 m up, tensioning the top cable |
| The antennae | flexible rods 1.80 m long and 10 mm thick, at the side bands' outer edges, the top 80 cm in red and white stripes (rule 2.4) |
| Drainage | none of its own: the lawn takes the rain where it falls, and the court falls 5 mm a metre at most, the way the lawn already falls |

## Hold points

1. **The footings flush.** The footings' tops sit flush with the grass, a cap over each sleeve when its post is out.
2. **The height checked.** The net's height is measured over the centre line and over both side lines before play.

## Steps

1. **Footings:** cast the two footings with their sleeves, outside the side lines at the posts' 1.0 m.
2. **Posts:** drop the posts into the sleeves; fit the pads and the winch.
3. **Net:** hang the net on its top cable, tension it at the winch, set its height for the game; fit the side bands and the antennae.
4. **Drainage:** after the first heavy rains, look for water lying on the court; if it lies, dig a gravel trench along the low side, the fallback.

## Open questions

1. How big must the footings be? A builder decides once the soil is seen.
2. Which way does the lawn fall? Along the court's 24 m the fall can reach 120 mm, across its 15 m up to 75 mm; it is read on site.
