# Volleyball: urushundura n'amazi y'imvura

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Urushundura, inkingi n'uburebure bwazo bikurikiza amategeko ya FIVB; ingano y'ibirenge (footings) itegereje ko umwubatsi abona ubutaka [A].

Ibyo umwubatsi akeneye byose ku rushundura, uhereye ku birenge kugeza kuri antenne, n'aho amazi y'imvura ajya ku kibuga kiri ku bwatsi.

## Mu ncamake

| Iki | Uko byateganyijwe |
|---|---|
| Urushundura | m 1 z'ubujyakuzimu na m 9.50 kugeza 10 z'uburebure, imyobo y'umukara ya cm 10 kuri 10 (square), umurongo w'umweru wa cm 7 hejuru yarwo utwikiriye insinga (cable), n'umurongo wa cm 5 hasi yarwo (itegeko 2.2) |
| Uburebure bwarwo | m 2.43 ku bagabo na m 2.24 ku bagore hejuru y'umurongo wo hagati, kimwe hejuru y'imirongo yombi y'impande (itegeko 2.1) |
| Inkingi | zihetamye kandi zoroshye, m 2.55 z'uburebure, itiyo y'icyuma ya mm 90 z'umurambararo, zipfutse (padded) kugeza kuri m 1.8, ziri m 1.0 hanze y'imirongo y'impande, nta nsinga zizifata (guy wires) (itegeko 2.5) |
| Winch | ku nkingi ya hafi, m 1.1 hejuru, ikurura insinga yo hejuru |
| Antenne | inkoni zihindagurika (flexible) za m 1.80 z'uburebure na mm 10 z'ubugari, ku nkombe zo hanze z'imirongo y'impande (side bands), cm 80 zo hejuru zifite imirongo itukura n'iy'umweru (itegeko 2.4) |
| Amazi y'imvura (drainage) | nta muyoboro wihariye: ubwatsi bwinjiza imvura aho igwa, kandi ikibuga gihanamye mm 5 kuri metero bitarenze, nk'uko ubwatsi busanzwe buhanamye |

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Ibirenge bingana n'ubwatsi.** Hejuru y'ibirenge hangana n'ubwatsi, buri muyoboro (sleeve) ugapfundikirwa iyo inkingi yawo itarimo.
2. **Uburebure bugenzurwa.** Uburebure bw'urushundura bupimwa hejuru y'umurongo wo hagati no hejuru y'imirongo yombi y'impande mbere yo gukina.

## Intambwe

1. **Ibirenge:** shyiraho beto y'ibirenge bibiri n'imiyoboro yabyo (sleeves), hanze y'imirongo y'impande kuri m 1.0 z'inkingi.
2. **Inkingi:** shyira inkingi mu miyoboro; shyiraho ibizipfuka (pads) na winch.
3. **Urushundura:** manika urushundura ku nsinga yarwo yo hejuru, urukurure kuri winch, ushyireho uburebure bwarwo bukwiye umukino; shyiraho imirongo y'impande (side bands) na antenne.
4. **Amazi y'imvura:** nyuma y'imvura nyinshi za mbere, reba niba hari amazi asigara ku kibuga; niba asigara, cukura umuferege wuzuyemo amabuye mato (gravel) ku ruhande rwo hasi, ari bwo buryo bw'ingoboka.
