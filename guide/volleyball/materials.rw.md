# Volleyball: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano ziva muri volleyball.json no mu mategeko ya FIVB; nta kintu cy'ikibuga kirashakirwa aho kiva cyangwa ngo gihabwe igiciro [A].

Ibyo ikibuga gikeneye: ibikoresho byacyo (kit), n'urushundura rwacyo hamwe n'inkingi n'ibirenge (footings), nk'uko urupapuro rwacyo rubigaragaza.

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: inkingi n'imiyoboro yazo (sleeves), urushundura na antenne zarwo, intebe n'imitaka.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/volleyball/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
