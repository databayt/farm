# Icyumba cy'abashyitsi: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31; buri cyumba gifite douche yacyo, WC na lavabo (D-061). Ingano ziva muri guest-room.json; ibiciro bigaragazwa ni ibiciro by'agateganyo bya sitidiyo (AS-21), kandi nta kintu kirashakwa [A].

Ibyo icyumba kimwe cy'abashyitsi gikeneye: ibikoresho byo mu nzu, ubwiherero bwacyo, ibiryamirwa n'amatara yo ku buriri, n'ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi, nk'uko urupapuro rwacyo rubigaragaza.

## Ibyo kugura

[Igitanda, icyumba na veranda, ubwiherero, n'ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi](../../src/components/guest-room/docs-sections.tsx#GuestRoomMaterials)

Hasi, ibiti (battens) na lining bikurikiza urutonde rwa sitidiyo, rugenwe ku ngano y'icyumba, kandi membrane y'ubwiherero, amakaro n'amatiyo bikurikiza ubwiherero bwa sitidiyo ([ibikoresho bya sitidiyo](../studio/materials.md)).

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: igitanda na matela yacyo, ibikoresho by'ubwiherero, akabati k'imyenda (wardrobe), ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare w'ibyumba bitatu.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/guest-room/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
