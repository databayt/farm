## Before you start

1. Decide whether each room gets its own shower and WC (D-029), or the guests use the public bathroom as drawn: an en-suite adds plumbing and a water-heater circuit to every room.
2. Measure the joined pair as the studio's survey does: the length, the depth, the openings and the posts at the joint.
3. Book the welder for whatever hot work the survey finds: frames, the joint's posts and its beam.

## Steps

1. **Welder:** all the hot work, before anything that can burn goes in.
2. **Carpenter:** the membrane and the floor sheets, then the battens, as in [the studio's plan](../studio/plan.md).
3. **Electrician:** the first fix for the 4 circuits, with the board by the bed ([electric](electric.md)).
4. **Electrician:** continuity and insulation tests on every cable, a photograph of every wall, the release for the insulation.
5. **Insulation and linings:** insulate between the battens and fix the linings, as the studio does [A]; the partitions between the rooms.
6. **Carpenter:** the oak planks, then the furniture ([materials](materials.md)): the bed, the bedside tables, the bench, the wardrobe, the desk and the hooks.
7. **Electrician:** the second fix (the board, the lights, the wall fan, the sockets, the AC isolator and the smoke alarm), then the tests and the certificate.
8. **Carpenter:** the veranda's boards, its steps and the slatted shade; its chairs and table.
9. **Owner:** the bedding, the lamps and the rug; photograph each room.

## Hold points

1. **Measure before you cut.** Nothing is cut or ordered before the block is measured.
2. **No welding after the insulation.** All hot work comes first.
3. **Nothing buried untested.** The cables pass their tests before the linings close.
4. **No power on before the certificate.** A RURA-licensed electrician signs the tests first.

## Done when

1. The electrical tests pass and the certificate is signed.
2. Each room has its bed with a lamp on both sides (D-029), its wardrobe and desk, and its veranda chairs.
3. Its guests have a shower and a WC: the public bathroom open, or each room's own.
