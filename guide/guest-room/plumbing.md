# Guest room: plumbing

Status: decided 2026-10-31 (D-061): every room has its own shower, WC and basin. Every run and size is design intent for the plumber to confirm [A]; where the pipes go is drawn once the block is measured.

Everything the plumber needs for the three guest rooms' bathrooms: the supply, the hot water, the drains and the vent, the tests, and what the rooms need from the farm.

The three rooms are piped the same, so one page serves all three.

The bathroom drawn: [the guest room page](/en/guest-room) · When it happens: [the execution plan](plan.md) · The method in full: [the studio's plumbing](../studio/plumbing.md)

## At a glance

| What | Design |
|---|---|
| Bathroom | across each room's front-left corner, behind a sliding door: a walk-in shower on a 900 × 900 tray with a fixed glass screen, a floor-standing WC with a low cistern under the window, a basin on a timber console |
| Supply | the farm's storage tank and booster pump at 2.5 to 3 bar, as the studio's ([the farm's water](../farm/water.md)); a stop valve for each room |
| Pipe | PP-R PN20: 25 mm for the main along the block, 20 mm into each bathroom, in the linings |
| Hot water | an instant heater, 5.5 kW, IPX4, on the shower's side wall, for the shower only, as the studio's (D-053); the basin is cold |
| Drains | WC 110 mm, shower 50 mm, basin 40 mm, all at 1:40 or steeper: down through the floor between the container's cross-members, out under the veranda to an inspection chamber in front of each room, then to the farm's septic tank |
| Vent | a 75 mm open vent from each bathroom, ending above the roof |

## Hold points

1. **The rooms drawn first.** No hole goes through the floor before the three bathrooms are drawn on the measured block.
2. **The 10 bar test.** Every pipe holds 10 bar for one hour before the linings close.
3. **The septic sized for every user.** It counts the studios, these three bathrooms, the public bathroom and the galley before it is bought ([the farm's water](../farm/water.md)).
4. **No guests before the inspection.** The septic system passes its inspection first (D-016).

## Steps

1. **First fix:** the main from the farm's tank along the block; the supply in the linings to each bathroom; the drains through the floor between the cross-members and out under the veranda to each inspection chamber; the vents.
2. **Tests before the linings:** 10 bar for one hour; photograph every wall; sign the release.
3. **Second fix:** the tray and its screen, the WC, the basin and its tap, then the heater once the electrician has fitted its isolator, as the studio's (S09, S10).
4. **Commissioning:** fill-test every drain to its chamber; run the shower at full flow to about 40 °C.
