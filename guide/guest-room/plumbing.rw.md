# Icyumba cy'abashyitsi: imiyoboro y'amazi

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: byemejwe ku wa 2026-10-31 (D-061): buri cyumba gifite douche yacyo, WC na lavabo. Buri nzira y'amatiyo na buri ngano ni icyo igishushanyo giteganya (design intent), umufundi w'amazi agomba kwemeza [A]; aho amatiyo anyura hazashushanywa inyubako imaze gupimwa.

Ibyo umufundi w'amazi (plumber) akeneye byose ku bwiherero bw'ibyumba bitatu by'abashyitsi: aho amazi aturuka (supply), amazi ashyushye, imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye n'umuyoboro w'umwuka (vent), igerageza, n'ibyo ibyumba bikeneye ku ifamu.

Amatiyo y'ibyumba bitatu ashyirwa kimwe, bityo iyi paji ikora kuri byose uko ari bitatu.

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Ibyumba bibanza gushushanywa.** Nta mwobo ucibwa hasi mbere y'uko ubwiherero butatu bushushanywa ku nyubako yapimwe.
2. **Igerageza rya bar 10.** Buri tiyo igumana bar 10 mu isaha imwe mbere y'uko lining zifungwa.
3. **Septic itegurwa kuri buri muyikoresha.** Ibara sitidiyo, ubu bwiherero butatu, ubwiherero rusange n'igikoni gikuru (galley) mbere y'uko igurwa ([amazi y'ifamu](../farm/water.md)).
4. **Nta bashyitsi mbere y'igenzura.** Septic system ibanza gutsinda igenzura ryayo (D-016).

## Intambwe

1. **First fix:** umuyoboro mukuru uva ku kigega cy'ifamu unyura ku nyubako; amazi anyura muri lining ajya kuri buri bwiherero; imiyoboro y'amazi yanduye inyura hasi hagati ya cross-members igasohokera munsi ya veranda ijya kuri buri inspection chamber; vents.
2. **Igerageza mbere ya lining:** bar 10 mu isaha imwe; fotora buri rukuta; sinya uruhushya (release).
3. **Second fix:** tray n'ikirahure cyayo, WC, lavabo na robine yayo, hanyuma heater umuyagankuba amaze gushyiraho isolator yayo, nk'iya sitidiyo (S09, S10).
4. **Gutangiza (commissioning):** gerageza buri muyoboro w'amazi yanduye uwuzuza amazi kugeza ku cyobo cyawo; fungura douche yose kugeza amazi ageze hafi kuri 40 °C.

## Inzira z'amatiyo (runs)

Zizashushanywa inyubako imaze gupimwa kandi ubwiherero butatu bwashyizwe ku gishushanyo cyayo.

## Points

Zizashushanywa hamwe n'inzira z'amatiyo.
