# Galley: plumbing

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. Every run and size is a design choice for the plumber to check; nothing is sourced or priced [A].

Everything the plumber needs for the galley: the supply, the hot water, the drains to the grease trap, the tests and every point.

Kinyarwanda: [soma aya mabwiriza mu Kinyarwanda](plumbing.rw.md) · The pipes drawn: [the galley page](/en/galley#plumbing) · When it happens: [the execution plan](plan.md) · The method in full: [the studio's plumbing](../studio/plumbing.md)

## At a glance

| What | Design |
|---|---|
| Supply | one cold supply from the farm's tank and booster pump at 2.5 to 3 bar ([the farm's water](../farm/water.md)), in under the staff door's end |
| Main in | a stop valve, a Y-filter and a check meter by the hand-wash basin, then along the front wall under the worktop to every outlet |
| Hot water | a 100 L electric storage heater on the east end wall above the bins; the hot runs back along the same wall to the pre-rinse spray, both sinks and the basin |
| Drains | every drain at 1:40 or steeper to one 110 mm collector under the plinth along the front, out at the east end to the kitchen's grease trap and its own soak pit |
| Dishwasher | none drawn: a 50 mm waste and a cold tee are left capped under the dish drop for one |

## Hold points

1. **Battens first.** Start the first fix only when the battens are fixed and the floor sheets are down.
2. **The 10 bar test.** Every pipe holds 10 bar for an hour, written down and signed, before the linings close.
3. **The grease trap first.** No drain is connected before the grease trap and its soak pit are in (D-055).

## Steps

1. **First fix:** the cold main in under the staff door's end through the stop valve, filter and meter; the cold along the front wall to every outlet and to the heater; the hot back along the same wall; the wastes down through the floor to the collector.
2. **Tests before the linings:** 10 bar for an hour; photograph every wall; sign the release.
3. **Second fix:** the heater, the sink mixers and the pre-rinse spray, the hands-free basin tap, the drinking-water filter, the air admittance valve and the floor gully.
4. **Commissioning:** fill-test every drain to the grease trap; run the hot at every tap.
