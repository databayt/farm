Hasi, ibiti (battens) na lining bikurikiza urutonde rwa sitidiyo, rwongerewe ku ngano y'agasanduku ka 40 ft ([ibikoresho bya sitidiyo](../studio/materials.md)).

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: cooking line na hood yayo, ububiko bukonje (cold store), worktop ya stainless.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe, igihe gaz cyangwa amashanyarazi bimaze kwemezwa.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/galley/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
