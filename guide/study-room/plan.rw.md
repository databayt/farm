## Mbere yo gutangira

1. Pima kontineri nk'uko isuzuma rya sitidiyo ribikora: uburebure, ubugari n'aho ifunguka hari ibirahure ku rukuta rw'amajyaruguru.
2. Teganya umusuderi ku mirimo yo gusudira isuzuma rizagaragaza: ikadiri y'aho ifunguka hari ibirahure n'umutwe w'amalouvres.
3. Network y'ibiro igomba kubanza gukora: access point y'icyumba ihabwa umuriro unyuze mu nsinga yayo uvuye kuri PoE switch y'ibiro.

## Intambwe

1. **Umusuderi:** imirimo yose yo gusudira, mbere y'uko hashyirwamo ikintu cyose gishobora gushya.
2. **Umubaji:** membrane n'impapuro za hasi, hanyuma ibiti (battens), nk'uko biri muri [gahunda ya sitidiyo](../studio/plan.md).
3. **Umuyagankuba (electrician):** first fix y'imizunguruko 4, priza ebyiri (double socket) inyuma ya buri ntebe ebyiri, priza n'aho data icomekwa ku rukuta rwa screen, n'agasanduku insinga yo hanze yinjiriramo ([amashanyarazi](electric.md)).
4. **Umuyagankuba:** igerageza rya continuity n'insulation kuri buri nsinga, ifoto ya buri rukuta, n'uruhushya rwo gushyiramo insulation (release).
5. **Insulation na lining:** insulation hagati y'ibiti na pulayiwudi ya mm 12, isize ibara ry'umweru wijimye (off-white), nk'uko sitidiyo ibikora [A].
6. **Umubaji:** imbaho z'igiti (oak planks), hanyuma ibikoresho byo mu nzu ([ibikoresho](materials.md)): ameza maremare n'intebe zayo, akabati ka screen, lectern, urukuta rw'amasahani y'ibitabo, inguni yo gusomeramo.
7. **Umuyagankuba:** second fix (board, amatara amanitse (pendants), fan ebyiri zo ku rukuta, priza na access point), hanyuma igerageza n'icyemezo (certificate).
8. **Umubaji:** imbaho za deck, intambwe yayo n'amalouvres y'imbaho.
9. **Nyir'ifamu:** screen ku cyuma cyayo (bracket), itapi n'ibimera; inama ya mbere yo kugerageza Wi-Fi na buri mudasobwa igendanwa (laptop).

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Pima mbere yo gukata.** Nta kintu gikatwa cyangwa gitumizwa mbere y'uko kontineri ipimwa.
2. **Nta gusudira nyuma ya insulation.** Imirimo yose yo gusudira ikorwa mbere.
3. **Nta kintu gihishwa kitageragejwe.** Insinga zitsinda igerageza ryazo mbere y'uko lining zifunga.
4. **Nta muriro mbere y'icyemezo.** Umuyagankuba ufite uruhushya rwa RURA abanza gusinya igerageza.

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Igerageza ry'amashanyarazi ryatsinze kandi icyemezo (certificate) cyasinywe.
2. Buri ntebe muri umunani ifite priza yayo, kandi Wi-Fi itwara laptops na screen icyarimwe.
3. Ameza, screen, amasahani n'inguni yo gusomeramo byageze mu mwanya wabyo.
