## Before you start

1. Measure the container as the studio's survey does: the length, the width and the glazed opening along the north wall.
2. Book the welder for the hot work the survey finds: the frame of the glazed opening and the louvres' head.
3. Have the office's network running first: the room's access point is powered over its cable from the office's PoE switch.

## Steps

1. **Welder:** all the hot work, before anything that can burn goes in.
2. **Carpenter:** the membrane and the floor sheets, then the battens, as in [the studio's plan](../studio/plan.md).
3. **Electrician:** the first fix for the 4 circuits, a double socket behind each pair of seats, the screen wall's socket and data outlet, and the entry box for the outdoor cable ([electric](electric.md)).
4. **Electrician:** continuity and insulation tests on every cable, a photograph of every wall, the release for the insulation.
5. **Insulation and linings:** insulation between the battens and 12 mm ply, painted off-white, as the studio does [A].
6. **Carpenter:** the oak planks, then the furniture ([materials](materials.md)): the long table and its chairs, the screen cabinet, the lectern, the bookshelf wall, the reading corner.
7. **Electrician:** the second fix (the board, the pendants, the two wall fans, the sockets and the access point), then the tests and the certificate.
8. **Carpenter:** the deck's boards, its step and the timber louvres.
9. **Owner:** the screen on its bracket, the rug and the plants; a first meeting to test the Wi-Fi with every laptop.

## Hold points

1. **Measure before you cut.** Nothing is cut or ordered before the container is measured.
2. **No welding after the insulation.** All hot work comes first.
3. **Nothing buried untested.** The cables pass their tests before the linings close.
4. **No power on before the certificate.** A RURA-licensed electrician signs the tests first.

## Done when

1. The electrical tests pass and the certificate is signed.
2. Each of the eight seats has its socket, and the Wi-Fi carries the laptops and the screen together.
3. The table, the screen, the shelves and the reading corner are in.

## Open questions

1. The screen is 86 inches on the page, but the design load of its socket in study-room.json is for a 65-inch one: which is it?
2. Do two wall fans and the open glazing keep a full room cool with no air conditioning?
