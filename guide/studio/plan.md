## Before you start

![Day 1: measure the 7 numbers and write them on the sheet](../../public/designs/studio/guide/measure.en.svg)

1. Measure both studio containers; mark them U1 and U2 on the doors with chalk.
2. Write the 7 numbers on the [survey sheet](../../study/studio/studio-site.md) and send a photo of it the same day.
3. Book the welder: all the hot work comes first, once the supports are level (S01).
4. Have the farm's shared works ready or under way: the septic tank, the storage tank and its booster, the site board and its feeder ([the farm's water](../farm/water.md), [the farm's electric](../farm/electric.md)).
5. Count every bundle for the studio on site before the first step.

## Steps

### Floor: membrane and sheets (S02)

![The floor: 5 sheets, the cut ones in red](../../public/designs/studio/guide/floor.en.svg)

1. Finish all the welding first (S01): no welding once the foam is in.
2. Lay the membrane over the old floor, laps 150 mm. Never sand the old floor.
3. Lay the sheets as drawn and screw them down: screws 4 × 30 every 300 mm.

### Battens (S03)

![Back wall: the batten centres, measured from the steel of end A](../../public/designs/studio/guide/battens-back.en.svg)

![End A, the front wall and the battens over the sliding door](../../public/designs/studio/guide/battens-end-front.en.svg)

![The ceiling: the batten centres, measured from the steel of end A](../../public/designs/studio/guide/battens-ceiling.en.svg)

1. Battens 38 × 50, standing on the floor sheets.
2. Mark each centre from the steel, at the numbers drawn.
3. Winged screws 5.5 × 50 at both ends and every 600 mm.

### Pipes and cables, then foam (S04, S05)

1. The electrician and the plumber put in their cables and pipes (S04): [electric](electric.md), [plumbing](plumbing.md).
2. The pipes are tested at 10 bar: wait for the signed paper.
3. The foam goes in between the battens; wait 24 hours, then cut it flush.

### Lining panels (S05)

![The 15 lining panels with their sizes](../../public/designs/studio/guide/panels.en.svg)

1. The ceiling first, then the walls.
2. Every panel edge sits on a batten centre.
3. Screws 4 × 30: every 200 mm on the edges, every 300 mm in the middle.

### Bathroom (S06 to S10)

![The bathroom from above, with every size](../../public/designs/studio/guide/bathroom.en.svg)

### Kitchen (S11 to S14)

![The kitchen, seen from the living room](../../public/designs/studio/guide/kitchen.en.svg)

1. Lay the oak planks first, from end A to the partition.
2. Then the three bases, the worktop, the two wall cabinets either side of the hob, and the chimney hood between them, 650 mm over the hob, its chimney cover up to the ceiling.

### Bed, seating and trims (S15 to S18)

- The bed, the bedside table, the table and chairs and the trims come from the workshop as numbered kits: build each one from its kit sheet.

### Second fix, roof and stair (S19 to S27)

1. The electrician's second fix and tests (S19): [electric](electric.md).
2. Bolt the deck beams onto the corner castings, then screw down the deck boards with 5 mm gaps (S20, S21).
3. Bolt the guardrail posts, fix the hardwood top rail and tension the stainless cables (S22, S23).
4. Set the veranda posts and the stair footings; bolt the two flights and the landing (S24, S25).
5. Place the chairs and the side table, then bond the steelwork to earth (S26, S27).

## Hold points

1. The measurements are sent → revision B → the workshop cuts.
2. All the welding is done → then the foam.
3. The pipe test has passed → then the foam.
4. The foam has cured for 24 hours → then the panels.
5. The bathroom membrane is checked → then the tiles.
6. A structural engineer has inspected the roof → then guests use the terrace (S27).

## Done when

1. The electrical tests pass and the certificate is signed (S19); the pipes held 10 bar before the foam, the drains are fill-tested, and the shower holds about 40 °C at full flow.
2. The doors and the drawers swing freely; the bathroom's sliding door runs to its stop and closes softly; the slider locks; the insect mesh is tight.
3. The farm's septic system has passed its inspection: no guest stays before it (D-016).
4. Each studio is cleaned and photographed, and its measurements are kept with its drawing set.
