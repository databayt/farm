# Ubuhinzi: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: ibiciro by'agateganyo byo ku wa 2026-10-31: buri giciro ni ikigereranyo cy'agateganyo kugeza habonetse ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo [A]. Ikigega cy'amazi (tank), pompe n'isoko by'umurima ni iby'ifamu ([amazi y'ifamu](../farm/water.md)).

Ibyo uturima {{farming.bedsTotal}} twa mbere dukeneye byose ngo dutangire: ubutaka n'uturima, amazi, uruzitiro, pepiniyeri (nursery), imbuto n'ingemwe, ibikoresho n'abakozi.

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: amatiyo y'ibitonyanga (drip) n'itiyo yayo, uruzitiro, ifumbire y'imborera (compost) n'imbuto.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare w'uturima {{farming.bedsTotal}}.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Hindura igiciro cy'umurongo muri `src/components/farming/farming.json` wongere ukoreshe generator y'umurima (`scripts/site/farming.mjs`), kugira ngo urutonde n'urupapuro bihinduke.
