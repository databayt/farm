## Ibiti: ishyamba riringaniye

1. **Ishyamba:** igiti kinini kimwe (canopy tree) buri m 12 ku murongo usimburana (staggered grid), buri kimwe kigasunikwa ku buryo butunguranye kugeza kuri kimwe cya gatatu cy'intera, kugira ngo urebeye kure kigaragare kiringaniye kandi urebeye hafi kigaragare nk'icy'umwimerere: ibiti {{site.trees_woodland}}.
2. **Hirya no hino y'urugo rw'inyubako:** uruziga rudakomeye buri m 14, m 6 uvuye kuri kontineri, kugira ngo bitange igicucu amababi atagwa ku bisenge: ibiti {{site.trees_compound}}.
3. **Mu murima:** ibiti bike binini bitanga igicucu ku mfuruka z'urwuri no ku nkengero z'uturima, ntibijye na rimwe hejuru y'uturima: ibiti {{site.trees_field}}.
4. **Ku mbibi:** umurongo buri m 8, m 2 imbere y'umurongo w'imbibi: ibiti {{site.trees_boundary}}.
5. **Intera:** buri giti kiguma kuri m 4 uvuye kuri kontineri, uturima, inzu, ikibuga, septic, solar, inzira n'imihanda.
6. **Amoko:** ibiti byo ku mbibi no mu murima bikurikiza [gahunda y'ibihingwa](../../study/design/crop-plan.md) y'inyigo: Grevillea na Markhamia, Gliricidia nk'uruzitiro rubisi (live fence), umwembe nk'igiti cy'imbuto aho amazi atarizerwa, kandi nta eucalyptus hafi y'ibihingwa cyangwa y'ikiyaga. Amoko y'ibiti binini by'ishyamba azemezwa n'ushinzwe amashyamba mu karere cyangwa pepiniyeri ya RAB [A].

Muri rusange, ibiti {{site.trees}} ku gishushanyo cy'agateganyo.

## Inzira na serivisi

- **Umuhanda wo kugera ku ifamu,** ubugari bwa m 4, kuva ku irembo kugeza ku rugo rw'inyubako; aho irembo riri biracyagomba kwemezwa.
- **Inzira y'ubusitani,** m 2, kuva ku turima kugeza ku gikoni gikuru.
- **Inzira y'uruziga yo mu ishyamba,** m 2, izenguruka ikibanza cy'ibirori.
- **Amazi:** ikigega cy'amazi na pompe iruhande rw'urugo rw'inyubako.
- **Imyanda:** septic tank n'urwobo rwinjiza amazi mu butaka (soakaway) nibura kuri m 50 uvuye kuri borehole kandi munsi yayo ku musozi.
- **Umuriro:** imirasire y'izuba (solar array) ni amahitamo ku byatsi; amabwiriza y'umuriro (power guide) ni yo azabyemeza.

## Ibikurikiraho

1. Bona pin cyangwa UPI, wongere ushushanye imbibi, kandi wemeze amajyaruguru n'irembo.
2. Pima intera iri hagati y'ukuboko (arm) n'ikiyaga, hanyuma wemeze aho inzu z'inkoko n'ihene zizubakwa.
3. Shinga imambo (pegs) z'ibice ku butaka kandi ubizenguruke n'amaguru hamwe na nyir'ifamu.
4. Nyuma y'ipima, shyira ibimenyetso by'ibiti hakurikijwe urutonde rw'aho buri giti kiri.
