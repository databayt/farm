# Ifamu: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: ibiciro by'agateganyo byo ku wa 2026-10-31: buri giciro ni ikigereranyo cy'agateganyo kugeza habonetse ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo [A].

Ibyo imirimo ifamu isangiye ikeneye: septic system, ububiko bw'amazi na booster. Sisitemu y'umuriro izatoranywa hakurikijwe ibiciro biri ku [mashanyarazi y'ifamu](electric.md).

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: septic tank, ikigega cy'amazi na pompe yongera umuvuduko (booster pump), sisitemu y'imirasire y'izuba.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Ongera wubake icyo umurongo ubarirwamo (`pnpm studio:build` ku mirimo isangiwe), kugira ngo ibiciro byose byawo bihinduke.
