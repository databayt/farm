# Ifamu: gaze

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: inama yo ku wa 2026-10-31. Galley (igikoni) itekesha gaze LPG (D-065); gaze ikoresha ni igereranya [E] kugeza igihe menu n'ifuru (range) bizemezwa, kandi ibiciro ni uko byatangajwe ku matariki yavuzwe.

Niba galley ikwiye guteka kuri gaze yo mu macupa (LPG), ingano yayo yakoresha n'icyo itwara, n'uko amacupa abikwa kandi agahuzwa mu mutekano, ku bw'nyir'ifamu n'uwushyiraho (installer).

Inama mu murongo umwe: muri galley mutekeshe LPG yo mu macupa abiri manini abitswe hanze mu kazu k'icyuma (cage) gafite umwuka, ashyirwaho n'umutekinisiye ufite uruhushya rwa RURA; amashanyarazi asigare ku byuma bikonjesha (fridges), ku cyuma gisohora umwotsi (extraction) no ku matara.

## Mu ncamake

### Ibyo galley yakoresha

Uburyo: guteka kwa galley ku mashanyarazi ni nka 17 kWh ku munsi (25 kWh ku munsi byose ku mashanyarazi, nka 8 kuri gaze, [igitabo cy'amashanyarazi](electric.md)). Ubushyuhe bugera ku nkono ni bumwe ku bicanwa byombi, bityo gaze ni ubwo bushyuhe bugabanyijwe n'ubushobozi (efficiency) bw'iziko rya gaze n'ingufu ziri mu kilo kimwe cya LPG [E, inputs in AS-30].

| Iki | Agaciro | Ikimenyetso |
|---|---|---|
| Guteka ku mashanyarazi isimbura | nka 17 kWh ku munsi | [E] bivuye mu bikoresho by'igitabo cy'amashanyarazi |
| Ubushyuhe bugera ku nkono | nka 12.8 kWh ku munsi (17 × 75% ku ifuru y'amashanyarazi) | [A, AS-30] |
| LPG ikoreshwa | nka 2.2 kg ku munsi (12.8 ÷ 45% efficiency y'iziko ÷ 13.1 kWh/kg), nka 65 kg ku kwezi | [E, AS-30] |
| Amacupa | nka icupa rimwe rya 38 kg buri minsi 17, cyangwa amacupa atanu cyangwa atandatu ya 12 kg ku kwezi | [E] |

Inakura kandi imirongo y'amashanyarazi ya three-phase yo guteka ya galley ku kibaho (board) na nka 17 kWh ku munsi ku byo ifamu ikoresha, bityo sisitemu y'izuba ishobora kugabanuka kuva kuri nka 10 ikagera kuri nka 8 kWp ([igitabo cy'amashanyarazi](electric.md)), kandi galley igakomeza guteka iyo umuyoboro wabuze.

## Aho guhagarara ngo bisuzumwe (hold points)

1. **Amacupa hanze.** Nta cupa, ryuzuye cyangwa ryarangiye, rihagarara imbere muri galley; ahagarara ahagaze mu kazu (cage) gafite umwuka hanze y'urukuta rwayo, kandi gaze ikinjira inyuze mu muyoboro ufashe [V ENG-30].
2. **Umutekinisiye ufite uruhushya.** Umuyoboro, regulator na change-over valve bishyirwaho n'umutekinisiye ufite uruhushya rwa RURA rwa reticulation, kandi bigeragezwa ku gitutu (pressure test) mbere y'ifunguro rya mbere [V ENG-30].
3. **Gufunga no kumenya ko gaze iri gusohoka.** Valve yo gufunga aho umuyoboro winjirira, imwe kuri buri gikoresho, icyuma kimenya gaze (gas detector) hasi (LPG iremereye kurusha umwuka), n'ikiringiti cy'umuriro (fire blanket) na kizimyamwoto (extinguisher) hafi y'umuryango.
4. **Guteka munsi y'icyuma gisohora umwotsi.** Buri ziko rikorera munsi ya hood igitabo cy'amashanyarazi cya galley gisanzwe giha amashanyarazi.

## Intambwe

1. Mwemeze ibicanwa bya galley (gaze cyangwa amashanyarazi) hamwe na chef na menu, mubyandike mu gitabo cy'ibyemezo (decision log): bihindura imirongo y'amashanyarazi ya galley ([amashanyarazi ya galley](../galley/electric.md)) n'ingano y'izuba.
2. Mubaze abacuruzi babiri ba LPG bafite uruhushya bageza i Bugesera igiciro cyabo ku kilo ku macupa ya 38 cyangwa 50 kg, bagejejwe aho, n'ingwate (deposit) kuri buri cupa.
3. Musabe umutekinisiye wa reticulation ufite uruhushya rwa RURA igiciro cy'akazu, umuyoboro, regulator ihinduranya ubwayo (automatic change-over) ku macupa abiri, valve n'icyuma kimenya gaze.
4. Mwubake akazu ku rukuta rw'inyuma rwa galley: fondasiyo ya beto ku butaka, impande z'urushundura (mesh), igisenge kirinda izuba n'imvura, ingufuri, kandi nibura m 3 uvuye ku miyoboro y'amazi yanduye n'imyobo iri munsi y'ubutaka, nk'uko RURA ibisaba amaduka [V ENG-30].
5. Muhitemo ibikoresho bikora kuri gaze (ifuru, griddle, fryer) igihe ibikoresho bya galley bigurwa.
6. Mwigishe abakozi intambwe za RURA iyo gaze isohoka: gufunga valve y'icupa, gufungura inzugi n'amadirishya, kudakora ku cyuma gicana (switch) cyangwa telefoni, no guhamagara abazimya inkongi iyo yafashe [V ENG-30].
7. Mwandike buri cupa ryinjiye: itariki, ingano n'igiciro. Nyuma y'ukwezi kwa mbere, mugereranye 2.2 kg ku munsi n'ibyanditswe (AS-30).
