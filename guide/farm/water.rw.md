Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31, gikomoka ku bishushanyo by'imiyoboro y'amazi ya sitidiyo; nta kintu kirapimwa. Amazi borehole itanga n'ubwiza bwayo, umuvuduko w'umuyoboro rusange (mains) n'aho septic izajya ntibirazwi [U].

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Umwanya w'umwuka (air gap).** Amazi y'umuyoboro rusange yinjira mu kigega anyuze mu mwanya w'umwuka (float valve iri hejuru y'aho amazi arenga asohokera, overflow), kugira ngo amazi y'ifamu atazigera asubira mu muyoboro rusange (D-016).
2. **Percolation test ibanza.** Aho septic tank na soak pit yayo bizajya hemezwa gusa nyuma ya percolation test: soak pit ishyirwa gusa aho amazi yo mu butaka ari munsi ya m 2.5 kandi ubutaka bwinjiza mm 25 kugeza 125 mu isaha [V STU-10].
3. **Nta bashyitsi mbere y'igenzura.** Nta mushyitsi ucumbika mbere y'uko septic system itsinda igenzura ryayo (D-016).

## Intambwe

1. **Gerageza borehole:** pima amazi itanga mu gihe cy'amasaha 6, kandi usuzumishe urugero rw'amazi yayo ([gahunda y'umurima](../farming/plan.md)).
2. **Hitamo aho septic tank izajya:** nibura kuri m 50 uvuye kuri borehole no ku kiyaga, m 3 kugeza 7 uvuye ku nyubako no ku mbibi, munsi ya borehole ku musozi, kandi muri m 30 uvuye aho imodoka ividura (tanker) ishobora kugera.
3. **Yitegure buri muyikoresha:** ikigega cya {{studio.septic_m3}} m³ ni icya sitidiyo zombi; ongeraho ibyumba by'abashyitsi, ubwiherero rusange n'igikoni gikuru (galley) mbere y'uko kigurwa (D-055).
4. **Baza akarere ibijyanye na soak pits:** Ikigo gishinzwe umutungo w'amazi (Water Resources Board) cyasabye ko zivanwaho buhoro buhoro (2026, ingano yabyo ntisobanutse) [V STU-57]; igisubizo cy'ingoboka ni ikigega gifunze kividurwa ku gihe giteganyijwe.
5. **Shyiraho ikigega cy'amazi na booster pump,** umuyoboro rusange winjira unyuze mu mwanya w'umwuka (air gap).
6. **Shyiraho amatiyo ashyinguye y'amazi meza n'ay'amazi yanduye** ajya kuri buri hantu uko hubakwa; igikoni gikuru n'akabari bisohora amazi yanduye binyuze muri grease traps zabyo bwite (D-055).
7. **Teganya igenzura rya septic** mbere y'umushyitsi wa mbere.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Borehole itanga amazi angana iki kuva muri Kamena kugeza muri Kanama, kandi ayo mazi akwiriye kunyobwa no kuhira?
2. Septic tank izajya he, kandi igomba kungana iki iyo ibyumba by'abashyitsi, ubwiherero rusange n'igikoni gikuru bibariwemo?
3. Akarere kazemera soak pit, cyangwa ikigega kigomba gufungwa kikajya kividurwa ku gihe giteganyijwe?
4. Umurima uzakoresha amazi ya borehole cyangwa ay'umuyoboro rusange?
