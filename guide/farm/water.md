## Hold points

1. **The air gap.** Mains water enters the storage tank through an air gap (a float valve above the overflow), so private water never flows back into the main (D-016).
2. **The percolation test first.** The septic tank and its soak pit are sited only after a percolation test: a soak pit only where the groundwater is more than 2.5 m down and the soil takes 25 to 125 mm an hour [V STU-10].
3. **No guests before the inspection.** No guest stays before the septic system passes its inspection (D-016).

## Steps

1. **Test the borehole:** measure what it gives over 6 hours, and have a sample tested ([the field's plan](../farming/plan.md)).
2. **Site the septic tank:** at least 50 m from the borehole and the lake, 3 to 7 m from buildings and the boundary, downhill of the borehole, within 30 m of tanker access.
3. **Size it for every user:** the {{studio.septic_m3}} m³ tank is for the two studios; add the guest rooms, the public bathroom and the galley before it is bought (D-055).
4. **Ask the district about soak pits:** the Water Resources Board has called for them to be phased out (2026, scope unclear) [V STU-57]; the fallback is a sealed tank emptied on a schedule.
5. **Set the storage tank and the booster pump,** with the mains coming in through the air gap.
6. **Lay the buried supply and drain runs** to each place as it is built; the galley and the bar drain through grease traps of their own (D-055).
7. **Book the septic inspection** before the first guest.

## Open questions
