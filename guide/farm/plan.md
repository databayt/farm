## Before you start

1. **The location:** a Google Maps pin of the gate, or the UPI on the land title (gate G0).
2. **The title:** freehold or lease, the holder, the dates, and the land use stated on it.
3. **The lake:** how far the farm is from the flood line, and whether it touches a swamp.

## Steps

1. **Survey the land:** redraw the boundary from the UPI, fix north and the gate, and peg the zones with the owner ([the master plan](master-plan.md)).
2. **Test the soil and the water:** the soil samples to RAB's laboratory and the borehole's yield and quality ([water](water.md)).
3. **Ask for the power:** a written EUCL connection quote and three quotes for the solar system ([electric](electric.md)).
4. **Build the water:** the storage tank, the booster pump and the mains through the air gap ([water](water.md)).
5. **Build the septic:** the percolation test, the site, the size for every user, then the tank ([water](water.md)).
6. **Run the feeders and the pipes** to each place as it is built: the studios and the field first, then the rest in an order still to decide.

## Hold points

1. **Measure before you cut.** The workshop cuts nothing before the measurements come back.
2. **No welding after the foam.** All hot work is done first.
3. **Nothing gets buried untested.** The cables pass their tests and the pipes hold 10 bar for one hour before anything closes over them.
4. **No power on before the certificate.** A licensed electrician signs the tests first.
5. **No animals yet.** First the keeper's registration, a vet, the 200 m from the lake, the fence and the water ([livestock](../livestock/plan.md)).

## Done when

1. The location and the title are confirmed and the land is surveyed.
2. The farm has its connection quote and a chosen power system.
3. The borehole is tested, and the storage tank and the booster supply every place built so far.
4. The septic system has passed its inspection.
