# The farm: waste

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. Nothing is weighed yet, and the collector who would serve Gashora is unknown [U].

What the farm throws away, where each kind goes, and the rules it must keep, for the owner and the staff.

Recommendation, in one line: sort everything where it is made, feed what rots back to the hens and the compost, buy no single-use plastic, and hand the rest to licensed collectors; burn and bury nothing.

When it happens: [the farm's execution plan](plan.md) · The septic tank and the grease traps: [the farm's water](water.md) · The compost bays: [the livestock yard](../livestock/plan.md)

## Hold points

1. **Sorted at the source.** Every place that makes waste (the galley, the bar, the rooms, the office) has three bins: food, dry, and e-waste or batteries. Nothing is mixed after that.
2. **No single-use plastic bought.** The galley and the bar buy no carrier bags, straws, plastic cutlery, cups, plates or take-away boxes [V REG-26].
3. **Nothing burned or buried.** No waste is burned or buried on the land; e-waste never [V REG-27].
4. **Hot compost before food.** Compost touches nothing eaten until it has reached 55 °C for 3 days ([the livestock yard](../livestock/plan.md)).

## Steps

1. Ask Gashora sector office which licensed collector serves the sector, how often, and at what fee; if none comes, plan a weekly run to the collection point the sector names.
2. Build a roofed waste store by the service road, with bays for glass, cans, paper and card, other plastic, and a locked box for e-waste and batteries.
3. Put the three bins in every place before it opens, and label them in Kinyarwanda and English.
4. Choose the bar's and the galley's reusables before the first order: glass, steel straws or none, cloth, and refillable water.
5. Find a licensed e-waste collector for the batteries and panels at the end of their life, when the solar system is bought.
6. Book the septic truck with the inspection ([the farm's water](water.md)), and check its RURA licence.
7. For the first month, weigh each bin's contents once a week, to size the store and the collection.
