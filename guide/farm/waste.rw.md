# Ifamu: imyanda

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Nta kintu kirapimwa, kandi uwakusanya imyanda wakorera Gashora ntaramenyekana [U].

Ibyo ifamu ijugunya, aho buri bwoko bujya, n'amategeko igomba kubahiriza, ku bw'nyir'ifamu n'abakozi.

Inama mu murongo umwe: muvangure byose aho bikorerwa, ibibora mubisubize ku nkoko no mu ifumbire (compost), ntimugure plastiki ikoreshwa rimwe, ibisigaye mubihe abakusanya imyanda bafite uruhushya; ntimutwike kandi ntimutabe.

English: [read this guide in English](waste.md) · Igihe bikorwa: [gahunda y'imirimo y'ifamu](plan.md) · Septic n'imitego y'amavuta (grease traps): [amazi y'ifamu](water.md) · Ibyumba by'ifumbire: [ikiraro cy'amatungo](../livestock/plan.md)

## Aho guhagarara ngo bisuzumwe (hold points)

1. **Kuvangura aho bikorerwa.** Buri hantu hakorerwa imyanda (galley, bar, ibyumba, biro) hagira indobo eshatu: ibiryo, ibyumye, na e-waste cyangwa bateri. Nta kintu kivangwa nyuma yaho.
2. **Nta plastiki ikoreshwa rimwe igurwa.** Galley na bar ntibigura amasashe, imiheha, ibikoresho byo kurya bya plastiki, ibikombe, amasahani cyangwa udukarito two gutahana [V REG-26].
3. **Nta gutwika cyangwa gutaba.** Nta myanda itwikwa cyangwa itabwa ku butaka; e-waste na rimwe [V REG-27].
4. **Ifumbire ishyushye mbere y'ibiryo.** Ifumbire ntikora ku kintu kiribwa kugeza igeze kuri 55 °C mu minsi 3 ([ikiraro cy'amatungo](../livestock/plan.md)).

## Intambwe

1. Mubaze ibiro by'umurenge wa Gashora ukusanya ufite uruhushya ukorera umurenge, inshuro n'amafaranga; niba ntawe uza, muteganye urugendo rwa buri cyumweru ku ho gukusanyiriza umurenge uzavuga.
2. Mwubake ububiko bw'imyanda butwikiriye hafi y'umuhanda w'akazi, bufite ibice by'ibirahure, amakopo, impapuro n'amakarito, indi plastiki, n'agasanduku gafunze ka e-waste na bateri.
3. Mushyire indobo eshatu ahantu hose mbere y'uko hafungura, mwandikeho mu Kinyarwanda no mu Cyongereza.
4. Muhitemo ibikoreshwa kenshi bya bar na galley mbere y'itumiza rya mbere: ibirahure, imiheha y'icyuma cyangwa ntayo, imyenda, n'amazi yongera kuzuzwa.
5. Mushake ukusanya e-waste ufite uruhushya uzakira bateri n'imirasire iyo zishaje, igihe sisitemu y'izuba iguzwe.
6. Mutumize imodoka ya septic hamwe n'igenzura ([amazi y'ifamu](water.md)), murebe uruhushya rwayo rwa RURA.
7. Mu kwezi kwa mbere, mupime ibiri muri buri ndobo rimwe mu cyumweru, kugira ngo mumenye ingano y'ububiko n'iy'ikusanywa.
