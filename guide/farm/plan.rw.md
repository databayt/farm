# Ifamu: gahunda y'imirimo

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: gahunda yo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ibyose bijyanye n'ikibanza nyirizina bitegereje aho ifamu iri n'icyangombwa cyayo (gate G0); amatariki ni intego, uko imirimo ikurikirana ni byo bifite akamaro kurusha.

Imirimo ifamu yose isangiye, ku murongo: aho iri n'icyangombwa, ibigerageza, umuriro, amazi na septic, n'insinga n'amatiyo bigaburira buri hantu uko hubakwa.

## Mbere yo gutangira

1. **Aho iri:** pin ya Google Maps y'irembo, cyangwa UPI iri ku cyangombwa cy'ubutaka (gate G0).
2. **Icyangombwa:** freehold cyangwa lease, nyir'icyangombwa, amatariki, n'icyo ubutaka bwagenewe kanditse kuri cyo.
3. **Ikiyaga:** intera iri hagati y'ifamu n'umurongo w'imyuzure, n'uko ikora ku gishanga cyangwa itagikoraho.

## Intambwe

1. **Pima ubutaka:** ongera ushushanye imbibi uhereye kuri UPI, wemeze amajyaruguru n'irembo, kandi ushinge imambo z'ibice hamwe na nyir'ifamu ([igishushanyo mbonera](master-plan.md)).
2. **Gerageza ubutaka n'amazi:** ingero z'ubutaka zijyanwa muri laboratwari ya RAB, n'amazi borehole itanga n'ubwiza bwayo ([amazi](water.md)).
3. **Saba umuriro:** igiciro cyanditse cya EUCL cyo guhuzwa n'ibiciro bitatu bya sisitemu y'imirasire y'izuba ([amashanyarazi](electric.md)).
4. **Ubaka uburyo bw'amazi:** ikigega cy'amazi, pompe yongera umuvuduko (booster pump) n'umuyoboro rusange unyura mu mwanya w'umwuka (air gap) ([amazi](water.md)).
5. **Ubaka septic:** igerageza ry'uko ubutaka bwinjiza amazi (percolation test), aho izajya, ingano yayo kuri buri muyikoresha, hanyuma ikigega ([amazi](water.md)).
6. **Shyiraho insinga n'amatiyo** bigaburira buri hantu uko hubakwa: sitidiyo n'umurima mbere, hanyuma ibindi ku murongo utarafatwaho icyemezo.

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Pima mbere yo gukata.** Atelier ntigira icyo ikata mbere y'uko ibipimo bigaruka.
2. **Nta gusudira nyuma ya foam.** Imirimo yose yo gusudira ikorwa mbere.
3. **Nta gihishwa kitageragejwe.** Insinga zitsinda igerageza ryazo kandi amatiyo agumana bar 10 mu isaha imwe mbere y'uko hagira ikibitwikira.
4. **Nta muriro mbere y'icyemezo.** Umuyagankuba (electrician) ufite uruhushya abanza gusinya igerageza.
5. **Nta matungo ubu.** Mbere na mbere, kwandikisha umworozi, umuganga w'amatungo, intera ya m 200 uvuye ku kiyaga, uruzitiro n'amazi ([amatungo](../livestock/plan.md)).

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Aho ifamu iri n'icyangombwa byemejwe kandi ubutaka bwapimwe.
2. Ifamu ifite igiciro cyo guhuzwa n'umuriro na sisitemu y'umuriro yatoranyijwe.
3. Borehole yageragejwe, kandi ikigega cy'amazi na booster bigaburira buri hantu hamaze kubakwa.
4. Septic system yatsinze igenzura ryayo.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Ese ~RWF 35M irahari ubu cyangwa izaza mu byiciro (tranches), kandi nyir'ifamu ashobora kuba ku kibanza iminsi ingahe mu cyumweru?
2. Ni ahahe hantu hazakurikira sitidiyo n'umurima, kandi ku wuhe murongo?
3. Ni nde uzashyiraho foam, kandi ni nde uzajya avidura septic tank?
