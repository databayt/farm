## The rules

1. No recognisable person without their consent, and no number plates (D-018, D-019).
2. Anything made or changed by AI is shown as a concept render, never as "today".
3. The voice is a stock AI voice or no voice, with music; never a cloned voice.
4. Every shot of the farm to come follows the owner's brief (D-029): timber-clad containers among trees and flowers, roof terraces with black steel stairs, transparent front canopies, the cigar and whisky lounge.

## Steps

1. **Pick:** choose the best moment from each clip on the story table; tone-map the HDR footage to standard video.
2. **Master:** for each scene of the farm to come, prepare a folder of reference images and a prompt on the Desktop (as for the camp); the founder makes the stills in Nano Banana (Gemini app) and drops them back.
3. **Animate:** slow pans and push-ins on the stills, or short AI video clips if the founder makes them in the Gemini app.
4. **Cut:** edit to music, add the titles and the end card; a stock AI voice only if the story needs words.
5. **Check:** no faces without consent, no plates, every concept shot labelled; then publish to `cdn.databayt.org/farm/en/film/video.mp4` and the reels beside it.
6. **Share:** the film on the homepage, the reels on social and WhatsApp status.
