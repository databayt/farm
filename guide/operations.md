# How the farm runs

Status: operating design of 2026-10-28. Team sizes and timings are assumptions [A] to test in the first months.

How a guest's stay, the kitchen, the field, water, power and waste work together day by day, and who does what.

## The loops that tie it together

| Loop | How it runs |
|---|---|
| Food | The beds and the hens feed the galley; the galley feeds the guests; scraps go to the hens and the compost; the compost and the manure feed the beds. |
| Water | Mains water, the borehole and the roof rain fill one tank through an air gap; the booster pump feeds every building; rain from the canopies waters the beds by drip. |
| Energy | The grid and the panels feed the site board; pumping, water heating and laundry run at midday on solar; the batteries keep Wi-Fi, CCTV, fridges and the path lights on at night ([power guide](farm/electric.md)). |
| Waste | WCs, showers and basins drain to the septic tank, desludged at least every 3 years; the kitchen drains to its grease trap; dry waste is sorted; food waste and manure become compost. |
| Money | Guests book and pay online or by mobile money; the galley, the bar and the field record what they sell; surplus eggs, vegetables and animals are sold. |

## A guest's stay, step by step

1. **Book:** on the website, by WhatsApp, or on a listing site; the booking holds a studio, a room or a tent.
2. **Pay and get ready:** pay by mobile money or card; receive the directions, the gate time, the house rules and a door code.
3. **Arrive:** the office at the gate welcomes, checks the booking and shows the way.
4. **Stay:** the studio, the guest room or the tent is clean, lit and stocked; Wi-Fi works everywhere.
5. **Eat:** breakfast and dinner from the galley, drinks in the bar lounge, or cooking in the guest kitchen and at the BBQ.
6. **Do:** volleyball, the fire pit, the woodland walk, the field and the animals, the study room for work or a workshop.
7. **Leave:** check out at the office, settle the bar and the meals, and get a message asking for a review.

## The team

| Role | People | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Host and manager | 1 | Bookings, guests, money, the team, suppliers |
| Housekeeping | 1 to 2 | Rooms, studios, tents, laundry |
| Galley | 2 | The cook and a helper: meals, the bar's food, the kitchen's stock |
| Field and animals | 1 to 2 | Beds, drip lines, compost, hens and goats |
| Night guard | 1 | Gate, rounds, the night lights |
| Electrician and plumber | on call | Faults, the monthly checks |

## A day on the farm

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 06:00 | Feed and water the animals, collect the eggs, walk the beds for pests and leaks |
| 07:00 to 09:00 | Breakfast from the galley |
| 10:00 to 14:00 | Rooms cleaned; pumps, hot water and laundry run on the sun; dinner's vegetables harvested |
| 14:00 | Deliveries; the galley preps dinner |
| 18:00 | The path lights switch on; the fire pit is lit; the bar lounge opens |
| 22:00 | Quiet hours; the guard's first round |

Every week: plan the menu from what the beds and the hens will give, turn the compost, count the stock, test the RCBO buttons and the alarms. Every year: the septic tank inspection, and desludging at least every 3 years.

## Where each part sits, and why

- **The beds next to the galley** (the garden walk on the [master plan](farm/master-plan.md)): the cook picks what the menu needs every morning.
- **The animals behind the beds:** close enough to carry scraps and manure, far enough that guests don't smell them; the houses wait for the 200 m lake check.
- **The compost and the tanks between the beds and the animals:** scraps go one way, compost comes back.
- **The office at the gate:** every guest and every delivery passes it.
- **The bar lounge and the fire pit in the courtyard:** the evening gathers there, away from the rooms' windows.

## Meat for the galley

Selling animals live needs no meat licence ([regulations](../study/research/regulations.md)); meat served in the galley comes from a licensed slaughterhouse. Eggs and vegetables go straight from the field to the kitchen.
