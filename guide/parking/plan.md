# Parking: execution plan

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The way in is drawn from compound.json and a common way of laying grass pavers on a clay soil; the depths are planning figures until a builder sees the ground and the soil is tested [A].

Every step of building the way in: the green parking inside the outer gate, its edging, strips and stops, the lights and where the rain goes.

The way in drawn: [the parking page](/en/parking) · Its other pages: [materials](materials.md), [lighting](lighting.md), [drainage](drainage.md) · Where it sits: [the master plan](../farm/master-plan.md)

## Before you start

1. Confirm the gate's position on the survey: the parking, the turn and the rock road are drawn from it.
2. Have a builder see the ground and test the soil: the sub-base thickens if it is soft.
3. Run a percolation test where the soakaway will go, before its size is fixed.

## Steps

1. **Builder:** lift the turf and dig the box out about 0.32 m.
2. **Electrician:** lay the lighting duct before anything goes over it, about 450 mm down under a warning tape ([lighting](lighting.md)).
3. **Builder:** compact a crushed-stone sub-base of about 0.2 m, then a 0.04 m bedding of sharp sand and topsoil.
4. **Mason:** set the fieldstone edging round the box to hold the pavers.
5. **Builder:** lay the paver blocks butted; fill their cells with topsoil and seed them.
6. **Mason:** the granite sett strips between the bays, the turn and the rock road; pin the wheel stops.
7. **Builder:** the overflow trench across the west end and the soakaway pit ([drainage](drainage.md)).
8. **Electrician:** the box on the sign wall, the letters and the lanterns; the tests.
9. **Gardener:** plant the shade trees; water the cells while the grass takes.

## Hold points

1. **The duct before the pavers.** Nothing is laid over the lighting's route before its duct is in.
2. **The soakaway after the percolation test.** Its size is a placeholder until the test.
3. **No cars on the grass too soon.** 6 to 8 weeks of rain or watering before cars park on it.

## Done when

1. The five bays stand on grown grass, with their strips and their stops.
2. The sign and both lanterns light at dusk and go off at the timer.
3. A heavy shower soaks away or runs to the trench, with none of the road's water running in through the gate.

## Open questions

1. Do five bays carry a full house? A full house is 22 guests in 11 parties and a team of about 7 (D-031): if half the parties drive, that is 6 cars for 5 bays before the team's.
2. Where does a minibus or a delivery truck turn? The open end is 9 × 6 m and the rock road 4 m wide; as drawn, it backs out through the gate across the public road.
3. Reversing out: a parked car's tail is about 3 m from the sign wall or the hedge, less than a car's length; bays at 90 degrees usually have about 6 m behind them [A].
4. A car waiting at the shut gate stands on the road: the gate line is 0.8 m from the road's edge.
