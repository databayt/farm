# Parking: execution plan

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The way in is drawn from compound.json and a common way of laying a gravel car park on a clay soil (D-065); the depths are planning figures until a builder sees the ground and the soil is tested [A].

Every step of building the way in: the gravel parking inside the outer gate, its timber edging, strips and stops, the solar lights and where the rain goes.

| What | Design |
|---|---|

| Drainage | the gravel takes some of the rain and its base sheds the rest, along the lot's fall to a trench at the west end and a soakaway pit |

## Before you start

1. Confirm the gate's position on the survey: the parking, the turn and the rock road are drawn from it.
2. Have a builder see the ground and test the soil: the base thickens if it is soft.
3. Run a percolation test where the soakaway will go, before its size is fixed.

## Steps

1. **Builder:** lift the turf and dig the box out about 0.15 m, its floor shaped to the falls.
2. **Builder:** lay the geotextile over the dug floor.
3. **Carpenter:** peg the timber edging round the box, its top 10 mm above the finished gravel.
4. **Builder:** compact a base of murram or crushed stone with its fines, about 0.11 m in two layers.
5. **Builder:** spread about 0.04 m of 6 to 14 mm angular gravel and roll it in.
6. **Carpenter and mason:** the timber strips between the bays and the wheel stops; the granite setts of the turn and the rock road.
7. **Builder:** the trench across the west end and the soakaway pit ([drainage](drainage.md)).
8. **Electrician:** the solar bollards on their footings, the lanterns on the pillars and the sign's panel, box and letters; each tried at dusk ([lighting](lighting.md)).
9. **Gardener:** plant the shade trees.

## Hold points

1. **The geotextile before the stone.** No stone goes on the bare clay.
2. **The soakaway after the percolation test.** Its size is a placeholder until the test.
3. **The falls checked.** The lot's and the turn's falls are checked with a level before the gravel goes down.

## Done when

1. The five bays stand on rolled gravel in its edging, with their strips and their stops.
2. The bollards, the sign and both lanterns light at dusk on their own, and the letters go off at the timer.
3. A heavy shower runs to the trench and the soakaway, with none of the road's water running in through the gate.

## Open questions

1. Do five bays carry a full house? A full house is 22 guests in 11 parties and a team of about 7 (D-031): if half the parties drive, that is 6 cars for 5 bays before the team's.
2. Where does a minibus or a delivery truck turn? The open end is 9 × 6 m and the rock road 4 m wide; as drawn, it backs out through the gate across the public road.
3. Reversing out: a parked car's tail is about 3 m from the sign wall or the hedge, less than a car's length; bays at 90 degrees usually have about 6 m behind them [A].
4. A car waiting at the shut gate stands on the road: the gate line is 0.8 m from the road's edge.
