# Parikingi: amazi y'imvura

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano ya soakaway igenwa nyuma ya percolation test ikorewe ku kibanza [U]; kugeza icyo gihe ingano yayo ni iy'agateganyo [A].

Aho amazi y'imvura ajya ku nzira yinjira: anyura mu bwatsi akajya mu mabuye, ubuhaname (falls) butuma amazi y'umuhanda atinjira, umuferege na soakaway, na buri point.

English: [read this page in English](drainage.md) · Amazi y'imvura mu bishushanyo: [urupapuro rwa parikingi](/en/parking#drainage) · Igihe bikorwa: [gahunda y'imirimo](plan.md)

## Mu ncamake

| Iki | Uko byateganyijwe |
|---|---|
| Uyobora amazi (drain) | amapave na sub-base yayo: imvura igwa inyura mu myobo y'ubwatsi igafatwa mu mabuye mu gihe icengera mu butaka, bityo ubwatsi ntibukenera gully |
| Ubuhaname (falls) | parikingi ihanamye 1 kuri 100 mu burebure bwayo yerekeza ku mpera yayo y'iburengerazuba; aho imodoka zihindukirira hahanamye 1 kuri 50 hasubira ku muhanda rusange, bityo amazi y'umuhanda ntazigera yinjira anyuze mu irembo |
| Amazi arenze (overflow) | umuferege wuzuyemo amabuye mato (gravel) wambukiranya impera y'iburengerazuba, inyuma y'impande z'amabuye, ujyana imvura nyinshi mu cyobo cya soakaway kiri munsi y'ubwatsi |
| Umuhanda w'amabuye | uzamutse hagati (crowned), amazi atembera ku mpande zawo z'amabuye no mu bwatsi buri hirya yazo |

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Percolation test mbere.** Icyobo cya soakaway gicukurwa ku ngano igerageza ritanga.
2. **Ubuhaname bugenzurwa.** Ubuhaname bwa parikingi n'ubw'aho imodoka zihindukirira bugenzurwa na level mbere y'uko amapave ashyirwaho.

## Intambwe

1. **Ubuhaname:** ringaniza ahacukuwe hahanamye 1 kuri 100 herekeza ku mpera y'iburengerazuba, n'aho imodoka zihindukirira 1 kuri 50 herekeza ku muhanda.
2. **Sub-base:** amabuye amenaguye afata imvura, asindagiwe mu byiciro.
3. **Umuferege:** wambukiranya impera y'iburengerazuba, inyuma y'impande z'amabuye: amabuye asukuye ya mm 20 kugeza 40 muri geotextile azengurutse itiyo ifite imyobo (perforated pipe), ihanamye 1 kuri 100 ijya ku cyobo.
4. **Soakaway:** icyobo, amabuye apfunyitswe muri geotextile munsi y'igipfundikizo cya beto (slab cover), gifite ingano igerageza ryatanze.
