# Parking: drainage

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The soakaway is sized after a percolation test on site [U]; until then its size is a placeholder [A].

Where the rain goes at the way in: into the gravel and off its base, the falls that keep the road water out, the trench and the soakaway, and every point.

Kinyarwanda: [soma iyi paji mu Kinyarwanda](drainage.rw.md) · The drainage drawn: [the parking page](/en/parking#drainage) · When it happens: [the execution plan](plan.md)

## At a glance

| What | Design |
|---|---|
| The surface | gravel on a compacted base (D-065): the gravel takes some of the rain where it falls, and the base sheds the rest along the falls |
| Falls | the lot falls 1 in 100 along its length to its west end; the turn falls 1 in 50 back toward the public road, so the road water never runs in through the gate |
| The trench | a gravel trench across the west end, outside the edging, takes the run-off into a soakaway pit under the lawn |
| The rock road | crowned, shedding to its fieldstone edgings and the grass beyond them |

## Hold points

1. **The percolation test first.** The soakaway pit is dug to the size the test gives.
2. **The falls checked.** The lot's and the turn's falls are checked with a level before the gravel goes down.

## Steps

1. **Falls:** grade the dug box to fall 1 in 100 to the west end, and the turn 1 in 50 to the road.
2. **Base and gravel:** the base compacted in two layers to the falls, then the gravel rolled into it ([the execution plan](plan.md)).
3. **Trench:** across the west end, outside the edging: clean 20 to 40 mm stone in geotextile round a perforated pipe, falling 1 in 100 to the pit.
4. **Soakaway:** the pit, stone in a geotextile wrap under a slab cover, sized to the test.
