# Parikingi: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano n'imibare biva muri parking.json no muri gahunda; nta kintu cyo ku nzira yinjira kirashakirwa aho kiva cyangwa ngo gihabwe igiciro [A].

Ibyo inzira yinjira ikeneye: ibice byayo (irembo, icyapa, amapave, amabuye ya granite, ibihagarika imodoka n'ibiti), amatara yayo n'imiyoboro y'amazi y'imvura, nk'uko urupapuro rwayo rubigaragaza.

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: amapave, amabuye amenaguye, amabuye ya granite (setts), irembo.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/parking/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
