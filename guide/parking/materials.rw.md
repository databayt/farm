# Parikingi: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Buri mubare uva muri parking.json no muri gahunda [E]; umurongo ubona igiciro gusa aho sitidiyo igiha igice nk'icyo, igiciro cy'agateganyo cya AS-21 [A], bityo byinshi byo ku nzira yinjira bitegereje ibiciro by'abacuruzi.

Ibyo inzira yinjira ikeneye, ku rutonde rumwe: irembo, urukuta rw'icyapa n'uruzitiro; parikingi y'amabuye mato izengurutswe n'imbaho; aho imodoka zihindukirira n'umuhanda w'amabuye; amatara y'izuba; amazi y'imvura n'ibiti.

## Ibyo kugura

[Urutonde rw'ibikoresho](../../src/components/parking/docs-sections.tsx#ParkingMaterials)

Ishingiro, amabuye mato na geotextile biza ku mibare iri hejuru umwubatsi amaze kwemeza ubujyakuzimu (D-065).

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: amabuye mato n'ishingiro ryayo, amabuye ya granite (setts), irembo, ibikoresho by'izuba by'icyapa.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Ha umurongo igiciro cyawo muri `src/components/parking/bom.ts`, cyangwa ugihe igice muri `scripts/studio/prices.py` iyo buri hantu hakigura (hanyuma ukoreshe `python3 scripts/site/prices.py`), kugira ngo uru rutonde n'urw'ifamu bikigaragaze.
