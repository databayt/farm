# Marketing

Status: plan of 2026-10-28. Every message to a buyer or partner is a draft that a person approves before it is sent.

Who the farm is for, what it says, where it says it, and the steps from the build to a full calendar.

## The promise

An hour from Kigali: timber-clad container studios in a young forest, lake views, fire-pit nights, and food from the farm's own field. The gate sign says DIGITAL FARM.

## Who it is for

| Guests | What they want | Offer |
|---|---|---|
| Kigali residents | a weekend away without a long drive | Friday-to-Sunday stays, dinner and the fire pit |
| Expats and diplomats | quiet, safe, comfortable | studios, the lounge, good Wi-Fi |
| Companies | team days and retreats | the study room, volleyball, the camp, a set menu |
| Schools | camps and study days | tents, the study room, the field and the animals |
| Event planners | a place for birthdays and small weddings | the lawn, string lights, the galley |

## Where to say it

1. **The website** (farm.databayt.org): the film, the buildings, a WhatsApp button to book.
2. **WhatsApp Business:** a catalogue of the studios, rooms, tents and packages; quick answers; payment links.
3. **Instagram and TikTok:** the build as a weekly series, then farm life: dawn, the beds, the fire, the lounge.
4. **Google Business Profile:** the pin, photos, reviews, opening hours.
5. **Listing sites:** Airbnb and Booking.com for the studios and rooms, on one shared calendar.
6. **mkan:** databayt's own Kigali rental marketplace.
7. **Partners:** Kigali tour operators, company HR teams, event planners, and the schools that already use databayt.

## From the build to a full calendar

1. **Now, while it is built:** post the fit-out every week (the time-lapse from Day 1); open a WhatsApp waiting list.
2. **Soft opening:** one weekend for friends and partners; collect photos, reviews and what to fix.
3. **Opening:** the listings go live, the film goes out, the first nights at an opening price.
4. **Every month:** one event night (fire pit and music) and one company package, announced two weeks ahead.
5. **Every quarter:** refresh the photos with the season; answer every review.

## Measure

- Bookings and revenue by channel, and the cost of each booking.
- Review score and what guests praise or complain about.
- Waiting-list sign-ups before opening.
