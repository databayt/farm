# The digital farm

Status: plan of 2026-10-28. Manual first: a step is automated only once it is measured and repeated (the study's rule).

How bookings, payments, the gate, water, power, the field and the books run on simple digital tools, and in what order to add them.

## Stage 1: at opening

| Need | Tool |
|---|---|
| One calendar for every booking | the website, WhatsApp and the listing sites synced to one calendar |
| Payment | mobile money and card links |
| Guest questions | a WhatsApp concierge: directions, check-in, menus, house rules; a person takes over when needed |
| Doors | a smart lock per studio and room, a new code for each booking |
| Bar and galley | a simple point of sale with stock counts |
| Records | one shared sheet: bookings, sales, costs, eggs, harvest |

## Stage 2: the first season

| Need | Tool |
|---|---|
| Water | a level sensor on the tank and an alarm before it runs dry |
| Power | the solar inverter's app: production, battery, grid use |
| Cold food | temperature alarms on the fridges and freezers |
| The gate | CCTV at the gate and the parking, recorded on site, with privacy rules |
| The field | farm records: eggs a day, feed, harvests, treatments |
| One view | a dashboard for the manager: tonight's guests, cash, water, power, alerts |

## Stage 3: when the farm is steady

- Drip irrigation on timers, then soil-moisture valves, once the beds have proved themselves.
- Prices that move with demand on weekends and holidays.
- A guest page with the stay, the menu and the activities.

## Rules

1. Everything keeps working when the internet is down: the locks, the pumps and the till.
2. Cameras never point at rooms, tents or the bathrooms.
3. Guest data stays with the farm and is never sold.
4. Each tool has one person responsible and a paper fallback.

databayt builds and runs these tools on the same base as its other products: mkan's WhatsApp agent for the concierge, its booking and payment flows, and the school platform's records.
