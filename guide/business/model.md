# The business model

Status: first model of 2026-10-28. Every rate and occupancy is an assumption [A] until 10 comparable listings near the farm are collected and the first 3 months are measured.

What the farm sells, what drives each line, where it breaks even, and why it can keep itself going.

## What we sell

| Line | What | What drives it |
|---|---|---|
| Stays | 2 studios, 3 en-suite rooms, 6 tents | nights sold × nightly rate |
| Food and drink | breakfast and dinner from the galley, the cigar and whisky lounge | guests × spend per day |
| Events | night camps, birthdays, team days, retreats, small weddings | events per month × net per event |
| The study room | day passes, workshops, school groups | days booked |
| Activities | volleyball, the woodland walk, the field and the animals | packages sold with stays and events |
| The field | surplus eggs, vegetables and live animals | what the galley doesn't use |

| Events | 2 a month × 500,000 net | about 12M |

Method [E]: each line is units × rate × days × occupancy; guest-days count 2 guests per studio, room and tent at their occupancy. Costs to set against it [A]: the team of about 7 at about 150,000 a month each (about 12.6M a year), food at about 35% of food sales (about 9.6M), power (about 13.6M a year from the grid alone, about half that with the [solar system](../trades/power.md)), listing fees, upkeep and the septic desludging.

## When it breaks even

1. Add up the fixed costs per month: the team, power, internet, insurance, upkeep.
2. Work out what one sold night leaves after its own costs (cleaning, laundry, guest supplies, the listing fee).
3. Divide the first by the second: that is the number of nights a month the farm must sell. Check it against the occupancy above before building more.

## Why it can keep itself going

- **Food from the field:** the beds and the hens cut the galley's food bill and give guests a reason to come.
- **Power from the sun:** the panels run the pumps, the hot water and the daytime loads, so the biggest running cost shrinks.
- **Water kept:** roof rain is stored and drips onto the beds; the septic and the grease trap keep waste away from the borehole.
- **Trees and soil:** about 270 trees, no eucalyptus near the crops or the lake, compost back into the beds.
- **Local people:** the team is hired locally and the guides are in Kinyarwanda; materials come from Kigali workshops.

## What to measure first

1. Collect 10 comparable listings (glamping, eco-stays, lake lodges) within two hours of Kigali: rate, reviews, occupancy where shown.
2. Track every booking's channel, nights and spend from the first night.
3. After 3 months, replace each [A] above with what was measured, and record the change in the decision log.
