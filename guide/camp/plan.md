# Camping: execution plan

Status: design intent of 2026-10-31. The tent is the footage's hexagonal pop-up; the furniture's sizes are read off the render, not sourced [A].

Every step of pitching and furnishing a tent on the camping lawn, for a guest arriving at dusk. The design brings no power and no water to the pitches.

## Before you start

1. Mow a level patch of the camping lawn short, with nothing under it.
2. Count the tents against the public bathroom: it is sized for the campers among its users.

## Steps

1. **Groundsheet:** a groundsheet a little smaller than the tent's floor, 2.8 m across the corners, so rain runs off rather than under.
2. **Tent:** open the pop-up over it and peg its six poles outside the corners; roll the door up to its strap.
3. **Furnished:** the rug in the middle, the futon along the right wall, the floor chair at the back left, the cushions and the throw inside the door.
4. **Ready:** the tea board on the rug and the lantern at the back, for a guest arriving at dusk.

## Hold points

1. **Level grass, nothing under it.** The tent stands straight on the grass: no deck, no platform, no stones.
2. **The bathroom first.** No camper stays before the public bathroom is open and the septic system has passed its inspection (D-016).

## Done when

1. Each tent is pitched level and pegged, its door rolls up and down, and the rain runs off its groundsheet.
2. Each tent is furnished as drawn, and its guests have the public bathroom open.

## Open questions

1. The lantern: a flame or a battery?
2. A shower for the campers at the public bathroom: the tents have no washing of their own.
3. The guests' own kitchen and BBQ by the camp (D-028): where does it stand?
4. The distances on the lawn are the plan's, not yet surveyed.
