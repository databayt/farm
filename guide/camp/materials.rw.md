# Ingando (camping): ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano ziva muri camp.json, zasomwe ku ishusho (render); nta kintu kirashakwa cyangwa ngo gihabwe igiciro [A].

Ibyo ingando imwe ikeneye: ihema n'ibikoresho byaryo, kimwe ku kindi, nk'uko urupapuro rwayo rubigaragaza.

English: [read this list in English](materials.md) · Ingando mu bishushanyo: [urupapuro rw'ingando](/en/camp) · Igihe bikenewe: [gahunda y'imirimo](plan.md)

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: ihema, futon, itapi.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare w'amahema atandatu.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/camp/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
