# Ingando (camping): gahunda y'imirimo

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igishushanyo giteganywa (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ihema ni ihema rifite impande esheshatu ryizamura ubwaryo (pop-up) rigaragara muri video; ingano z'ibikoresho byo mu nzu zasomwe ku ishusho (render), ntizavuye ku bacuruzi [A].

Buri ntambwe yo gushinga no gutegura ihema ku byatsi by'ingando, ku mushyitsi uhagera nimugoroba. Igishushanyo ntikizana umuriro cyangwa amazi aho amahema ashingwa.

## Mbere yo gutangira

1. Kata ibyatsi bigufi ku gice kiringaniye cy'ibyatsi by'ingando, nta kintu kiri munsi yabyo.
2. Gereranya umubare w'amahema n'ubwiherero rusange: bwateguwe hagendewe no ku bakambitse bari mu babukoresha.

## Intambwe

1. **Isashi yo hasi (groundsheet):** isashi nto gato ugereranyije na hasi h'ihema, m 2.8 uvuye ku mfuruka imwe ujya ku yindi, kugira ngo imvura itemba hejuru aho kunyura munsi.
2. **Ihema:** fungura pop-up hejuru yayo, ushinge imambo (pegs) z'inkingi zaryo esheshatu hanze y'imfuruka; zinga urugi hejuru urufatishe umukandara warwo.
3. **Gutegura imbere:** itapi hagati, futon ku rukuta rw'iburyo, intebe yo hasi inyuma ibumoso, imisego n'igitambaro imbere y'urugi.
4. **Byiteguye:** urubaho rw'icyayi ku itapi n'itara inyuma, ku mushyitsi uhagera nimugoroba.

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Ibyatsi biringaniye, nta kintu munsi yabyo.** Ihema rishingwa ku byatsi ubwabyo: nta deck, nta platform, nta mabuye.
2. **Ubwiherero bubanza.** Nta mushyitsi ukambika mbere y'uko ubwiherero rusange bufungurwa kandi septic system ikaba yaratsinze igenzura ryayo (D-016).

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Buri hema rishinzwe neza riringaniye kandi rifashwe n'imambo, urugi rwaryo ruzingwa rukanamanurwa, kandi imvura itemba hejuru ya groundsheet yaryo.
2. Buri hema ritegurwa nk'uko ryashushanyijwe, kandi abashyitsi baryo bafite ubwiherero rusange bufunguye.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Itara: iry'umuriro w'urumuri (flame) cyangwa irya bateri?
2. Douche y'abakambitse ku bwiherero rusange: amahema nta ho kwiyuhagirira afite.
3. Igikoni cy'abashyitsi ubwabo na BBQ iruhande rw'ingando (D-028): bizashyirwa he?
4. Intera ziri ku byatsi ni iz'igishushanyo, ntirapimwa.
