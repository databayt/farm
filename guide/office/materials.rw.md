# Ibiro: ibikoresho

> **Ubusobanuro:** iyi nyandiko yahinduwe na mudasobwa (machine draft). Igomba gusuzumwa n'umuntu uvuga Ikinyarwanda kavukire mbere yo kuyiha abakozi. Iyo hari itandukaniro, kurikiza iy'Icyongereza.

Status: igitekerezo cy'igishushanyo (design intent) cyo ku wa 2026-10-31. Ingano ziva muri office.json; ibiciro by'amashanyarazi bigaragara ni ibiciro by'agateganyo bya sitidiyo (AS-21), naho ibikoresho byo mu nzu n'ibya network ntibirashakirwa aho biva cyangwa ngo bihabwe igiciro [A].

Ibyo ibiro bikeneye: ibikoresho byo mu nzu n'ibindi bikoresho, ibikoresho by'amashanyarazi n'ibya network, nk'uko urupapuro rwabyo rubigaragaza.

## Uko igiciro kiba icy'ukuri

1. Hera ku mirongo ihindura igiciro cyose: mudasobwa na screen, ibikoresho bya network, intebe imeze nka L n'ameza manini.
2. Saba ibiciro bitatu byanditse (quotes) kuri buri murongo, bifite ibisobanuro bimwe (specification) n'umubare umwe.
3. Andika buri giciro mu [gitabo cy'ibiciro](../../study/registers/prices.csv) n'itariki yacyo n'ugitanga (supplier), hanyuma ugaragaze ko umurongo wacyo wemejwe (verified).
4. Andika igiciro ku ikarita yacyo muri `src/components/office/rails.ts`, kugira ngo urupapuro n'iyi mbonerahamwe bikigaragaze.
