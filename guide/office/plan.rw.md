## Mbere yo gutangira

1. Suzuma uko umuyoboro wa telefoni ugera ku kibanza mbere yo kugura ibikoresho bya radio: router ya 4G na access points birabitegereje.
2. Pima kontineri nk'uko isuzuma rya sitidiyo ribikora: uburebure, ubugari n'aho ifunguka.
3. Teganya umusuderi ku mirimo yo gusudira isuzuma rizagaragaza: amakadiri y'inzugi z'ibirahure, ibyuma bya deck n'uruzitiro.

## Intambwe

1. **Umusuderi:** imirimo yose yo gusudira, mbere y'uko hashyirwamo ikintu cyose gishobora gushya.
2. **Umubaji:** membrane n'impapuro za hasi, hanyuma ibiti (battens), nk'uko biri muri [gahunda ya sitidiyo](../studio/plan.md).
3. **Umuyagankuba (electrician):** first fix y'imizunguruko 4, n'insinga za data zijya ku kabati, kuri access point no ku meza manini ([amashanyarazi](electric.md)).
4. **Umuyagankuba:** igerageza rya continuity n'insulation kuri buri nsinga, ifoto ya buri rukuta, n'uruhushya rwo gushyiramo insulation (release).
5. **Insulation na lining:** shyira insulation hagati y'ibiti kandi ufatishe lining z'ibara ry'umweru wijimye (off-white), nk'uko sitidiyo ibikora [A].
6. **Umubaji:** hasi h'imbaho z'igiti (oak), hanyuma ibikoresho byo mu nzu ([ibikoresho](materials.md)): intebe imeze nka L n'akameza kayo, ameza manini.
7. **Umuyagankuba:** second fix (board, amatara, priza, akabati ka network na router, switch, gateway na UPS yako), hanyuma igerageza n'icyemezo (certificate).
8. **Umubaji:** imbaho za deck na esikariye; hanyuma ibikoresho bya ahwa.
9. **Nyir'ifamu:** screen, mudasobwa n'intebe; igerageza rya mbere rya network unyuze kuri access point.

## Ingingo zo guhagarara ukagenzura (hold points)

1. **Pima mbere yo gukata.** Nta kintu gikatwa cyangwa gitumizwa mbere y'uko kontineri ipimwa.
2. **Nta gusudira nyuma ya insulation.** Imirimo yose yo gusudira ikorwa mbere.
3. **Nta kintu gihishwa kitageragejwe.** Insinga zitsinda igerageza ryazo mbere y'uko lining zifunga.
4. **Nta muriro mbere y'icyemezo.** Umuyagankuba ufite uruhushya rwa RURA abanza gusinya igerageza.

## Byarangiye iyo

1. Igerageza ry'amashanyarazi ryatsinze kandi icyemezo (certificate) cyasinywe.
2. Akabati ka network gakora kuri UPS yako, kandi access point y'ibiro n'aho data icomekwa ku meza manini birakora.
3. Ibikoresho byo mu nzu byageze mu mwanya wabyo, kandi ibikoresho bya ahwa biri kuri deck.

## Ibibazo bitarasubizwa

1. Ikibanza kibona umuyoboro wa telefoni umeze ute, kandi uturuka kuri sosiyete iyihe (operator)? Router, SIM yayo ya kabiri na access points bitegereje isuzuma.
2. AC na fan yo ku rukuta byavuyemo hamwe n'ibikoresho bya kera: ese umuzunguruko wo gukonjesha uzagaruka ku murongo usigaye (spare, C4)?
3. Ingano za Studio Display na Mac Studio ni iza Apple zibutswe gusa [A]: zemeze mbere y'uko ameza akorwa.
