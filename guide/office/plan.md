## Before you start

1. Survey the site's mobile signal before any radio hardware is bought: the 4G router and the access points wait for it.
2. Measure the container as the studio's survey does: the length, the width and the openings.
3. Book the welder for the hot work the survey finds: the glazed doors' frame, the deck's steel and the railings.

## Steps

1. **Welder:** all the hot work, before anything that can burn goes in.
2. **Carpenter:** the membrane and the floor sheets, then the battens, as in [the studio's plan](../studio/plan.md).
3. **Electrician:** the first fix for the 4 circuits, and the data cables to the cabinet, the access point and the big desk ([electric](electric.md)).
4. **Electrician:** continuity and insulation tests on every cable, a photograph of every wall, the release for the insulation.
5. **Insulation and linings:** insulate between the battens and fix the off-white linings, as the studio does [A].
6. **Carpenter:** the oak floor, then the furniture ([materials](materials.md)): the L sofa and its table, the big desk.
7. **Electrician:** the second fix (the board, the lights, the sockets, the network cabinet with its router, switch, gateway and UPS), then the tests and the certificate.
8. **Carpenter:** the deck's boards and the stair; then the ahwa set.
9. **Owner:** the screen, the computer and the chair; the network's first test through the access point.

## Hold points

1. **Measure before you cut.** Nothing is cut or ordered before the container is measured.
2. **No welding after the insulation.** All hot work comes first.
3. **Nothing buried untested.** The cables pass their tests before the linings close.
4. **No power on before the certificate.** A RURA-licensed electrician signs the tests first.

## Done when

1. The electrical tests pass and the certificate is signed.
2. The network cabinet runs on its UPS, and the office's access point and the big desk's data outlet work.
3. The furniture is in, and the ahwa set is on the deck.

## Open questions

1. What signal does the site get, and from which operator? The router, its second SIM and the access points wait on the survey.
2. The AC and the wall fan were dropped with the old furniture: does a cooling circuit come back on the spare (C4)?
3. The Studio Display's and the Mac Studio's sizes are Apple's from memory [A]: confirm them before the desk is made.
