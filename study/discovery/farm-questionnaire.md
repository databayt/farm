# Farm discovery questionnaire

Status: Stage 1 — for the founder to fill in · Created: 2026-10-19

Answer what you can and write `don't know` for the rest; a blank line is not an answer. The questions are ranked by how much the answer can change the farm's design or economics. P1 answers unblock the site-specific study. Send photos and documents alongside (see the end).

---

## P1 — answer first (these change everything)

**1. Where exactly is the farm?**
Why: climate, soil, altitude, water, crop choice and the drive to Kigali all follow from the location. None of the candidate zones can be confirmed without it.
- Google Maps pin of the gate (in Maps, long-press the spot, then Share, and paste the link):
- If possible, also pins of the corners, or a GPS track of a boundary walk:
- District / sector (umurenge) / cell (akagari) / village (umudugudu):
- The lake's name, and the distance from the nearest boundary to the shoreline (m):
- The route from Kigali (which road, the last town you pass, how long it takes):

**2. Land title.**
Why: the title type and term decide whether long-cycle trees (avocado, macadamia, timber) are safe investments and what can be used as loan collateral. The UPI also lets the parcel be looked up officially.
- UPI (Unique Parcel Identifier) number(s) on the title:
- Title type (freehold / emphyteutic lease / other):
- Registered holder (you personally / your company / someone else):
- Lease start and expiry date, if it is a lease:
- Land use stated on the title or the district land-use plan (agricultural / other):
- Mortgages, disputes, rights of way or shared use:
- Is there a survey plan (cadastral plan) and are the boundary beacons in place?

**3. Area.**
Why: every quantity in the plan (trees, herd size, fencing, water) scales with the usable area.
- Total area (ha or m², and where the figure comes from: title, GPS, estimate):
- Roughly how much is steep, rocky, swampy or otherwise unusable:
- How much falls inside any lake or wetland buffer (if known):

**4. Water today.**
Why: water is the most likely limit, and lake water may not be legally usable without a permit.
- Which sources exist on or next to the land (borehole, hand-dug well, spring, stream, lake frontage, piped WASAC supply, rainwater tank, none):
- For each: distance from the centre of the farm, and whether it still gives water in the long dry season (June–August):
- Have you or anyone else used lake water for irrigation or animals here? Any permit?
- Roof area of existing buildings (for rainwater harvesting), if known:

**5. Money and timing.**
Why: the plan was sized to under USD 25k (about RWF 37M) over two years; how and when it arrives changes the sequence.
- Is the envelope available now, in tranches, or from income over time?
- Would you consider a loan (e.g. BDF-backed), a partner, or reinvesting early sales?
- How much monthly income, if any, does the farm need to produce, and by when?

**6. People and time on site.**
Why: labour availability and supervision decide which lines are realistic (goats need daily care; orchards have seasonal peaks).
- Who will live on or visit the farm daily? Any workers now (how many, monthly pay)?
- How many days a week can you personally be there?
- Typical local daily wage for casual farm work (RWF/day), if known:

## Photos and documents to send

- A scan or photo of the land title and any survey plan.
- Photos: from each corner looking inward, from the centre in the four directions, each water source, each building, the access road. Keep phone location tagging on so each photo carries GPS.
