## What this changes in the plans

1. **The guest-room block is a rebuild, not a fit-out.** The joined 40 ft pair needs stripping to the steel, the leak found and fixed, then a new ceiling, walls, floor and services, before D-061's three en-suites start [E].
2. **Water from the hill comes first.** A cut-off swale or drain above the compound, and the gullies checked, before anything new is built at the foot of the hill.
3. **The field is a slope.** The vegetable blocks of D-031/D-050 are drawn as flat beds. On the hill they need terraces or contour beds, or they move down to the flatter ground at the foot of the slope.
4. **The brick walls are a resource.** Finished, they can hold the wet rooms (public bathrooms, guest kitchen) in masonry instead of in containers.
