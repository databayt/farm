# Pitch deck — slide by slide

Status: Stage 6 draft · Date: 2026-10-19 · **Internal. Not for circulation without the founder's approval.**
Purpose: a concise, honest deck for conversations with advisers, a possible partner or a lender **after** the gates in `financing-strategy.md`. It is **not** a fundraising deck: external capital is not justified now. The deck's job today is to show a farm that knows what it knows.

## Legend (every slide carries these tags)

| Tag | Meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **CONFIRMED** | A cited source states it, or the founder said it | Source ID on the slide notes |
| **ESTIMATE** | Derived; the method is in the study | Shown with "est." and its label |
| **TARGET** | A future goal or plan, not a result | Shown with "target" and a date or gate |
| **UNKNOWN** | A real gap | Shown, not hidden |

Rules for the deck: no invented traction, customers, partners, commitments, certifications, photographs or financial performance. Any photograph is a labelled placeholder. Currency RWF; USD only with the rate (RWF 1,475.24/USD, BNR, 2026-10-19 [V MKT-04]). Placeholders in brackets are blanks for the founder.

## Slide 3 — Market opportunity

**On slide:**
- Current e-Soko national medians (RWF/kg, farm-gate / wholesale / retail): avocado 600 / 700 / 800; passion fruit 1,800 / 2,000 / 2,400; goat meat 7,250 / 8,000 / 8,500.
- Avocado exports grew from about 840 t (2019/20) to over 4,200 t (2024/25), about 90% to the UAE; NAEB is planting avocado in Bugesera and Rwamagana.
- Goat meat is 19% of national meat output; retail goat/sheep meat rose from RWF 6,000 to 7,500-8,000 in a year.
- Costs and prices are rising fast: urban CPI +17.2%, rural +22.0% (Sept 2026).
- Gap: most fruit and goats move through traders; farmers are unorganised and have no buyer contracts.

## Slide 9 — Technology and operational advantage

**On slide:** What technology is justified now: a records system (plantings, costs, sales, lot codes), a weighing scale, phone-based ordering, simple weather and water logging. The advantage we are aiming for is **information discipline**, not gadgets.
