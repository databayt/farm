## Fire and life safety (per studio)

- Smoke alarm (10-year battery) in the room, heat alarm in the kitchen, tested at every changeover.
- 2 kg ABC extinguisher and a fire blanket by the kitchen.
- No gas indoors (induction hob); the water heater has a temperature-and-pressure relief valve piped outside.
- The 3 m slider is the escape route; keep the deck clear; the slider lock opens from inside without a key.
- Roof terrace: guardrail 1,100 mm, no climbable infill, stair lights on, closed to guests until the engineer signs (R26).
- Hot water at 41-44 C at the shower and basin (thermostatic mixer).

## Guest information card

House rules, Wi-Fi, the drinking-water tap, how the hob works (induction pans provided), the hot-water timer, the emergency contact, the escape route, insect mesh closed at dusk.

## Changeover checklist

Strip and remake the bed; clean the bathroom and the kitchen; empty the fridge; check the alarms, the slider lock and the insect mesh; restock the filter, soap and paper; note anything broken by part id (the ids are on the parts and in the 3D model).

## Maintenance

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Monthly | RCBO test buttons; alarms; clean the extract fan, the hood filter and the gutters |
| Every 6 months | Re-seal silicone in the shower; check the roof deck bolts and the guardrail; check for condensation behind the bathroom panels |
| Yearly | Electrician's inspection; earth-electrode reading; descale the heater; repaint rust spots |
| Every 3 years | Desludge the septic tank [V STU-11] |
