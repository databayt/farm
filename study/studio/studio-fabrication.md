# Studio fabrication

Status: workshop instructions for rev A. Fabrication starts only from a FOR CONSTRUCTION set (after the survey).

## The workshop pack

Hand the workshop, per batch of three kits:

1. Cutting diagrams C-101 to C-108 and the [cut list](../../public/designs/studio/data/cut-list.csv): every part with its id, size, material, edge banding and step.
2. Kit sheets J-101 to J-107: exploded views and part tables.
3. Templates T-01 printed at 100% (check the 100 mm bar) and the 32 mm drilling jig.
4. The hardware list (bags A to L) and the [bill of materials](studio-bom.md).

## Order of work

| Stage | What | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Buy | Boards for three kits at once, from one batch (thickness varies between batches; plywood sold as 18 mm can measure 17.8) | Measure ten sheets; if not 18.0 plus or minus 0.3, tell the designer before cutting |
| 2 Rip | Rip every sheet into strips along the red lines | Strip widths plus or minus 0.5 mm |
| 3 Crosscut | Crosscut strips into parts; mark each id on a hidden face immediately | Diagonals of large panels equal within 1 mm |
| 4 Edge | ABS on the edges the cut list marks (L1 = one long edge, L2S2 = all round) | No glue lines; corners trimmed |
| 5 Drill faces | Templates or jig: confirmat, dowel, shelf rows, hinge cups | Test-assemble one carcass per batch |
| 6 Drill edges | Bushed jig: 5 mm pilots and 8 mm dowels in panel edges | Pins line up across the joint |
| 7 Finish | PU varnish on linings and exposed ply; seal every marine-ply edge and hole in the bathroom kit | Dry, dust-free |
| 8 Dry-fit | Assemble unit 1's kitchen, wardrobe and bed in the workshop | Doors and drawers run; adjust before packing |
| 9 Pack | One bundle per kit per studio, each at most about 25 kg, labelled `Studio n / Kit / bundle x of y` with a parts list | Count against the cut list |

## Batch of three

All three studios are cut in one batch: one board order, one set-up per operation, and the dry-fit of unit 1 catches errors before units 2 and 3 are drilled. Parts are sized to the smallest surveyed unit; each unit's scribe and filler strips are cut on site.

## Steel workshop

The roof frame, canopies, stair and the W1 infill follow the [roof terrace](studio-roof.md) drawings and the steel list. Bolted connections, holes drilled before painting, every member stamped with its id. A trial assembly of one roof frame on the ground, before painting, is worth the day it takes.
