# Sales plan — specification sheets, offers, delivery, payment, tracking, feedback

Status: Stage 6 draft templates · Date: 2026-10-19 · Location: **unconfirmed** · Stock: **none**
Nothing in this file is an offer. No sheet may be shown to a buyer as "available" until the product exists and the founder approves. Fields in `[brackets]` are blanks to be filled from buyer interviews, not facts.

## 0. Rules for every sales document

1. Only describe what the farm can deliver **today**. Targets go in a separate line labelled "target".
2. No "organic", "pesticide-free", "export grade", "GlobalG.A.P.", "certified" or "cold chain" unless a certificate or the equipment exists and can be shown.
3. Every lot carries a lot code (§2.2) so a complaint can be traced to a harvest or animal group.
4. Prices in RWF. USD only with the BNR rate and date.
5. Weights are by the farm's own scale, calibrated; record the scale check date.
6. Only contracts the farm can fulfil (§3 committable volume rule).

## 2. Sample offers

### 2.1 When a sample offer may be made

Only when: (a) the farm has fruit or animals meeting the sheet; (b) the founder approves the buyer and the quantity; (c) the lot is weighed, labelled and photographed. Before that, the only thing that may be shared is the draft specification sheet, marked "DRAFT - not an offer".

### 2.2 Sample offer template (use after harvest)

```
SAMPLE OFFER - [FARM NAME] - [date]
To: [buyer, contact]
Product / spec: [product, variety, grade - spec sheet v__ attached]
Sample: [kg or head] at [RWF/kg or RWF] (or free, if the founder agrees)
Lot code: [e.g. AV-2027-03-01 = product-year-month-lot]
Harvest date: [date]    Delivery: [day, place]
How to judge: [what we ask you to check - size, ripeness, taste, packaging]
Feedback: please tell us [by date] by [WhatsApp / email]; a 10-minute call is also fine.
No obligation to buy. This is a sample, not a supply agreement.
```

Lot code rule: `PRODUCT-YYYY-MM-NN` (AV avocado, PF passion fruit, GT goat group). A lot is one harvest block on one day, or one group of animals bought or born in the same month.

### 2.3 Trial order offer (after a positive sample)

One delivery, quantity no more than 60% of the farm's proven weekly supply [A], at the price formula in `buyer-strategy.md` §4, delivery note and rejection rule included, payment per §4 below.

## 3. Contracts: only what the farm can fulfil

**Committable volume rule [A]:** weekly committed volume to all buyers combined is the smaller of (a) 60% of the average weekly volume actually harvested or ready over the previous 4 weeks, and (b) 60% of the seasonal forecast for the coming 4 weeks. At the start, for the first season, there is no history, so commit **nothing** beyond trial lots.

Contract guardrails:
1. No forward fixed prices beyond one season; use the formula in `buyer-strategy.md` §4.2.
2. Non-exclusive; no supply penalty above the value of the late delivery; a notice period of 14 days for volume changes.
3. Force majeure covers drought, disease, quarantine, road closure and fuel shock; the farm gives notice within 48 hours.
4. No promise of export grade, traceability systems, certificates or cold chain that do not exist.
5. Payment terms per §4; late-payment consequence: suspension of the next delivery.
6. Rwandan lawyer reviews the first contract and the LOI template before use [A].
7. Goat contracts: delivery is conditional on movement being legal on the delivery date.

## 4. Delivery procedure

| Step | Who | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Confirm order the day before: product, quantity, price, delivery window | Farm manager | order line in the tracking sheet |
| 2. Harvest in the morning, shade, clean crates (fruit); weigh and inspect animals (goats) | Farm team | harvest log, lot code, weights |
| 3. Grade to the agreed sheet; set aside off-spec for the surplus channel | Farm team | grading counts |
| 4. Pack, label (farm name, product, lot, date, net weight) | Farm team | photo of packed lot |
| 5. Load, secure from sun and rain; for animals, relocation certificate and papers on board | Driver | transport log (km, fuel, time) |
| 6. Deliver within the window; buyer weighs and inspects with the driver present | Driver and buyer | signed delivery note (two copies) |
| 7. Rejections: record weight and reason on the note at the door | Driver and buyer | note, photos |
| 8. Invoice the same day; send the delivery note scan | Founder or admin | invoice number |
| 9. Feedback call within 48 hours | Founder | feedback form |

Delivery note fields: date, buyer, address, product, lot code, ordered kg or head, delivered, accepted, rejected with reason, price, signatures, vehicle, driver.

## 5. Payment terms (policy [A])

| Situation | Term |
|---|---|
| First two deliveries to any buyer | Payment on delivery (cash or mobile money) or within 7 days |
| After two clean deliveries | Up to 14 days, then up to 30 days on request |
| Over 30 days | Not accepted without written security; over 60 days never [buyer database §4.3] |
| Method | Mobile money or bank transfer against invoice; cash only with receipt |
| Late payment | Reminder on day 1; call on day 7; next delivery suspended on day 14 |
| Rejections | Credited at the rejected weight and agreed price on the next invoice |
| Deposit | For goat orders above [RWF X] or special requests, 30% deposit [A] |

Tax and invoicing: unprocessed farm produce is VAT-exempt [V MKT-25]; income-tax treatment of the RWF 12M turnover exemption for a company is [U MKT-28]. Use the invoice format the accountant approves; state the tax status on each invoice.

## 7. Customer feedback process

1. Call or message within 48 hours of each delivery. Three questions: Was the quality as agreed? Was delivery on time? Would you reorder at the same price? Score 1-5 and one comment.
2. Monthly 15-minute review with each regular buyer: volumes forecast, price formula check, spec changes.
3. Record verbatim comments tagged "buyer-stated". Do not paraphrase into praise.
4. Quarterly: summarise changes made because of feedback (a "you said, we did" list). Publish on the website only with the buyer's written permission and real quotes.

## 8. Complaint resolution

| Step | Time | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Receive | same day | Log: date, buyer, lot code, issue, photos, quantity affected |
| Acknowledge | within 24 hours | Apology without blame-shifting; say what happens next |
| Investigate | within 3 days | Check harvest log, grading, transport temperature/time, weights |
| Resolve | within 7 days | Replacement, credit note or refund at the agreed price; a goat health complaint goes to the vet the same day |
| Root cause | within 14 days | Record the cause and the change (grading rule, pack, route) |
| Close | | Buyer confirms or the founder closes with a note |

Escalation: a food-safety or animal-health complaint stops deliveries of that lot code and the founder decides on any notice to the buyer and authorities. Rules for reporting animal disease: every suspected contagious disease must be reported without delay [V LVS-40].
