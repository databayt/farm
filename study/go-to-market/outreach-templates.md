# Outreach templates

> **DRAFTS. DO NOT SEND without the founder's explicit approval, one contact at a time.**
> Per `CLAUDE.md` ("No outreach"), nobody has been contacted and no message here has been sent. These are scripts for the founder to review, edit and send himself (or to authorise a named person to send).

Status: Stage 6 draft · Date: 2026-10-19 · Location: **unconfirmed** · Product: **none harvested**

## How to use

1. Before sending, check the buyer's row in `../registers/buyers.csv`: public contact exactly as published, approach note, status. Confirm the business is still operating (several sites show 2021-2022 footers: KabFresh, Pride Farms, GET IT).
2. Fill every `[bracket]`. Do not leave a bracket in a sent message.
3. **Honest framing is mandatory:** the farm is being developed; there is no product to sell yet; this is market research. Never write "we supply", "we have", "our produce", "organic" or "export quality". Never name a location until the founder confirms it.
4. Do not attach price lists or specification sheets marked DRAFT unless the message says they are drafts.
5. Do not send bulk or the same message to many people at once. Log every sent message in the buyer register (date, channel, reply).
6. Two versions are given: **A. Standard English** and **B. Simple English** (short sentences, common words, easy to translate into Kinyarwanda or read on a phone). Kinyarwanda and French translations should be done by a native speaker before use; this file does not provide them [U]. Common greeting words known to be correct: *Muraho* (hello), *Murakoze* (thank you). Use them only if the founder is comfortable with them.
7. Never reveal another buyer's name, price or terms.

Placeholders used: `[FOUNDER NAME]`, `[FARM / PROJECT NAME]` (not yet chosen, see `../brand/brand.md`), `[PHONE / EMAIL]`, `[BUYER NAME]`, `[CONTACT NAME / ROLE]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[DATE]`.

## 11. Letter of intent follow-ups (only after the buyer qualified, per `../research/buyer-database.md` §4.3, and the LOI text is lawyer-reviewed)

**11.1 Offering the LOI**
> Subject: Non-binding letter of intent - draft for your review
>
> Dear [CONTACT NAME],
>
> Thank you for the useful conversation on [DATE]. As discussed, I attach a draft non-binding letter of intent and a draft specification sheet for [PRODUCT]. The letter records only that you intend to consider buying once supply starts; it creates no obligation to buy or sell until a purchase order or supply agreement is signed. The specification and price in it are the ones you gave me; please correct them.
>
> The farm does not yet have this product. Expected supply could begin no earlier than [DATE RANGE - estimate; do not state without the founder's approval], and I will only commit volumes I can deliver.
>
> I would be grateful if you could review it, change what is not right and tell me if you would sign.
>
> Kind regards,
> [FOUNDER NAME]

*Simple English:* "Thank you for talking with me on [DATE]. I attach a short letter. It says only that you may want to buy later. It is not a contract. I do not have the product yet. Please check the product, the size and the price. Tell me what to change. If you agree, can you sign?"

**11.2 Reminder after 7 days** (send once)
> Dear [CONTACT NAME], a short follow-up on the draft letter of intent sent on [DATE]. I am happy to answer questions or change any item. If this is not the right time, please tell me and I will note it. Thank you. [FOUNDER NAME]

*Simple English:* "Hello [NAME]. I sent you a letter on [DATE]. Do you have questions? Do you want to change anything? If now is not a good time, please tell me. Thank you."

**11.3 After a signed LOI**
> Dear [CONTACT NAME], thank you for signing. I have recorded the intended volume of [VOLUME] and price basis of [BASIS]. I will tell you at each stage (planting, first flowering, harvest forecast) how supply is progressing, including any delays. Nothing will be delivered until the product meets the specification we agreed.

**11.4 After a trial lot** (see `sales-plan.md` §7)
> Dear [CONTACT NAME], thank you for receiving the trial lot [LOT CODE]. Could you tell me about size, ripeness, packaging, delivery time and price, and whether you would reorder at the agreed price? A 10-minute call is enough. If anything was wrong, I want to know today.

*Simple English:* "Thank you for the first delivery, lot [CODE]. Was the size right? Was it ripe? Was the pack good? Was the delivery on time? Would you buy again at this price? Please call me or write. If something was wrong, tell me today."

**11.5 If the buyer says no or goes quiet**
> Dear [CONTACT NAME], thank you for your time. I understand this does not fit now. Could you tell me the main reason (price, quality, volume, timing, other)? It will help me improve. I will not contact you again unless you ask.

Log the reason in the register; never argue or push.

## 12. Do-not-say list

"organic", "100% natural", "pesticide-free", "export quality", "certified", "we supply [buyer-name's competitors]", "our customers include...", "guaranteed volume", "lowest price", "fully traceable", any figure not on a signed document, any location before the founder confirms it, any claim that the farm has a partner, grant or investor.
