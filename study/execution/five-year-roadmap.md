## 2. The calendar the work has to fit

Rainfall is bimodal in the eastern zones: rains March–May and October–December, dry June–August [V LOC-21]. CHIRPS monthly means at Gashora run 133–154 mm in March–April, 4–19 mm in July–August, and 110–129 mm in October–November [E LOC-34]. Higher zones (Shyira) are wetter and have a shorter dry spell [E LOC-34]. NISR statistical seasons are A September–February, B March–June, C July–September [E LOC-34]. This plan uses these working labels, all `[E]` until the site's own rainfall record exists:

| Window | Label | What it is used for |
|---|---|---|
| Oct–Dec | **Season A rains** | Planting window for fodder, trees and fast-cash crops in a normal year; pasture regrowth; gap-filling; year-end goat demand peak [V LVS-07] |
| Jan–Feb | **Short dry season** | Civil works: fence, tanks, holes, compost, soil correction; land preparation before the March rains |
| Mar–May | **Season B rains** | **Main planting window** (NAEB gives March–June for avocado [V CRP-34]); pasture and fodder sown; parasite peak for small ruminants in April [V LVS-46] |
| Jun–Aug | **Long dry season** | Water and feed are the binding constraints [V LVS-15, LVS-16]; young-tree watering; hay and silage use; the dry-season water check; the 2026 dry spell scorched Eastern pasture [V LVS-13] |
| Sep | Transition | Rains build; land preparation for Season A; avocado supply season begins (about September–March [V CRP-19]) |

### G5 checklist (all must be true, each backed by a record)

1. **Disease status.** Written confirmation from the district veterinary office or RAB that the quarantine is lifted for the farm's district and the source district, and that a farm-gate purchase and a move are lawful. The move is approved through IremboGov "Livestock Relocation" (free, 3 days) [V LVS-29]. Today's status is [U]; the last facts are from 2026-09 [V LVS-13].
2. **Placement.** Animal house and quarantine pen built (T44) outside the 200 m and 50 m lines on the staked base map (T31), with a dated photo.
3. **Fence and water.** Perimeter and paddock fences inspected (T37). Water covers the herd and the young trees through the dry season. Goats need about 1.5–3.3 L a day at maintenance, more when lactating [V LVS-27]; 16 adults need about 24–53 L a day [E]. The dry-season check (T48) has a first reading.
4. **Feed.** Fodder planted in March has had its first cut (about 90 days [V LVS-15]), and a feed reserve covers at least 8 weeks [A]. If not, the gate waits for October.
5. **Cash.** The purchase leaves a reserve for 4 months of closed markets and operating costs [A, from the 2 to 4 month 2026 closure, LVS-13 and LVS-05].
6. **Veterinarian.** A named vet is engaged (T29); the vet has reviewed the biosecurity SOP (T45) and the purchase source (T47).
7. **Animals.** A source with health records, a pre-purchase inspection, a quarantine plan (`study/operations/livestock-management.md`).
8. **Records.** The animal register and mortality log are live (T38).

## 5. Float and the critical path

Critical path to the first planting (days of float are measured against a 2027-03-01 planting start; rains-driven, so the real date moves with the weather):

`T01 pin / UPI` → `T04 title` → **G0** → `T05 site visit` → `T06 lab samples` (lab turnaround not known [U]) → `T13 results` + `T14 usable area` → **G1** → `T16/T17 matrix and model` → **G3** → `T20 seedling order` → `T32 nursery inspection and delivery` → `T39 planting`
Parallel feed into the same date: `T12 quotes` → `T22 contractors` → **G4** → `T23/T35/T37 fence and water` → `T24/T34 holes, compost, soil correction`.

| Link | Planned | Latest before the 2027-03-01 planting | Float |
|---|---|---|---|
| G0 | 2026-11-01 | 2026-11-08 [A] | about 1 week |
| G1 (labs back) | 2026-11-29 | 2026-12-06 [A] | about 1 week |
| G3 (order seedlings) | 2026-12-27 | 2027-01-10 [A]: nurseries need lead time, not yet quoted (T10) | about 2 weeks |
| G4 (works start) | 2027-01-10 | 2027-01-24 [A]: fence, tanks and holes need about 5 weeks | about 2 weeks |
| Planting | 2027-03-01 | mid-April (NAEB's window runs to June [V CRP-34], but later planting meets the dry season with small roots) | 6 weeks, at a cost |

Total schedule float to the March start is about **one to two weeks** [E]. A slip beyond that moves the main planting to Season A (October 2027) or shrinks March to a pilot block, which is G3's own fall-back.

Second critical path (livestock): `RVF status written confirmation` + `T44 housing built` + `T41 fodder first cut` + `T48 dry-season water reading` → **G5** → `T47 source and move permit` → `T49 purchase`. Its float is the 3-month gap between July and the October fall-back.

Third (irrigation): `T19 permit filed` (RWF 35,000; up to 90 days [V LOC-04]) → `T36 design freeze` → drip install. A filing at the end of December 2026 gives a decision about the end of March 2027 [E]. Until then the young trees live on rain, hand-watering and stored rain water. This is why the first dry season (June–August 2027) is the most exposed period for the orchard.
