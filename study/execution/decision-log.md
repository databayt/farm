# Decision log

Every material decision about how the study is run, or what the farm will do, gets an entry. When a recommendation changes, a new entry says why, and the old one is not edited. Newest entries go at the bottom.

Format: **ID · date · decision** — context · alternatives considered · consequence · revisit when.

---

**D-001 · 2026-10-19 · The study lives in this repo under `study/`, next to the future website in `src/`.**
Context: the folder was a fresh Next.js 16 app created for the farm. Alternatives: a separate repo for the study; top-level folders as in the brief's sample layout. Chosen because one repo keeps the study and the site that will present it together without mixing the two (internal working documents vs public content). Consequence: the repo must stay private while it holds supplier, land and financial details. Revisit: if the website repo ever needs to become public, split `study/` out first.

**D-002 · 2026-10-19 · Every claim carries an evidence label: [V], [E], [A] or [U].**
Context: the brief forbids invented facts and asks to separate verified facts from estimates and assumptions. The labels, the source-ID registers and the no-outreach rule are in `CLAUDE.md`, so every later session and research agent works to the same standard. Consequence: unlabelled numbers are defects, and the research files are checked for them before synthesis.

**D-003 · 2026-10-19 · Run the work in stages; Stage 1 covers discovery and desk research only.**
Context: the brief defines seven stages and lists the Stage 1 deliverables. The farm's location is unconfirmed, so site-specific design would be fiction. Consequence: nothing site-specific is claimed; findings are framed by candidate zone; the website waits for Stage 6. Revisit: when the founder sends the pin or UPI.

**D-004 · 2026-10-19 · The founder's planning answers become constraints, not preferences.**
Context: asked during planning, the founder answered: location "not sure yet", area 2–10 ha, capital under USD 25k for years 1–2, land owned by him or his company. The brief says not to plan for the lowest possible budget, but a stated capital limit is a fact to design around. Consequence: the decision matrix weights initial investment (8) and time to first revenue (7); the financial model computes a funding gap for each farm model; ambitious lines are costed with a financing path rather than dropped.

**D-005 · 2026-10-19 · Desk research runs as five parallel research agents; synthesis and verification stay in the main session.**
Context: location, markets, crops, livestock and buyers are independent topics. Agents ran on the research model tier set in the founder's engine config, with an identical evidence brief; each wrote only its own files. Consequence: the main session re-checks the decision-critical claims (prices used in the model, yields, legal buffers, supplier existence) before they enter the synthesis.

**D-006 · 2026-10-19 · The financial model is generated by a script, with a monthly engine.**
Context: the brief asks for a 24-month monthly cash flow and annual projections of at least five years. Tree crops pay late (avocado and macadamia beyond year 5), so the model runs 120 months and summarises 10 farm years. It is in real 2026 RWF with no inflation, computes cash before financing, and has no terminal value (conservative). The sale age, first-birth lag and receivable lag are fixed in the formula structure and changed only by rebuilding. Alternatives: a hand-built spreadsheet, harder to keep correct and to regenerate. Consequence: `scripts/build_financial_model.py` is the source of truth for structure; inputs can be edited in the workbook or in the script, with changes recorded here.

**D-007 · 2026-10-19 · Stage 1 does not recommend a farm model.**
Context: site suitability carries 24% of the decision-matrix weight and is unknown. Consequence: the three models are defined and costed with placeholders; the recommendation is a Stage 3 deliverable, gated on site data, buyer interviews and real costs.
