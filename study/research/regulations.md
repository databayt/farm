## 1. What changes the plan (read first)

1. **Two new laws are in motion and may replace the rules this register relies on.** An animal husbandry, animal health and fisheries law passed the Chamber of Deputies on 2026-07-28 and awaited presidential assent [V REG-14]. A plant production, seeds and crop protection law also passed Parliament [V REG-15, REG-16]. Neither gazette text was found [U]. They merge or replace Law 54/2008 (animal diseases), the seed and plant-health laws and the livestock-identification law; farmer registration, trade-premises registration and input licensing are new duties in them [V REG-14, REG-16]. Re-check the gazette before spending money on animals, a nursery or input trading.
2. **Livestock cannot be moved or bought freely.** All animal movement needs prior veterinary authorisation [V REG-01 Art 4]. In 2026 the RVF quarantine covers the Eastern Province and slaughter and meat sales are suspended in affected districts [V LVS-03, MKT-09]. No source found says it has ended [U]. Pig trading in Bugesera was also banned from 2026-04-10 for African swine fever (no pigs are planned) [V REG-22].
3. **The farm's own sale of meat is not "just selling a goat".** Slaughter, transport and sale of unprocessed meat sit under RICA: slaughterhouse licence, premise registration, a carrier registration for meat transport [V REG-03 to REG-06, BUY-61]. A slaughterhouse premise needs an EIA certificate [V REG-04] and is a listed ESIA project [V LOC-05]. Plan to sell live animals to licensed buyers or slaughter at an existing licensed abattoir, not to build a slaughter slab [E].
4. **Land and water rules are stricter than most small-farm assumptions.** Foreign holders get leases or concessions held under an approved investment business plan; a 90-day renewal notice applies [V LOC-41, LOC-45, LOC-07]. Buffers (50 m installations, 200 m kraals, 20 m swamp) and the RWB permit for irrigation over 1 ha are in `location.md` section 4 [V LOC-02, LOC-04].
5. **Tax is mostly light for a farm this size, with one open question.** Unprocessed farm products are VAT-exempt, agricultural turnover up to RWF 12 million is income-tax exempt (Law 027/2022 Art 21) [V MKT-25, MKT-27, MKT-28]. Whether Art 21 reaches a company is not stated in the text read ("an agriculturalist or a pastoralist") [U]. This affects the company-or-sole-trader decision.
6. **Employing even one worker brings RSSB pension at 12% of gross (6% + 6%) since January 2025,** rising toward 20% by 2030 [V REG-12], plus an occupational safety duty and a safety committee [V REG-13 Arts 77, 78].
7. **Export is a separate licensing layer** (NAEB horticulture licence, phytosanitary certificate, clearing agent). Its fees were not verified [U]. It applies only if the farm exports directly; selling to NAEB-listed exporters pushes most of it onto the buyer [E from BUY-31].

**Should the farm sit in a separate company? [E / A: decision for the founder with an accountant and lawyer.]**

- *For a company:* it keeps a clean liability line (animal disease, worker injury, a buyer claim) from the founder's other assets [E]; a foreign-owned company holds land by lease/concession under an approved business plan anyway, so the investment route is the same either way [V LOC-41]; Art 21 of the income-tax law speaks of "an agriculturalist or a pastoralist" and the corporate regime pays 30% [V MKT-28 Art 48], so a company may lose the RWF 12 million exemption [U]; the 3% small-business lump sum applies to companies and persons with RWF 12–20 million turnover [V MKT-28, Arts 14, 49]. Company costs: annual filings, bookkeeping and one resident director [E].
- *For a sole trader ("enterprise"):* the RDB portal lists an individual-trader registration [V REG-17]. Whether a foreign individual can register as one, and hold a lease in his own name, is not stated [U]. The personal tax scale is 0% to RWF 720,000, then 10/20/30% [V MKT-28 Art 14].
- *Working assumption [A]:* register a small Ltd as the holder of the lease and the operating licences, because every Irembo licence checked asks for an RDB certificate and TIN in the applicant's name. The exemption question must be answered by an accountant before the structure is fixed (see section 17).

## 16. Compliance checklist by stage

**Before buying anything (land, lease, equipment)**
1. Get the title or lease and its UPI; confirm holder, tenure, term, permitted use, and the date by which a foreign holder must apply to renew (90 days before expiry) [LOC-41, LOC-45].
2. Check the 50 m, 200 m and 20 m buffers on the real boundary and walk the flood line [LOC-02].
3. Decide the structure (Ltd or sole trader) with an accountant; register with RDB, get a TIN [MKT-34, REG-17].
4. Confirm the founder's visa or resident-permit class (B1/B2 for agriculture) [REG-17].
5. Check whether a farmhouse, shed or pond needs sector review or an ESIA [LOC-44, LOC-05].
6. Re-read the gazette for the new animal and plant laws and for any buffer amendment [REG-14, REG-16, LOC-09].

**Before planting**
7. Apply for the RWB water-use permit if irrigating over 1 ha (RWF 35,000, up to 90 days; start early) [LOC-04].
8. Buy certified planting material from registered suppliers; keep invoices; ask the sector agronomist about registration under input programmes [CRP-05, CRP-12].
9. Get a harvest permit before felling trees to clear land (free, up to 30 days) [REG-10].
10. Register on Smart Nkunganire for subsidised fertiliser if eligible [MKT-24].
11. Plan pesticide use with registered products only; pick up PPE for sprayers [REG-13 Art 79].

**Before animals**
12. Confirm with the district vet that movement into the district is allowed and what the RVF status is; do not buy until confirmed [LVS-03, REG-01].
13. Check whether the farm must register as a livestock keeper under the new law [REG-14].
14. Site the kraal at least 200 m from the lake and 60 m from a river [LOC-02].
15. Arrange a vet and a vaccination plan; buy medicines from licensed premises [LVS-47].
16. Apply for each animal move via Irembo (free, 3 days) and keep the certificates [REG-02].
17. Register as employer with RSSB before the first worker starts [REG-12].

**Before first sale**
18. Fruit: check buyer grade specs and RSB standards; no licence found for selling fresh fruit [REG-20].
19. Live animals: sell with a movement certificate; buyers handle slaughter licensing [REG-02].
20. Meat or processed products: stop and apply for the RICA or Rwanda FDA licences first [REG-03 to REG-06, REG-21].
21. Set up bookkeeping for the RWF 12 million exemption claim and file declarations on time [MKT-28].
22. Monitor turnover against RWF 20 million (VAT) and RWF 12 million (exemption) [REG-11, MKT-28].

**Before export (only if exporting directly)**
23. NAEB horticulture licence and exporter registration (fees not verified) [REG-18, REG-19].
24. Phytosanitary certificate per consignment; licensed clearing agent; customs declaration [REG-19].
25. Importing-country permit and residue compliance [REG-19].
