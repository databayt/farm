## At a glance

- [V] Official BNR rate on 2026-10-19: USD 1 = RWF 1,475.24 average (buying 1,470.24, selling 1,480.24) (MKT-04). Capital of USD 25,000 = RWF 36.88 million [E] (25,000 x 1,475.24). The rate was 1,449.14 a year earlier and 1,354.76 two years earlier (MKT-04), so the franc loses roughly 2% to 7% a year against the dollar [E].
- [V] Current price data exist. e-Soko (esoko.rw) publishes retail, wholesale and farm-gate prices by market, with entries dated up to 2026-10-14, for avocado, mango, passion fruit, papaya, pineapple, citrus, dessert and cooking banana, live goat, live sheep, goat meat and sheep meat, in 67 markets including Kigali's Kimironko, Nyabugogo, Kicukiro, Kimisagara, Nyamirambo, Mulindi and Gahanga and Bugesera's Nyamata and Ruhuha (MKT-03). Its address and official status need confirming (see Sources).
- [V] The WFP series for avocado, mango, passion fruit, papaya, orange, live goat, live sheep, goat meat and mutton stops on 2015-12-15, about 11 years ago. Only bananas, beef and staples continue to 2026 (MKT-01). Do not use the WFP fruit prices as current.
- [V] FAOSTAT farm-gate (producer) prices for 2024 in RWF per kg: avocado 573, mango 1,394, cooking banana 387, orange 917, papaya 708, pineapple 458, lemon/lime 1,583, goat meat 5,601 (MKT-02). FAOSTAT has no Rwanda price for passion fruit or tree tomato, and no sheep-meat price after 2015.
- [V] Rift Valley Fever: RAB quarantined cattle, goats and sheep from 2026-06-26, first in six districts (Rulindo, Burera, Gicumbi, Kirehe, Kayonza, Rwamagana), then the whole Eastern Province and parts of the Northern Province. Livestock markets in Bugesera, Rwamagana and Kayonza were still closed in mid-August 2026 (MKT-09, MKT-10). Status after 2026-09-28 not found. This hits the Lake Rumira/Gashora and Lake Muhazi/Mugesera candidate zones directly.
- [V] Land law (Law 27/2021): a foreigner can hold land only by emphyteutic lease or land concession, and only for investment (Art. 16); lease and concession terms do not exceed 99 years (Art. 10), concession on State land 49 years (Art. 11); freehold for a foreigner only by Presidential Order (Art. 6); private freehold is capped at 2 ha per person unless the Minister authorises more (Art. 6) (MKT-07). Whether a locally registered company with foreign shareholders counts as a "foreigner" is not settled by the text and needs a Rwandan lawyer [U].
- [V] PSTA 5 (2024/25 to 2028/29) names avocado, passion fruit, chilli and French beans as export priorities, puts sheep and goat "animal hubs" on land less suitable for cultivation, and enforces zero-grazing in the Eastern Province (MKT-05).
- [V] National Agriculture Insurance Scheme: government pays 40% of the premium, farmers 60%; eight crops and three livestock types covered (MKT-05). PSTA 5 lists insured livestock as cattle, pigs, poultry, fishponds and rabbits, so goats, sheep and fruit trees do not appear covered [U, confirm with an insurer].
