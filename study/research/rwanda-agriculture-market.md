Status: Stage 1 desk research — preliminary
Researched: 2026-10-19
Location dependency: mostly none (national/Kigali)

Labels: [V] verified (source ID) · [E] estimate (method shown) · [A] assumption (validation named) · [U] unknown. USD only with the BNR rate (RWF 1,475.24 per USD, 2026-10-19, MKT-04). Every price used is in `study/registers/incoming/mkt-prices.csv` (MKT-P01 onward).

## At a glance

- **Current prices exist [V/E].** e-Soko (esoko.rw, MINAGRI's market-price system) has farm-gate, wholesale and retail prices by market, latest entry 2026-10-14 (MKT-03). National medians of the last 30 days, RWF per kg, farm-gate / wholesale / retail [E from V observations]: avocado 600 / 700 / 800; passion fruit 1,800 / 2,000 / 2,400; tree tomato 1,300 / 1,500 / 1,533; pineapple 600 / 700 / 800; papaya 700 / 750 / 1,000; cooking banana 383 / 430 / 500; goat meat 7,250 / 8,000 / 8,500; sheep meat 5,000 / 5,500 / 6,000 (only 6 observations). Kigali markets are higher for avocado (700 / 740 / 800) and passion fruit (2,000 / 2,100 / 2,200).
- **Older datasets are stale for the lines in scope [V].** WFP series for avocado, mango, passion fruit, papaya, orange, live goat, live sheep, goat meat and mutton end 2015-12-15 (MKT-01). FAOSTAT: annual farm-gate to 2024 (RWF/kg avocado 573, mango group 1,394, cooking banana 387, orange 917, papaya 708, pineapple 458, goat meat 5,601), monthly only to January 2025, no passion fruit, tree tomato, or sheep meat after 2015 (MKT-02).
- **Prices and costs are rising fast [V/E].** NISR September 2026: urban CPI +17.2% y/y, rural +22.0%, urban meat +33.6%, vegetables +35.1% (MKT-15). The franc moved only 1.8% against the dollar in 12 months (MKT-04), so USD figures hide local inflation [E]. e-Soko avocado farm-gate: 400 (Jan 2025) to 600 (Oct 2026), +50% [E].
- **Capital [E].** USD 25,000 x 1,475.24 = RWF 36.88 million (MKT-04).
- **Small-ruminant market [V].** Trader-dominated; hotels, bars and restaurants (brochettes) are the largest buyers; live goats go mainly to the DRC; Nyabugogo abattoir slaughtered at least 120 goats and 40 sheep a day in 2022 (MKT-16). Live goats 2022 in Bugesera: RWF 20,000 kid, 40,000 medium, 60,000 big (MKT-16). 2026: adult "inzungu" goat RWF 67,000-78,000 at Karenge, Rwamagana; goat meat retail RWF 8,000-9,000/kg (MKT-03).
- **Rift Valley Fever is the main near-term risk for goats and sheep [V].** RAB quarantined cattle, goats and sheep on 2026-06-26 (first Rulindo, Burera, Gicumbi, Kirehe, Kayonza, Rwamagana; later the whole Eastern Province and the Northern Province except Gakenke); slaughter and meat sales banned in affected areas; cattle, goat and sheep markets still closed on 2026-08-13 (MKT-09, MKT-10). Status after that date not found [U]. The Bugesera, Muhazi/Rwamagana and Mugesera/Ngoma zones are all in the Eastern Province; Shyira (Nyabihu, Western Province) was not named.
- **Land, company, tax [V].** A foreigner holds land only by emphyteutic lease or land concession, only for investment; terms capped at 99 years (lease and concession) and 49 years (concession on State land); freehold only by Presidential Order; private freehold capped at 2 ha unless the Minister authorises more (Law 27/2021 Arts. 6, 10, 11, 16; MKT-07). A private company needs one director ordinarily resident in Rwanda (MKT-32). Unprocessed farm produce is VAT-exempt and the Ministry of Finance list exempts seedlings, drip irrigation, pumps, tanks, breeding goats and sheep, feed and veterinary medicines (MKT-25, MKT-27). Farm income up to RWF 12 million turnover is income-tax exempt (MKT-28); whether this covers a company is [U].
- **Insurance, subsidy, finance [V].** NAIS pays 40% of premiums but does not yet cover goats, sheep or rabbits, and fruit trees are not on its crop list (MKT-21, MKT-22). The July-December 2026 fertiliser subsidy covers fruit trees and bananas (MKT-24). BDF was folded into BRD on 2025-12-31 (MKT-35, MKT-39). The PSAC matching-grant window (avocado, mango; Bugesera and Rwamagana eligible) ran 5-27 February 2026 and is closed (MKT-40).
- **Name changes [V].** `rica.gov.rw` is the Rwanda Inspectorate, Competition and Consumer Protection Authority, not the Rwanda Institute for Conservation Agriculture (MKT-44). The University of Rwanda has no "CAVM" now: CAFF (Busogo) and CVAS (Nyagatare) (MKT-52).

## 2. Market structure

### 2.1 Fruit to Kigali

- [V] e-Soko records three price levels; in Kigali the ladder is narrow: avocado 700 / 740 / 800, passion fruit 2,000 / 2,100 / 2,200 (last 90 days) [E] (MKT-03). Nationally retail is above farm-gate by about 14% (avocado), 33% (passion fruit), 31% (cooking banana), 17% (goat meat) [E]. Narrow gaps suggest e-Soko "farm-gate" is taken near the market, so a remote farm likely gets less [A]; validate with three traders.
- [V] PSTA 5: few products pass through formal channels; e-Soko finds 50-100% price differentials across markets; feeder roads, storage, transport and market information are the constraints (MKT-05). Post-harvest loss about 40% (MKT-54, C).
- [V] Kigali Wholesale Market for Fresh Produce (Kigali SEZ) is not operating. Kilimo Trust, Aug 2025: "under construction", RWF 30 billion (MKT-54); a New Times report of 2026-01-22 (not directly verified, MKT-56) says the budget rose to about USD 53.4 million with agreements due February 2026 and 2-2.5 years of construction; EU/Netherlands support announced 2024 (MKT-55). Opening date [U].
- [V] Export: avocado exports about 840 t (2019/20) to over 4,201 t, over USD 8.6 million (2024/25), about 90% to the UAE (MKT-17); Hass is for export, Kigali consumers prefer Jumbo (MKT-18). Horticulture and flower exports USD 28.7 million (2020) to over USD 86 million (2025) (MKT-20); fruit about USD 604,000 of a USD 13.8 million export week, 5-9 Oct 2026 (MKT-42); all agri exports USD 1.1 billion FY2025/26 (MKT-19).
- [A] Farm to collectors or cooperatives, to wholesalers and stallholders at Nyabugogo, Kimironko, Nyarugenge, Kimisagara; hotels, restaurants, supermarkets via wholesalers or known growers; processors and exporters via aggregation. Not found (searched: fruit supply chain Kigali collectors, aggregators, supermarkets, hotels, processors, passion fruit buyers, English and Kinyarwanda, about 6 searches). Validation: buyer-discovery interviews with two hotels, two supermarkets, three wholesalers.

### 2.2 Goats and sheep

- [V, MKT-16] Chain: breeder, local trader or intermediary, livestock or export market, slaughterhouse (slaughter service only; the trader gets meat and skins back), meat retailers, supermarkets, hotels; skins to collectors and tanneries. No formal producer organisation; farmers complain of low prices from neighbours and traders.
- [V, MKT-16] Buyers: hotels, bars, restaurants (brochettes RWF 800-3,000 in Kigali, 2022), households at festivals, farmers buying breeding stock. Market fee RWF 500 per animal (2022). Main export is live goats to the DRC (7,000-10,000 goats and 4,000-5,000 sheep a week); skins to Kenya and Uganda.
- [V, MKT-16, MKT-09] Main Kigali outlet is the Nyabugogo abattoir (Saban): 120 goats and 40 sheep a day in 2022; four days without goats or sheep in July 2026.
- [V, MKT-10] Flock: goats 1.9-2.8 million, sheep about 601,836.
- No livestock market inside Kigali City found (searched: Kigali livestock market, goat market, Kinyarwanda, about 3 searches). The 2022 survey visited ten markets, including Ruhuha (Bugesera: 270 goats, 46 sheep on the day), Kayonza (200, 20), Rukomo (250, 30) (MKT-16). Supply to Kigali came from Eastern Province traders (MKT-09) and since the quarantine mainly from the Western and Southern provinces (MKT-10). A reopening timetable for some eastern markets appeared only in search summaries; not verified.

### 4.2 Fruit-tree, avocado, small-ruminant programs

- [V] PSAC (NAEB, IFAD, Spain; MKT-41): avocado 29,130 seedlings on 97.6 ha in Bugesera, Rwamagana, Huye, Rusizi; mango 4,579 seedlings on 15.3 ha in Bugesera; RWF 2.7 billion matching grants. Call 5-27 Feb 2026 closed; ceilings only on the online platform (MKT-40) [U]. Windows may reopen.

### 4.3 Input subsidies and VAT

- [V] Fertiliser July-Dec 2026: subsidy urea 45%, DAP 46%, NPK 43%, other blends 18-46%; list prices per kg urea 1,980, DAP 1,950, NPK 17:17:17 1,861; covers fruit trees and bananas; farmers register in Smart Nkunganire (MKT-24, C).
- [V] VAT: unprocessed agricultural and livestock products exempt (Law 049/2023 Art. 8(1)(k), kept by Law 009/2025); the Ministry of Finance list of November 2025 (142 pages) exempts seeds, seedlings, cuttings, fertiliser, irrigation pumps, drip tubing, water tanks, breeding goats and sheep, feed, vaccines, veterinary medicines, forage seed, tractors, meat-processing cold rooms; barbed and paddock wire appear "with licence" (OCR reading) (MKT-25 to MKT-27). Non-listed purchases carry 18% VAT that an exempt farm cannot recover [E].

3. RVF: current quarantine, tagging, slaughter rules and market reopening in the farm's district; do not buy stock before this is known.
4. Zero-grazing rule for goats and sheep in the Eastern Province and the required shed standard.
5. Local prices: confirm e-Soko "farm-gate" with three traders and farmers in the zone; live-animal prices by age and breed at two livestock markets.
6. Ask MINAGRI whether esoko.rw is the official successor and whether the Aug-Oct 2025 gap can be filled.
