## At a glance

- **"Kuroshira" is not a place name in Rwanda's administrative lists.** No sector, cell or village in a 14,837-village list is called Kuroshira, Kurushira, Roshira or Rushira (searched: LOC-18). Closest sound match is "Ku + Rumira" (Ku-ru-mi-ra vs Ku-ro-shi-ra: two phonemes differ), which points to Lake Rumira at Gashora/Rilima, Bugesera; next Gishari (Gishali) on Lake Muhazi; then Shyira (Nyabihu). Ranking is a sound/lake/distance judgement [E]; the founder must confirm.
- **Lake buffer is a hard legal limit.** Land within 50 m of a lakeshore (measured from the furthest flood line) is State public domain: no buildings or activities except protection works or Minister-authorised activities after an EIA [V LOC-01]. Law 48/2018 Art 42 also bars agricultural and livestock installations within 50 m of lake banks and cattle kraals, slaughter houses and cattle markets within 200 m of lake banks; fine FRW 500,000 plus demolition [V LOC-02]. Rules were under parliamentary review in Oct 2025 [V LOC-09].
- **Goat and sheep housing must sit at least 200 m from the lake.** This follows from Art 42(6) of Law 48/2018 [V LOC-02]; on a 2-10 ha lakeshore parcel that can remove a large strip from building use [E].
- **Water permits.** Irrigation above 1 ha, any infrastructure in a lake or river, dams, and farm/livestock water supply need a Rwanda Water Resources Board permit: FRW 35,000 application fee, decision within 90 days, validity up to 15 years; irrigation above 10 ha also needs an EIA certificate and feasibility study [V LOC-04]. Using water without a required permit is an offence (2-3 months and/or FRW 500,000) [V LOC-03].
- **EIA.** Ordinary crop-and-livestock farming of 2-10 ha is not itself on the ESIA lists. Triggers that could apply: chemical farming in a wetland, "large-scale" outdoor chemical farming (undefined), artificial lakes, water-storage dams (no size limit stated), slaughter houses, warehouses of 0.5 ha or more for perishable produce [V LOC-05].
- **Foreign landholder.** Freehold is for Rwandans; a foreigner (including a foreign-law company) gets an emphyteutic lease of up to 99 years or a concession of up to 49 years, only for investment [V LOC-07]. The founder's title type is unknown [U].
- **Distance to Kigali** (OSRM road graph, free-flow, central Kigali to shoreline points): Rumira/Gashora cluster 55-64 km, about 50-69 min; Muhazi shore 34-111 km depending on side and end; Mugesera 43-105 km; Shyira about 110 km, 107 min [E LOC-19].
- **Lake sizes differ a lot.** Muhazi 33-35.7 km2 at about 1,443-1,450 m; Mugesera 39-46 km2 at 1,300-1,360 m; Rumira only 2.2-4.3 km2 and part of a cluster with Mirayi, Kidogo and Gashanga [V LOC-12, LOC-13; E LOC-17].

## 1. "Kuroshira" — interpretations

"Ku" is the Kinyarwanda locative ("at/to"), so the name may be "ku + place". Nothing was assumed about the spelling.

**Method.** (1) Web search for "Kuroshira" Rwanda returned no place of that name (searched: WebSearch, 2026-10-19; only Shyira coffee pages came back). (2) All 416 sectors, 2,149 cells and 14,837 villages in an open compilation (LOC-18) were matched against Kuroshira, Kurushira, Roshira, Rushira, Rosira, Kuruhira, Ruhira and Shyira with a phonetic matcher (l = r, o = u, z = s, sh/shy merged, optional "ku" prefix). No exact or one-letter match exists. (3) The named candidates were checked by hand, and shore distances were computed against OpenStreetMap lake polygons (LOC-17) and OSRM road routes (LOC-19).

**Scoring rubric [E]** (judgement, not a measurement). Sound (a): 3 = "Ku + name" differs from Kuroshira by two phonemes or less in the same 4-syllable pattern, or contains "shira"; 2 = shares K, SH and R in order; 1 = shares only the R-R or -ra pattern; 0 = none. Lake (b): 3 = nearest lake polygon 2 km or less from the sector centroid; 2 = 2-5 km; 1 = 5-15 km; 0 = more than 15 km. Kigali (c): 3 = 60 km or less by road and on a main corridor; 2 = 60-80 km; 1 = 80-110 km; 0 = more than 110 km. Sum 0-9. Distances are from sector centroids, not from a farm.

## 4. Lake and water constraints (legal text)

Status of the legal picture: the texts below were read in full from gazette copies (LOC-01 to LOC-07). The buffer rules were under parliamentary review in Oct 2025, so check for amendments before design is frozen [V LOC-09].

### 4.1 Buffer-zone widths

| Water body | Width | Instrument and article | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lake | 50 m from the shore, measured from the furthest line reached by water in successive floods | Ministerial Order N°007/16.01 of 15/07/2010, Art 2 [V LOC-01]; Law 27/2021 Art 31(2) makes lake-bank land State public domain up to a distance set by Ministerial Order, excluding exceptional floods [V LOC-07] | State public property; protected area; authorities may not allocate it as private property (Art 4) |
| Big river / small river / unlisted river | 10 m / 5 m / 2 m | Order 007/16.01 Art 3 [V LOC-01] | Same status; the river list is in the annex of the Order |
| Lake or river (installations) | 50 m lake, 10 m river/stream: no agricultural or livestock installation | Law 48/2018 Art 42(5) [V LOC-02] | Prohibited act |
| Lake or river (kraals) | 200 m lake, 60 m river/stream: no cattle kraal, slaughter house or cattle market | Law 48/2018 Art 42(6) [V LOC-02] | Prohibited act |
| Lake or river (building) | 50 m lake, 10 m stream: no building in water sources, streams, rivers, lakes and their buffer | Law 48/2018 Art 42(7) [V LOC-02] | Prohibited act |
| Swamp (wetland) | 20 m from the swamp boundary: no building | Law 48/2018 Art 42(10) [V LOC-02] | Prohibited act; draining swamps or rivers without authorisation is also prohibited (Art 42(11)-(12)) |

### 4.2 What is allowed or prohibited inside the buffer

- **Allowed:** only activities aimed at protecting lakes, rivers or their shores, or activities authorised by the Minister in charge of environment when judged non-destructive, and only after a prior EIA (Order 007/16.01 Art 4) [V LOC-01]. Natural vegetation is reserved; artificial vegetation may be planted only to restore damaged land or to control erosion or provide habitat (Art 5) [V LOC-01]. The Minister can grant exceptions for tourism-related or specific economic projects [V LOC-09, grade C].
- **Prohibited:** buildings and activities other than the above (Art 4); dumping solid or liquid waste (Art 6) [V LOC-01]; agricultural and livestock installations within 50 m of a lake (Law 48/2018 Art 42(5)) [V LOC-02].
- **Cultivation and grazing in the 50 m strip:** neither text says "cultivation" or "grazing" in terms; Order 007/16.01 Art 4 bars all activities except the exceptions above, and the 2025 press describes farming and grazing as prohibited [V LOC-09, grade C]. Treat the strip as no-farm, no-graze, no-structure unless a written authorisation exists [E].
- **Livestock watering from the lake:** neither text mentions it. Water supply for livestock needs a water-use permit (below); direct access of animals to the lake through the 50 m strip is not addressed [U].
- **Sanctions:** fine FRW 500,000 and demolition of installations (Law 48/2018 Art 49) [V LOC-02].

### 4.3 Water abstraction and use permits (Rwanda Water Resources Board)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who needs a permit | Irrigation of more than 1 ha; infrastructure in rivers or lakes; any dam; fish farming in a lake or artificial pond; domestic water supply, water supply for livestock/farms and community settlements; coffee washing stations; industrial use; recreation needing water (Ministerial Order 001/MoE/24, Art 3) [V LOC-04] |
| Exempt (per RWB) | Small-scale irrigation under 1 ha; temporary works; households collecting directly from natural sources without a service provider [V LOC-08, grade C] |
| Volume threshold | None in the Order for livestock or domestic supply; RWB says requirement depends on activity, not volume [V LOC-08]. Whether a farm's own borehole or lake pump for animals counts under Art 3(f) is [U] |
| Documents | Application through the District Mayor; form; FRW 35,000 non-refundable fee (FONERWA); ID or registration (Art 4). Farm/domestic supply: project feasibility study. Irrigation above 10 ha, dams, infrastructure in lakes/rivers: EIA certificate plus feasibility study (Art 5) [V LOC-04] |
| Process and time | Decision within 90 days of receipt (Art 7); an incomplete application lapses after six months (Art 8); online application system (Art 9) [V LOC-04] |
| Validity and duties | Initial period up to 15 years or the activity lifetime, renewable (Art 11); stay within maximum daily/monthly/seasonal/annual quantities; keep meters if domestic supply, hydropower or irrigation above 10 ha; annual water-use report (Art 12) [V LOC-04] |
| Priority in allocation | Domestic needs, then environmental protection, then economic activities (Law 49/2018 Art 19) [V LOC-03] |
| Penalty | Water use without a required permit: 2-3 months' imprisonment and/or FRW 500,000 (Law 49/2018 Art 36) [V LOC-03] |

### 4.4 Environmental impact assessment triggers

- Ministerial Order 003/MoE/25 (in force from the gazette of 20/08/2025) lists works needing a **full** ESIA (Annex 1) and a **partial** ESIA (Annex 2); no licence or permit may be issued without the approval certificate (Art 4) [V LOC-05].
- Items that could touch this farm: agricultural activities using chemical fertilisers or pesticides in a wetland; large-scale agricultural activities using chemical fertilisers or pesticides outdoors ("large-scale" undefined); construction of artificial lakes; construction of water storage dams (no size limit stated, so a farm dam or pond is [U]); marshland reclamation; slaughter houses; warehouses for perishable agricultural commodities of 0.5 ha or more [V LOC-05].
- The Authority may require an ESIA for an unlisted project of similar nature with negative, irreversible impact (Art 5(2)) [V LOC-05].
- Process: practitioner selected by the developer; terms of reference approved within 14 days of receipt (Art 7(5)); decision on the report within 20 working days (Art 12(2)); public hearing (Art 13) [V LOC-05]. Fees and the issuing office were not checked [U].
- Any activity inside the 50 m lake strip needs a prior EIA under Order 007/16.01 Art 4 [V LOC-01].
- Wetlands: protected swamp land allows only research; unprotected swamp land may be farmed only under a concession from the Ministry in charge of land, with prior EIA for infrastructure (PM Order 001/03 of 30/01/2024) [V LOC-06].

## Open questions and field validation

1. Which place did the founder mean? Ask for the Kinyarwanda spelling, the lake name, the nearest trading centre and a Google Maps pin or the UPI.
2. Title type and holder: freehold, emphyteutic lease or concession; held personally or through a company (Law 27/2021 treats a foreign-law company as a foreigner) [U].
3. Exact distance from the lakeshore to the parcel's lowest and nearest points, measured from the furthest flood line, not from the average water edge [U].
4. Whether the parcel touches a swamp (20 m building buffer; protected or unprotected status) [U].
5. Whether the 2010 order or the Law 48/2018 buffers have been amended since Oct 2025 (parliamentary review) [U].
6. Whether a farm borehole or lake pump for livestock needs a permit, and whether a farm pond counts as a "water storage dam" for ESIA [U].
