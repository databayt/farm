# The study room's market: courses, retreats and day passes

Status: Stage 1 desk research — preliminary
Researched: 2026-11-02
Location dependency: Gashora sector, Bugesera (D-017); the pin is still owed (G0)

What the study room ([its plan](../../guide/study-room/plan.md): eight seats, a 65-inch screen, no air-conditioning) can sell, who buys it, what the same thing costs elsewhere, and what the team can teach. The business model built on it: [the study room's business](../../guide/business/study-room.md).

Prices are as published. Foreign-currency prices are converted at USD 1 = RWF 1,476.48 and EUR 1 = RWF 1,651.30 (BNR, 2026-11-02 [V EDU-50]); a converted figure is an estimate [E] even when the price itself is verified. Every price is in [the prices register](../registers/prices.csv). A price seen only in a search snippet, or copied by a job board, aggregator or newspaper, is [U] until the provider's own page is read.

## At a glance

- **Kigali sets the price.** A Kigali hotel's published full day is USD 50 a person, about RWF 73,800 [V MICE-22]; most hotels quote on request [V MICE-28]. No Bugesera venue publishes a meeting rate [U].
- **A desk is cheap in Kigali:** RWF 8,000 to 10,000 a day [V MICE-01], and kLab's is free [V EDU-01]. The bus from Kigali to Gashora and back costs about RWF 5,400 to 5,800 [E LOC-P08, LOC-P09], and the drive is about 50 minutes each way by car [E LOC-19]. Day passes for Kigali's remote workers do not work [E].
- **Local courses sell for RWF 30,000 to 120,000 a person,** and free ones compete [U EDU-25, EDU-26, EDU-27, EDU-13, EDU-24].
- **The money for skills is large but indirect:** the Skills Development Fund, the World Bank's USD 200 million programme and the Mastercard Foundation's consortia pay through calls, partners and tenders [V EDU-15, EDU-18] [U EDU-20].
- **The buyers who tender retreats want three or four stars and 10 to 25 people** [V MICE-32, MICE-34, MICE-35]. The room's buyers are small teams who book directly [E].
- **The nearest buyers are next door:**
  - the economic zone in Gashora sector, 14 minutes away, with 12 factories and about 1,500 workers [V LOC-70, LOC-72];
  - the airport works, projected to pass 6,000 workers [V LOC-61];
  - a girls' science academy, an agriculture university and an IB school within a few kilometres [V LOC-79, LOC-81, LOC-82].
- **The airport (target 2028) brings demand and competition:** its planned city has conference hotels and a convention centre for 5,500 [V LOC-57, LOC-73].
- **The rules:**
  - ask RTB before a course is sold [V REG-35];
  - courses, rooms and meals are taxable at 18% once registered, and donor-funded buyers pay 0% [V REG-44];
  - room nights add 3% [V REG-47];
  - reserved public tenders wait six months after registration [V REG-52].
- **The team's proof is live:** a school system with two pilot schools, a Kigali rental marketplace, an ordering site for a Kigali restaurant, and the engine behind them [V SKL-03, SKL-04, SKL-05, SKL-07]. Its time is published at USD 10 an hour [V SKL-08].

## 1. The place: Gashora and Bugesera

Distances are to Gashora's sector centre, the point LOC-19 uses, not to the farm, which is not pinned yet.

### 1.1 Getting there

- **Central Kigali to Gashora centre:** 58.5 km, about 50 minutes free-flow [E LOC-19]. The press line "about 30 km from Kigali" describes the district's edge, not Gashora [E, LOC-19 against LOC-58].
- **Roads:**
  - An 11.6 km expressway from the airport area to Karumuna is under construction, with no completion date [V LOC-64].
  - The 69 km Masaka–Bugesera upgrade (works from March 2026, about 18 months) does not pass through Gashora [V LOC-65].
  - The Ngoma–Ramiro road is finished from Bugesera to the Gashora wetland [V LOC-66].
  - The travel time once the expressway opens is not published [U].
- **Buses:**
  - Buses to Bugesera leave from Nyanza (Kicukiro), not from Nyabugogo or Remera [V LOC-67].
  - Nyanza to Gashora costs RWF 2,297 a trip since 6 April 2026, about twice the 2020 fare [V LOC-67, LOC-69].
  - With the city bus to Nyanza, a round trip from Kigali costs about RWF 5,400 to 5,800 [E LOC-P08, LOC-P09].
  - Operators and frequencies were not found [U].

### 1.2 The economic zone next door

- **Where it is:** the Bugesera Special Economic Zone (335.68 ha) is inside Gashora sector, in Ramiro cell [V LOC-70], about 10 km and 14 minutes by road from Gashora centre [E LOC-63].
- **June 2026:** 27 factories attracted, 12 operating, about 1,500 workers "from nearby communities"; about 70 more factories are needed for full capacity [V LOC-72].
- **Sectors:**
  - in place: fertiliser, footwear, hygiene products and building materials [V LOC-73];
  - targeted: agro-processing, pharmaceuticals, packaging, glass, timber, meat and garments [V LOC-71].
- **Jobs at full development:**
  - about 7,500 direct (80% local) and 2,500 indirect, in the developer's environmental study [V LOC-70];
  - 40,000 to 45,000 in the press, with no basis given [V LOC-72].
  - Plan on the study's figure [E].
- **Training:** the developer works with Gashora sector on a list of unemployed residents for its contractors and factories, and plans its own vocational training institute in phase 2 [V LOC-70]. That is a pipeline the room could serve now, and a competitor later [E].

### 1.3 The airport and its city

- **Where it is:** Bugesera International Airport is in Rilima sector, about 10.6 km in a straight line and 20 to 29 km by today's roads from Gashora centre [E LOC-63].
- **When it opens:** the target is 2028 [V LOC-58, LOC-64]. Earlier targets slipped (2024, then 2026, then "2027–2028") [V LOC-60], so 2028 is a target, not a date [E].
- **Size and owners:** phase 1 handles 7 to 8 million passengers a year (the sources differ) [V LOC-57, LOC-58, LOC-59]. Qatar Airways holds 60% of the project, which costs about USD 2 billion [V LOC-60, LOC-58].
- **Workers:** about 2,000 built the ground works, and the building phase is projected to take the total past 6,000 [V LOC-61]. Jobs at opening were not found [U].
- **The Airport City** is reported to span Rilima, Juru, Nyamata and Gashora, with hotels, conference facilities, a convention centre for about 5,500 delegates, technical colleges and aviation training [V LOC-57, LOC-73]. Whether the farm lies inside it is [U].
- **A Formula One circuit** is planned about 3.5 km from the airport, with races "in the early 2030s" [V LOC-62].

### 1.4 Places to stay and meet

- **Supply:** Bugesera had 77 places to stay with 1,190 rooms in 2024, up from 13 and 529 in 2018 [V LOC-75], about 15 rooms each [E]. RDB's analyst says supply falls well short of expected demand, with very few three- and four-star hotels [V LOC-58].
- **In Gashora:** Eagle View Farm & Lake Retreat is growing from 6 to 20 rooms and reports about 300 visitors a week, by its own count [V LOC-58].
- **Bugesera Lodge,** an eco-lodge on the Akagera valley, publishes:
  - bed and breakfast at USD 60 for one and USD 87 for two in a bungalow, and USD 60 for a safari tent for two;
  - a four-course meal at USD 25 for guests who do not stay [V LOC-77, the "$" taken as USD].
  - It lists no meeting room.
- **La Palisse, Nyamata,** had 188 rooms and conference space for about 500 in 2015 [V LOC-78, possibly stale]. Its rates and brand today were not found [U].
- **No published meeting rates:** no Bugesera venue publishes a meeting-room or day-package rate [U]. They have to be asked.

### 1.5 Schools, colleges and youth centres

- **Gashora Girls Academy of Science and Technology:**
  - grades 10 to 12, 95 places a year (more than 860 applicants in 2024) [V LOC-79], about 285 students [E];
  - a Makerspace and Arduino workshops;
  - teacher-training retreats held abroad, in Kenya [V LOC-79].
- **The Rwanda Institute for Conservation Agriculture (RICA):** a three-year degree on a 1,300 ha campus in Gashora [V LOC-81], about 7 km from the centre [E LOC-63]. The district plan calls it a knowledge hub with openings for training partnerships [V LOC-86].
- **Ntare Louisenlund School,** Nyamata: an IB school opened in 2024, with more than 250 students [V LOC-82].
- **TVET:**
  - 11 accredited TVET schools in Bugesera in 2020/21, with electricity and computer systems among their trades [V LOC-83, stale];
  - Rwanda Polytechnic has no college in Bugesera [V LOC-84].
- **The Imbuto Hub,** Nyamata (opened 2026): free training, with an ICT lab and more than 20 rooms; it trained 117 young people in its first three months [V LOC-85]. A free competitor for youth courses, and a possible partner [E].
- **The district:** computer literacy is 14.5% (age 15 and over), and youth unemployment 23.66% against 17.54% nationally [V LOC-86].

### 1.6 What the plans say

- **The district's strategy** (2024/25–2028/29) wants Bugesera to be "a regional hub for sports tourism, conferences, and cultural events", and to use its flagship investments (the industrial park, RICA, the airport) "for skills development" [V LOC-86]. Its ICT budget is 0.05% of the five-year total [V LOC-86].
- **The national strategy (NST2)** targets meetings-and-conferences revenue rising from USD 95 million to USD 224 million by 2029 [V LOC-87].
- **Visitors:** Visit Rwanda calls Bugesera's wetlands among the most accessible birding areas in Rwanda, with about 415 species [V LOC-88]. Counts of memorial and birding visitors were not found [U].
- **Implication [E]:** the room's nearest buyers are not in Kigali. They are the zone's factories, the airport works and three schools within a few kilometres. Until 2028 the district has few rooms and no published meeting rates; from 2028 the airport city brings conference hotels. The room then competes by being small, quiet and taught, not by its size.

## 3. Who pays for skills

- **The Skills Development Fund** (RTB) funds private companies and training providers through competitive, results-based calls; ICT and energy are eligible; 282 institutions signed in March 2025; at least 70,000 young people expected [U EDU-20, EDU-21, EDU-22, EDU-23].
- **The World Bank:** the earlier skills programme trained about 24,000 people, 82% in work within nine months, with ICT not a named sector [V EDU-19]; its follow-on, PSGYE, is USD 200 million for more than 200,000 young people [V EDU-18]. That is about USD 1,000 a beneficiary [E], an upper bound that includes loans and hostels, not a cost per trainee; no cost per trainee was found.
- **Mastercard Foundation:** 300,000 young people in work by 2030 through about 30 partnerships [V EDU-14]; its digital-skills programme reached more than 10,000 through DOT Rwanda, kLab, Igire, Intango TSS and SOLVIT Africa [V EDU-15]. Its money flows through consortia; no open call to a single provider was found. The EdTech Fellowship gives up to USD 50,000 a company [U EDU-17].
- **One Million Rwandan Coders** (2024–2029) is online and self-paced, about eight weeks; IHS Rwanda and the ILO (EUR 4 million, Luxembourg-funded, to 2028) are partners [U EDU-07, EDU-08, EDU-09]. How training partners are chosen: not found.
- **GIZ** tenders skills work (BPO skills, the Cyber Hub's essential skills, project management for public ICT staff) [U EDU-36, EDU-37], and its Digital Transformation Center lends its event space to startups for free, a free competitor for a small venue [V EDU-04].
- **KOICA** (USD 43.1 million in three 2025 projects, one of USD 14 million on TVET with Rwanda Polytechnic), **LuxDev** (ICT short courses bought within a total of EUR 10,000) and **Enabel** [U EDU-39, EDU-42, EDU-49].
- **NGOs and microfinance banks** buy staff training through requests for proposals on job boards: SNV (local firms only, price 30 of 100 points), BRAC, World Vision, COPEDU [V EDU-38] [U EDU-40, EDU-41, EDU-46].
- **Implication [E]:** the money for skills is large, but it reaches a provider through calls, consortia and tenders. The farm reaches it first as the venue or a subcontractor of an existing partner, and databayt as a provider once the rules in §4 allow it.

## 4. The rules

Nothing here is legal or tax advice. What RTB, RRA, RDB and a lawyer must confirm is in the open questions. Earlier rules (the RDB tourism licence, REG-30, REG-31, STU-35; data protection, GTM-01, GTM-02) are not repeated.

### 4.1 Selling courses

- **RTB's 2024 guideline:** a company, NGO or association that wants to run short courses "must submit a request for that purpose to RTB". RTB checks that the curriculum is RTB-authorised, and only then does a course lead to RTB's Certificate of Competence [V REG-35]. No fee or timeline is published.
- **The education law:** a short-term TVET school opens only with the written approval of the agency that inspects basic education. Opening one, starting a new course or teaching an unauthorised curriculum without that approval is fined RWF 500,000 to 1,000,000, and the activity is suspended [V REG-34]. Neither text says whether a company's professional workshops are a "school" [U], and RTB's founding order gives it no licensing power [V REG-36].
- **Three kinds of certificate [E, from REG-34, REG-35, REG-37]:**
  - databayt's own certificate of attendance: no rule for or against it was found [U];
  - RTB's Certificate of Competence: it needs RTB's authorisation and an RTB-authorised curriculum;
  - a qualification on the national framework: only through an accredited institution.
  A course is never called a degree or a diploma.
- **The Skills Development Fund** co-finances employer-led short training. Companies, associations, incubation centres and experienced training providers may apply; the 2024 call closed on 10 January 2025 [V REG-39]. Whether it requires RTB's authorisation is [U].
- **So [E]:** ask RTB in writing before any course is advertised. Until it answers, sell closed courses to one company with databayt's own certificate of attendance, and reach funded cohorts through an accredited partner.

### 4.2 Hosting groups

- **No venue, conference or event licence** exists in the tourism law, its licensing order or RDB's 2026 service charter [E, from REG-40, REG-41, REG-43]. The farm's accommodation licence (RWF 80,000, no expiry [V REG-41, REG-P01]) is the one that applies.
- **Meals for day guests** who do not stay may need a restaurant licence (category B) [U].
- **Meeting rooms are graded, not licensed:** a lodge needs a function room of at least 1.5 m² a guest at one star [V REG-41]. The room is 11.94 × 2.26 m inside its linings ([its plan](../../guide/study-room/plan.md)), about 27 m², or 3.4 m² a person for eight [E].
- **Fire:** the 2014 rules for a "public building", one "intended to be used by many people", ask for a fire assessment before use, an alarm, smoke detectors, sprinklers, hose reels and CCTV [V REG-42]. Whether an eight-seat room is one is [U].
- **Guests who stay** are recorded with their identity documents for immigration officers [V REG-58].

### 4.3 Tax

- **VAT** is 18% or 0% [V REG-44]. A business registers above RWF 20 million of sales in 12 months or 5 million in three months [V REG-11].
- **Courses are taxable in practice [E].** Education is exempt only if it is on a MINEDUC list, and the four lists approved since June 2025 name only materials and equipment [V REG-44, REG-45]. Online courses have been taxable since April 2026 [V REG-46]. Room hire, meals and rooms are standard-rated as well [E].
- **Donor-funded buyers can be invoiced at 0%:** international organisations with agreements with Rwanda and the projects they fund [V REG-44]. The procedure was not found [U].
- **Room nights carry a 3% tourism tax** since 1 July 2025, on the room charge before VAT [V REG-47]. It goes on a separate line of every quote [E].
- **Employers deduct training** that is planned in their activity plan and invoiced on an RRA-accepted invoice; entertainment is not deductible [V REG-48]. So a retreat is sold as a programme, with an agenda, outcomes and a named trainer [E]. Public employers file their training plans by 28 February of the year before [V REG-38].
- **Withholding:** the 15% applies only to an unregistered payee or one with no recent declaration. Public tenders withhold 3%, waived with a tax clearance certificate (RWF 5,000) [V REG-48, REG-55].
- **The district trading licence** is RWF 100,000 to 280,000 a year by turnover band [V REG-50].
- **Corporate income tax is 28%** [V REG-49], not the 30% in the study's regulations research (MKT-28): a correction to carry there.

### 4.4 Selling to government and donors

- **Service tenders up to RWF 100 million are reserved for domestic companies registered for at least six months** [V REG-52], so a new Databayt Ltd waits six months [E]. A single-source deal up to RWF 3 million needs no Umucyo account, once per bidder [V REG-52].
- **A bid needs:** the RDB certificate showing the tendered activity (training is ISIC 8550), the RRA tax clearance and the RSSB certificate [V REG-52, REG-53].
- **Development partners:** the World Bank buys training as a consulting service, choosing for a small job on the consultants' qualifications from at least three firms [V REG-56]. UNDP Rwanda used a request for proposals [V REG-57].

### 4.5 Trainers

- **The founder,** on an entrepreneur permit, "may work" in his own business [V REG-59]. Whether its class (W2, for IT) also covers solar courses and the stays is [U].
- **A paid guest trainer** cannot work on the conference visa (V5: "work is not permitted"). The route is the R special pass, RWF 50,000 for up to 90 days [V REG-59, REG-60].
- **A teammate teaching from abroad on the screen:** no text read deals with it [U].

## 6. What this means for the room

Each point is an inference [E] from the sections above.

1. **Sell the room by the group, not by the seat:** a two-day course or retreat for one team of up to eight, Monday to Thursday, with a night at the farm. It is the only sale whose price pays for the drive (§2), and the team's skills (§5) are what a hotel lacks.
2. **Two markets, in order:**
   - Kigali's small teams now: companies, NGOs, startups in the hubs, boards (§2.4);
   - Bugesera's employers as they grow: the zone's factories, the airport works, the hotels (§1.2–1.4).
3. **Price under a Kigali hotel's day and above the local courses:** about RWF 40,000 a person for the day, against USD 50 at a Kigali hotel [V MICE-22], and tuition per group, not per seat.
4. **Funded money comes second:** through an accredited partner or a consortium member first, and as databayt itself only after RTB's answer and six months of registration (§3, §4).
5. **Schools are a line of their own:** groups of eight from the academy, the university and the IB school for a coding, AI or solar day, and training for their teachers (§1.5).
6. **Day passes are not a business:** Kigali's desks are cheap and nearer, and the zone's workers travel to their own jobs, not to a desk. A desk is sold only to guests who stay.
7. **Get in before 2028:** the airport city's conference hotels and the zone's training institute come later; a small, quiet, taught room is what they will not be.
8. **A check for the stays, not the room:** Bugesera Lodge publishes USD 60 to 87 a night with breakfast [V LOC-77], against the business model's RWF 30,000 to 45,000 a night [A AS-22].
