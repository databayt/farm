## Our Farming Model `/model`

**H1:** Our farming model

**Intro:** We plan to combine fruit trees, a small herd of goats and the fodder that feeds them on one site, so that manure, shade and water are used twice. This is our design; it is not yet running.

**Sections:**
1. **Trees.** We are studying avocado and passion fruit first. Avocado takes years to bear (first sales expected from tree-year 3-4, estimate). Passion fruit fruits in about a year but is short-lived and vulnerable to a virus; we will use certified planting material only. We plant a crop only when a buyer has told us in writing they would trial it.
2. **Goats.** Goats are our livestock candidate. A Rift Valley fever quarantine covered the Eastern Province from 2026-06-26, and animal movement and markets were closed for months. We will not buy animals until movement is legal and a buyer or abattoir route is agreed.
3. **Water and the lake.** We keep the legal buffers from the lake and apply for a permit where irrigation exceeds 1 hectare.
4. **Three models, honestly.** We modelled a conservative mixed farm, a diversified commercial farm and a technology-enabled farm. On today's placeholder costs, none pays back within 10 years in the base case; the best case, with higher yields and prices, reaches about a 10.7% return. We publish this because the point is to find out what works. [link: Sources]

## Livestock `/livestock`

**H1:** Livestock

**Lead:** Goats are the livestock we are studying. We have no animals yet.

**Body:** Goat meat has steady demand in Kigali, especially at year-end and around Eid, and the trade is dominated by traders. We want to supply healthy, recorded animals to a small number of buyers. Disease rules decide our timing: Rift Valley fever and peste des petits ruminants have affected the region. Before any purchase we will confirm the veterinary rules with the district office, use a registered veterinarian, vaccinate as advised and record every animal.

**Status:** [STATE] as of [DATE]. Current movement rules: [WHAT WE LAST CONFIRMED, WITH DATE AND SOURCE].

## Sustainability `/sustainability`

**H1:** Sustainability

**Lead:** We say only what we can show.

**Body:**
- **Practices we intend to use:** ground cover and compost; fodder grown on the farm; rainwater harvesting; keeping the lake buffers. These are plans until each is in place and recorded.
- **What we do not claim:** we do not call the farm "organic" and hold no certification. We may use subsidised fertiliser where it helps, and we will record it.
- **What we will measure:** soil test results by year; share of land under cover; water used per hectare; share of animal feed grown on the farm. We will publish the numbers.

## Technology and Innovation `/technology`

**H1:** Technology, kept practical

**Body:** We start with records: what we plant, what we spend, what we harvest, what we deliver and what buyers say. Every lot will carry a code. We will add sensors or automation only when the numbers show they pay for themselves. Our own model shows that adding technology without a measured benefit makes the farm's finances worse.

**What we use now:** a spreadsheet record system; a weighing scale (when bought); phone-based ordering.
**What we are not using yet:** sensors, automation, drones.
